# New Data: OpenAI's Lead Is Contracting as AI Competition Intensifies
**Source**: https://www.bigtechnology.com/p/new-data-openais-lead-is-contracting
**Date**: 2026-02-03
**Author**: Alex Kantrowitz, Big Technology
**Keywords**: OpenAI, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Claude, market share, AI competition, chatbot market

## Elevator pitch
New data shows ChatGPT's mobile market share dropped from 69.1% to 45.3% in one year while Gemini surged to 25.1% and Grok rocketed to 15.2%, signaling a tightening chatbot race — though Claude's engagement metrics tell a different story of depth over breadth.

## Takeaways
- ChatGPT's daily U.S. mobile app market share fell from 69.1% to 45.3% between January 2025 and January 2026
- Gemini rose from 14.7% to 25.1% and Grok from 1.6% to 15.2% in the same period
- Web visits to Gemini grew 647% year-over-year (267.7M to 2B), while ChatGPT grew 50% (3.8B to 5.7B)
- The chatbot race is not zero-sum: 20% of users now use at least two chatbot apps, up from 5% at end of 2023
- Claude's average daily session time surged from ~10 minutes in June 2025 to over 30 minutes today, suggesting deeper engagement despite fewer users

## Synthesis
Big Technology presents compelling new market data from Apptopia and Similarweb that paints a picture of a rapidly tightening AI chatbot competition. The headline story is the erosion of ChatGPT's dominance, but the underlying dynamics are more nuanced than a simple decline narrative.

On mobile, ChatGPT's daily U.S. user market share dropped from 69.1% to 45.3% in a single year — a dramatic contraction. The beneficiaries are Google's Gemini, which rose from 14.7% to 25.1%, and Grok, which exploded from 1.6% to 15.2%. On desktop and mobile web, the pattern is similar: ChatGPT visits grew 50% (3.8B to 5.7B), but Gemini's visits grew an extraordinary 647% (267.7M to 2B).

However, the overall market tells a more complex story. Total chatbot usage grew 152% since January 2025, meaning ChatGPT exhibited healthy absolute growth even as its relative share contracted. The market isn't zero-sum: 20% of chatbot users were using at least two apps by end of 2025, up from 5% at end of 2023. Most devices measured by Apptopia have never used a chatbot at all, indicating massive untapped potential.

Perhaps the most interesting data point concerns Anthropic's Claude. While Claude doesn't compete in raw user numbers with ChatGPT or Gemini, the time people spend with it has surged from approximately ten minutes daily in June 2025 to over thirty minutes today. This suggests a fundamentally different usage pattern — one of deep, sustained engagement rather than quick queries. This aligns with Claude's strength in coding and complex tasks via Claude Code and Cowork.

The market dynamics are showing early signs of maturation. Apptopia notes the market is "flattening a bit" with a potential gap emerging between early adopters and the mainstream. Similarweb observed a ChatGPT traffic dip in November/December that coincided with a Gemini growth spurt, though ChatGPT partially recovered in January.

The data suggests the AI chatbot market is evolving from a one-horse race into a genuine multi-player competition where different platforms serve different user needs. ChatGPT maintains overall leadership, Gemini captures the mainstream through Google's distribution advantage, Grok attracts a growing audience through X integration, and Claude differentiates through depth of engagement. The coming year will likely determine whether this diversification consolidates or accelerates.
