# Meta Bought the Other Half of Agentic Commerce: Stilla Acquisition + Revolut's US Bank Charter
**Source**: Linas's Newsletter (linas@substack.com)
**Date**: 2026-09-13
**Author**: Linas (Linas's Newsletter)
**Keywords**: Meta, Stilla, agentic commerce, Muse, Business Agent, Revolut, US bank charter, PRAGMA, fintech, AI agents, Block, Buzz, Mastercard Agent Suite

## Elevator pitch
Meta acquired Stockholm-based Stilla — a company-wide AI agent platform with per-user permissions, company memory, and sandboxed coding — folding it into Meta Business Agent to complete the agentic commerce loop (Muse for the buyer, Business Agent for the seller, Stilla for the post-sale work). Meanwhile, Revolut won a US national bank charter that Wise couldn't, powered by PRAGMA, its foundation model trained on 40 billion banking events.

## Takeaways
- Meta acquired Stilla (Stockholm, founded 2024, $5m pre-seed) to fold into Meta Business Agent — the internal-side layer Business Agent lacked: one agent across Slack, Teams, GitHub, and ~3,000 tools with company memory and SOC 2
- Stilla's founders previously sold Tictail to Shopify (2018) and worked on Shop Pay — Meta hired the people who built the checkout it plans to plug into Muse
- The three-product loop: Muse (buyer agent) + Business Agent (seller inbox) + Stilla (post-sale work agent) = a completed WhatsApp transaction with follow-on work, paid over rails Meta doesn't own but with Meta's agents at both ends
- Stilla is also a trust admission: Muse shipped with safeguard failures; enterprises need Stilla's permission model for GitHub and payroll access — Meta bought rather than built
- Revolut got preliminary conditional OCC approval for a national bank charter (Sept 3), 6 weeks after Wise was rejected — FDIC and Fed sign-off still needed, H1 2027 target launch
- Revolut's PRAGMA foundation model (trained on 40B behavioral events from 25M users across 111 countries) gives it an underwriting edge US neobanks can't replicate: credit scoring +130%, fraud recall +64.7%, 75% of support auto-resolved
- Block filed with the OCC for Builders Bank & Trust (bitcoin/stablecoin custody); Mastercard expanded Agent Suite for Merchants with Claude integration; Incore Bank trialed AI onboarding with Kyndryl/Google Cloud (99% accuracy)

## Synthesis
Linas delivers a two-story weekly roundup focused on agentic commerce and fintech charters. The Meta-Stilla acquisition is framed as completing a three-part product: Muse acts for the buyer, Business Agent handles the seller's inbox, and Stilla's team-and-technology handles the internal post-sale work across Slack, Teams, GitHub, and roughly 3,000 tools. The founders' Shopify/Shop Pay experience is key — Meta hired the people who built the checkout it plans to integrate. Linas argues the acquisition is also a trust admission: Muse shipped with reported safeguard failures, and enterprises won't accept that with GitHub and payroll attached, so Meta bought Stilla's permission model rather than building one.

The competitive landscape is mapped by analogy: Grok Bot is Stilla's cousin (teammates that finish work inside tools, no merchant inbox); Gemini Spark is Muse's cousin (Workspace context, no company-wide agent); OpenClaw is the exit for firms that won't keep company memory on Meta's infrastructure; Block's open-source Buzz (released July after cutting 40% of staff) is the same coordination bet with a signed audit trail on owned infrastructure.

The second story covers Revolut's OCC preliminary conditional approval for a national bank charter, granted September 3 — just six weeks after Wise was rejected. The charter swaps borrowed sponsor-bank rails for owned ones, with minimum capital of $95m and $500m set aside for the US push. The key differentiator is PRAGMA: a foundation model trained on 40B behavioral events that treats transaction histories like text, predicting the next likely action. No third-party model can replicate it because no third party has Revolut's data. The competitive wedge falls against Citi, HSBC, and Chase's international desks, not domestic neobanks. Linas also notes Block's OCC filing for Builders Bank & Trust (bitcoin/stablecoin custody), Mastercard's expanded Agent Suite with Claude integration, and Incore Bank's AI onboarding trial achieving 99% data extraction accuracy.
