# Legaltech's 'Land Grab' Moment: Lexroom Joins the Acquisition Race
**Source**: Sifted Daily (sifted@sifted.eu)
**Date**: 2026-09-14
**Author**: Tom Nugent (Sifted)
**Keywords**: legaltech, M&A, Lexroom, Legora, Harvey, AI legal platforms, European startups, consolidation

## Elevator pitch
Milan-based Lexroom, an AI platform for law firms and corporate legal teams, announced its first two acquisitions (Bulgarian Praven Intelekt and French Query Juriste) to expand across five European markets, entering a consolidation race dominated by larger players Legora ($5.6bn valuation) and Harvey ($15.5bn valuation) — a signal that legaltech M&A is heating up as incumbents sweep up companies to expand product and market reach.

## Takeaways
- Lexroom made its first two acquisitions: Bulgaria's Praven Intelekt and France's Query Juriste, extending its footprint to five European markets
- Legaltech consolidation is accelerating: Legora (raised $600m this year, valued $5.6bn) has signed 5 deals; Harvey (raised $550m, valued $15.5bn) has signed 4 deals this year
- Lexroom CEO Paolo Fois describes the market as entering a "land grab moment" with more consolidation expected across the board
- Lexroom's M&A strategy is geographic (going deeper in single markets), while Legora and Harvey's acquisitions are more product-driven — Fois doesn't expect to compete directly for the same targets
- US VC FirstMark argues Europe is "underrated" by American investors, backing companies like Legora and Synthesia
- Other deals: Resolutiion (AI disputes platform) emerged from stealth with $10.4m from LocalGlobe, Cherry Ventures, Octopus Ventures, Chevron; Greenly acquired Swedish competitor Normative; Mistral raised €3bn at €21bn valuation

## Synthesis
Tom Nugent reports from Sifted on the accelerating consolidation in legaltech, anchored by Lexroom's first two acquisitions. The Milan-based AI legal platform bought Bulgarian startup Praven Intelekt and French startup Query Juriste, expanding to five European markets. CEO Paolo Fois frames this as a "land grab moment" — a phase where larger incumbents sweep up smaller companies to expand product remit and market share.

The competitive landscape is formidable. Swedish legal AI firm Legora, which raised $600m this year at a $5.6bn valuation, and US-based Harvey, which raised $550m at a $15.5bn valuation, are both on buying sprees with five and four acquisitions respectively in 2026. Fois differentiates Lexroom's approach as geographic rather than product-driven, targeting depth in existing markets rather than the same opportunities as the gorillas.

The broader Sifted Daily covers US VC FirstMark's argument that Europe is underrated by American investors (partner Matt Turck cites Legora and Synthesia as portfolio advantages), Resolutiion's $10.4m stealth exit for AI dispute resolution, Greenly's acquisition of Normative, and Mistral's €3bn round at €21bn co-led by Samsung, the EU's Scaleup Europe Fund, and PSG Equity. Berlin unicorns N8n and Parloa are highlighted as biggest climbers on Sifted's DACH & CEE leaderboard, with Germany hosting 41 of the Sifted 100 DACH & CEE companies.
