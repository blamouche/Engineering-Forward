**Author**: Lenny Rachitsky
**Keywords**: AI-native company, work culture, millennial, pre-AI tech, field notes, organizational transformation

## Elevator pitch
Lenny's Newsletter shares field notes from a millennial raised in pre-AI tech on what it's actually like to work inside an AI-native company — the culture, the workflows, and the disorientation of someone who built their career in a world where AI wasn't the default.

## Takeaways
- **Field notes format**: A first-person account from someone who entered tech before AI was ubiquitous, now navigating a fully AI-native workplace.
- **Culture shock**: The article explores the gap between pre-AI tech culture — where individual expertise and manual work were valued — and AI-native culture where AI augmentation is the default.
- **Organizational transformation**: Insights into how an AI-native company restructures roles, workflows, and expectations around AI-augmented work.
