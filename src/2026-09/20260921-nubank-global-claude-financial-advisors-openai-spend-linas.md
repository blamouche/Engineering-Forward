# Nubank Goes Global Without Licenses; Claude Targets Financial Advisors; OpenAI Overtakes Anthropic in Developer Spend

## Elevator pitch
Linas's weekly fintech pulse dissects three major stories: Nubank launching in 35 countries using a Swiss legal loophole and stablecoin rails, Anthropic shipping Claude for Financial Advisors as the first role-specific AI product built around MCP connectors, and OpenAI overtaking Anthropic in developer API spend on OpenRouter for the first time in 2.5 years — driven by a July price war and GPT-6 Astra's September launch.

## Takeaways
- **Nubank's regulatory arbitrage**: Nu Global AG, incorporated in Zug, converts customer deposits into Circle's USDC or EURC, pays 3.5% APY on dollars, and uses Article 5(3)(f) of the Swiss Banking Ordinance to treat guaranteed funds as non-deposits — avoiding the need for banking licenses in any of the 35 countries it serves.
- **Stablecoin rails bypass correspondent banking**: While Wise and Revolut spent years assembling local accounts market by market, Nubank built one entity on stablecoin rails that settles globally, achieving 4-5% of a traditional bank's cost-to-serve.
- **Claude for Financial Advisors as a platform template**: Anthropic's plugin bundles MCP connectors to Schwab, BlackRock, Addepar, Orion, Envestnet, iCapital, Wealthbox, Vanguard, and Zocks — covering roughly 80% of an advisor's workday without giving investment advice. It's the first time Anthropic shipped its full Cowork + plugins + MCP + Enterprise stack around a single job title.
- **OpenAI vs Anthropic architectural split**: OpenAI licenses and hosts premium datasets (Daloopa, PitchBook, LSEG) for centralized retrieval; Anthropic queries systems via MCP with client data staying on custodian servers. One sells to trading floors, the other to thousands of small RIA practices.
- **OpenAI's spend reversal is mechanical, not qualitative**: The 2-week reversal on OpenRouter was driven by Luna's 80% price cut (14x volume jump) and GPT-6 Astra's launch, not by developers concluding Claude got worse. Chinese open-weight models (DeepSeek, Zhipu, Moonshot, Qwen) handle roughly two-thirds of tracked tokens on peak days.
- **Agentic AI payments going live**: Worldline became one of the first European firms to facilitate payments via agentic AI using the Universal Commerce Protocol, an open standard developed by Google and partners targeting $3-5 trillion in AI agent transactions by 2030.

## Synthesis
Linas's newsletter this week reads as a map of where AI and finance intersect in late 2026, and the three stories each represent a different axis of that intersection: regulatory arbitrage, vertical AI products, and infrastructure economics.

Nubank's global expansion is the most audacious regulatory play of the year. By incorporating in Zug and using Sygnum Bank's custody guarantee to invoke Article 5(3)(f) of the Swiss Banking Ordinance, Nu Global treats customer funds as non-deposits — because they're guaranteed by a licensed bank. This means no FINMA banking license, no local licenses in 35 countries, and no correspondent banking relationships. The stablecoin rails (USDC/EURC) handle settlement globally, and the revenue model mirrors Nubank's Brazilian operation: float spreads, interchange, and eventually credit. The risk is that Brazil's Resolution 561, effective October 2026, bars regulated eFX firms from using stablecoins for cross-border settlement. If regulators decide that placing international accounts inside a Super App used by 118 million Brazilians breaks the reverse-solicitation standard, the 35-country reach shrinks overnight. But if they don't, every large consumer fintech will copy the structure.

The Claude for Financial Advisors story reveals Anthropic's platform strategy with unusual clarity. The product itself is narrow — pre-built skills for meeting prep, post-meeting notes, rebalance reviews, estate and tax briefs, and SEC Marketing Rule screening, connected via MCP to custodians and portfolio platforms. But the template matters more than the product. Anthropic is assembling Cowork (desktop workspace), plugins, MCP connectors, and Enterprise controls into a role-based operating system, and Financial Advisors is the first time they shipped that stack end-to-end around a single job title. The same week saw Claude in Salesforce launch with 37 pre-built sales skills and Claude for Small Business expand with 43 new workflows. The architectural difference from OpenAI is deliberate: OpenAI hosts premium datasets centrally so bankers get fast retrieval; Anthropic queries via MCP so client data never leaves the custodian. Each approach matches its buyer — trading floors vs. compliance officers at small RIAs.

The OpenAI-Anthropic developer spend reversal is the most data-rich story. OpenRouter (now owned by Stripe) shows OpenAI taking the majority of combined spend for the first time since February 2024. But the mechanism is price-driven, not quality-driven: Luna's 80% price cut drove 14x volume, then GPT-6 Astra captured 19% of combined spend in its first measured week. The OpenRouter auto-router, rebuilt in August to weight the last 7 days of spend, reinforces momentum. Meanwhile, Chinese open-weight models handle two-thirds of tracked tokens on peak days, meaning the OpenAI-Anthropic dollar competition covers only the premium closed-model tier. The key question is whether Astra's share holds after trial usage fades, or falls back toward the Fable baseline when Anthropic ships its next model — potentially as early as late September.
