# What the Past Year Taught Meta
**Source**: https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/cp/214027435
**Date**: 2026-09-03
**Author**: Nikhyl Singhal (Lenny's Newsletter)
**Keywords**: Meta, AI transformation, product management, senior IC, data science, design, Facebook, hiring, organizational structure, Jagjit Chawla

## Elevator pitch
A conversation with Meta VP Jagjit Chawla reveals how Meta's AI transformation has actually played out over the past year: smaller, flatter orgs are building more, the senior IC track became real, expert functions evolved rather than died, and Facebook is experimenting faster and bigger than before.

## Takeaways
- Meta's smaller, flatter organization is building more, not less — with less process and more time spent on the product
- The senior IC track became a credible path to the top without managing anyone
- Expert functions like data science and design didn't die — they shed routine work and kept precision work
- Facebook is experimenting faster and bigger, with purpose-built apps for sellers, group admins, and creators that had been failing the prioritization bar for a decade
- The same shift PMs went through is now happening to data science and design roles
- Evals have become a core PM skill, not just an AI skill
- It's a "golden time to be a senior IC" according to the conversation

## Synthesis
Nikhyl Singhal's conversation with Jagjit Chawla, the VP behind Facebook's Feed and Reels, offers a ground-level view of how Meta's AI transformation has actually played out — complementing the more critical reporting on Meta's "Project OT." The picture is more nuanced than simple layoffs and AI replacement.

Four changes stand out. First, a smaller, flatter org is building more, not less. Less process means more time on the product itself. Second, the senior IC track got real — there's now a credible path to the top without managing anyone, which historically was difficult at Meta where management was the prestige path. Third, expert functions aren't dying: data science and design shed routine work but kept precision work. The same transformation that hit PMs is now happening to DS and design. Fourth, Facebook is experimenting faster and bigger — purpose-built apps for sellers, group admins, and creators that had been failing the prioritization bar for a decade are now getting the green light.

The conversation notes that when two billion people use your product daily, changing it is like Jenga — every change was measured dozens of times before shipping, and PMs were gatekeepers. The first wave of AI adoption was everyone building personal stacks; that energy has shifted into company-wide services and true, lasting transformation. Evals have become a core PM skill rather than an AI-specific one. The overall assessment: it's a golden time to be a senior IC, and the shift is creating opportunities for those who adapt.
