## Synthesis
Every announced a one-hour subscriber camp on September 4, 2026, focused on practical usage of two frontier models released within days of each other: Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.1 and OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra. Dan Shipper and other Every team members demonstrated what each model does well, where each falls short, and how they've adjusted their personal workflows and tool configurations in response.

The camp format is hands-on: live demos of working setups, guidance for attendees to replicate configurations, and interactive Q&A. This reflects a broader trend in AI education and adoption — practitioners need comparative, practical guidance rather than benchmark scores. Knowing that a model scores 61 vs. 63 on an intelligence index tells you little about which one to use for drafting emails, writing code, or analyzing spreadsheets. The camp fills that gap with real-world usage patterns.

The session is part of Every's broader camp offering, included with a $288/year subscription that also provides access to their suite of AI tools (Cora for email, Monologue for voice dictation, Sparkle for Mac organization, and Spiral for content repurposing). Over 200 people registered, suggesting strong demand for practical frontier-model comparison. The implicit message is that model selection has become a recurring operational decision — not a one-time choice — as providers ship new models on a weekly cadence.
