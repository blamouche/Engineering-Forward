# Samsung, Nvidia, ASML Back €3bn Mistral Round
**Source**: https://sifted.eu/2026/09/03/samsung-nvidia-asml-mistral-3bn-round
**Date**: 2026-09-03
**Author**: Sifted Daily (Tom Nugent)
**Keywords**: Mistral, Samsung, Nvidia, ASML, European AI, Series D, sovereign AI, semiconductor, EU Scaleup Fund, European tech funding

## Elevator pitch
French AI startup Mistral is set to announce a €3bn Series D led by Samsung with participation from Nvidia, ASML, and the EU Scaleup Fund — an oversubscribed round that signals deep strategic alignment between European AI and the global semiconductor supply chain.

## Takeaways
- Mistral is raising a €3bn Series D, oversubscribed and capped at €3bn, expected to close in coming days
- Samsung is the largest investor, followed by Nvidia and ASML
- ASML led Mistral's previous €1.7bn Series C at €11.7bn post-money valuation one year ago
- The round includes the European Union's Scaleup Fund, signaling political backing for sovereign European AI
- Nvidia backs Spanish spinout IPronics in $125m Series B for optical switches in AI data centers
- Sifted 100 Nordics ranking shows 27 of 100 fastest-growing Nordic startups are climate techs, with energy startups making up nearly 1 in 10
- Norwegian hydrogen startup Hystar ranks second overall on the Nordics leaderboard; Danish electricity supplier Reel ranks fourth

## Synthesis
Mistral's €3bn Series D represents one of the largest European AI funding rounds on record and signals a strategic realignment of the AI value chain. Samsung leading the round — rather than a traditional venture firm — positions Mistral as a software layer for Samsung's hardware ambitions. Nvidia's participation continues its strategy of funding both sides of the model war to ensure every customer has less bargaining power while its own GPU layer remains a monopoly. ASML's continued involvement, having led the previous round, ties European AI to the semiconductor equipment monopoly that the entire global chip industry depends on.

The EU Scaleup Fund's participation is politically significant. Europe is attempting to build sovereign AI capability that reduces dependence on US-based frontier labs, and Mistral — as Europe's most prominent open-weight AI company — is the natural recipient of that strategic investment. The round being oversubscribed and capped at €3bn shows demand significantly exceeds supply, validating Mistral's position.

The broader Sifted newsletter also covers the Nordic startup ecosystem, where climate tech and energy startups are defying the broader VC pullback from climate. The Sifted 100 Nordics ranking shows 27 of 100 companies are climate techs, with Norwegian hydrogen startup Hystar (PEM electrolysers) ranking second overall and Danish electricity supplier Reel ranking fourth. Reel's growth is attributed to the surge of renewables creating power-price volatility — incumbents struggle to solve this challenge, creating opportunities for nimble startups. IPronics' $125m Series B for optical switches in AI data centers further shows how the AI buildout is creating demand across adjacent hardware categories.
