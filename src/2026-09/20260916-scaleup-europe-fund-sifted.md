# The Scaleup Europe Fund's Next Bet
**Source**: Sifted Daily (sifted@sifted.eu)
**Date**: 2026-09-16
**Author**: Tom Nugent (Sifted)
**Keywords**: Scaleup Europe Fund, Mistral, TotalEnergies, AI safety, Euclyd, Exein, funding, Europe

## Elevator pitch
A flurry of European funding announcements highlights the continent's accelerating AI and tech ecosystem: Mistral secured a multi-year €100m deal with TotalEnergies, Dutch AI infrastructure startup Euclyd raised a €200m Series A co-led by the EU's Scaleup Fund, and cybersecurity startup Exein raised $270m. Meanwhile, a British MP called for parliament to scrutinize the AI Security Institute as concerns mount about whether safeguards are keeping pace with increasingly powerful technology, and the resignation of Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon continued to reverberate through the AI safety debate.

## Takeaways
- Mistral bagged a multi-year €100m deal with oil and gas giant TotalEnergies to build models for reservoir exploration, coming just a week after its €3bn Series D at a €21bn valuation
- Dutch startup Euclyd, building infrastructure to make AI more efficient, raised a €200m Series A co-led by the EU's Scaleup Fund and Samsung
- Cybersecurity startup Exein raised $270m in new funding, more than doubling its valuation in just nine months
- A senior British MP called for the country's AI Security Institute (AISI) to be questioned in parliament as scrutiny mounts over whether safeguards keep pace with AI capabilities
- Former Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon told Wired that "the next year or two is crunch time for humanity" and that colleagues believe "this is when Anthropic and its competitors decide the fate of humanity"
- London AI hiring startup Jack & Jill announced a $40m raise led by Air Street Capital
- Helsinki-based Steady Energy raised €40m from the European Investment Bank for small modular nuclear reactors

## Synthesis
Tom Nugent's daily briefing for Sifted captures a moment of convergence between Europe's AI ambition and its regulatory anxiety. On the commercial side, the deals are stacking up fast: Mistral's €100m TotalEnergies contract validates the French AI champion's enterprise play, while Euclyd's €200m Series A — co-led by the EU's own Scaleup Fund — signals that European public money is directly backing AI infrastructure. The Exein cybersecurity raise ($270m, doubling valuation in nine months) shows the security layer around AI is attracting capital at a comparable pace.

The safety thread is equally prominent. The UK's AI Security Institute faces parliamentary scrutiny from a senior MP, and the resignation of Anthropic's Jacob Coxon continues to reverberate, with Coxon telling Wired that "the next year or two is crunch time for humanity." The Sifted podcast digs into how this debate is materialising in Europe, where no frontier AI company is pushing the limits — raising the question of what startups and investors make of safety when they're downstream of the frontier. The episode also covers Europe's rising wave of defensive startups and the EU AI Act.

The newsletter also highlights Czech fintech Flowpay, which topped the Sifted 100: DACH & CEE leaderboard, with CEO William Jalloul arguing that Prague startups are "overlooked" and benefit from strong talent, low costs, and favourable taxation. The Sifted Summit on September 30–October 1 will launch the Sifted 250 ranking of Europe's fastest-growing startups.
