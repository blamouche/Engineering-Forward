## Elevator pitch
Revolut customers were hit by a fresh security incident after the broker the fintech uses for US stock trading suffered a breach. Meanwhile, Sifted compiled a list of Europe's most active pre-seed investors in 2026, German growth investor DTCP raised €455M for a defence fund, and defence tech investment in Europe hit €3.5bn in Q3 alone — more than double all of 2025.

## Takeaways
- **Revolut security breach**: The fintech's US stock trading broker suffered a breach affecting Revolut customers, adding to the company's security challenges.
- **Europe's most active pre-seed investors**: Sifted used its Investor Tracker to identify VCs consistently writing first institutional cheques, as many funds have moved to later-stage rounds.
- **DTCP raises €455M defence fund**: German growth investor's first close targets €500M, reflecting the surge in European defence tech investment.
- **Defence tech funding record**: Q3 2026 already hit €3.5bn, pushing the yearly total to €6.7bn — more than double 2025's full-year figure. Deal count is tracking 28% ahead of last year.
- **AI adoption gap**: 43% of Americans use AI at work vs only 32% in Europe, per a Sifted report with London Business School.
- **Stripe on AI pricing**: Leaders from Anthropic, Lovable, and ElevenLabs shared 5 monetisation trends for a world where agents make purchases without humans.
- **NY VCs warming to European tech**: US investors are looking at Europe's neolabs — from Yann LeCun's AMI Labs to David Silver's Ineffable — for the next Anthropic.

## Synthesis
The Sifted daily digest captures the widening gap between AI adoption in the US and Europe (43% vs 32%), even as European defence tech investment explodes — €3.5bn in Q3 alone, more than double 2025's total. The Revolut security breach underscores the operational risks fintechs face as they expand into new asset classes. Meanwhile, the pre-seed investor landscape reveals a structural shift: funds that built reputations at pre-seed are migrating to later stages, creating a gap in first-institutional-cheque funding that newer players may fill. The Stripe-sponsored discussion on AI pricing signals a real inflection point — agents are already making purchases, and product teams at Anthropic, Lovable, and ElevenLabs are redesigning their models for non-human transactions.
