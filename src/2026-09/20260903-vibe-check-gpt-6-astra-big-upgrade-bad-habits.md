## Elevator pitch
OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra impresses with writing, software operation, and visual design — but Anthropic's Fable 5.1 still has better instincts for building a product, and Astra has a habit of adding unneeded elements to outputs.

## Takeaways
- GPT-6 Astra is billed as OpenAI's most intelligent model yet, with impressive writing, consulting work, and visual design capabilities
- Astra's computer use — operating software through the interface — is particularly strong, impressing Dan Shipper
- Fable 5.1 still outperformed Astra on complicated app-building tasks, maintaining Anthropic's edge for product development
- Astra has a "bad habit" of adding copy and interface elements the task didn't need — a beautiful first result isn't always a finished product
- The first draft of the Vibe Check review itself was written by Astra in one shot, fooling the author's editor into thinking she had written it
- Practical recommendation: use Astra for writing, consulting work, and visual prototypes; reach for Fable 5.1 for complicated products

## Synthesis
OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra arrives with strong claims — billed as the company's most intelligent model yet, with weeks of previewed math results and cybersecurity capabilities requiring stronger safeguards. The Every team's Vibe Check puts it through its paces across writing, consulting work, visual design, and software operation.

The results are impressive but nuanced. Astra excels at computer use — operating software through the interface — which Dan Shipper found particularly enthusiastic. It produced writing and designs the team wanted to keep. In one meta-demonstration, Astra wrote the first draft of the review itself in one shot, and Dan thought Katie Parrott had written it.

However, Fable 5.1 (Anthropic) still outperformed Astra on the complicated apps the team tested. Astra also exhibited a recurring "bad habit" of adding copy and interface elements the task didn't need — a beautiful first result isn't always a finished product. Mike Taylor's AI training curriculum was built well from employee interviews, and Kieran Klaassen's cozy island design came with a guided-breathing exercise he hadn't asked for.

The practical takeaway: Astra is worth the fuss for writing, consulting work, and visual prototypes. For a complicated product, Fable 5.1 remains the better choice. The team is hosting a camp to show how they're using both models together — suggesting the practical approach is complementary rather than competitive.
