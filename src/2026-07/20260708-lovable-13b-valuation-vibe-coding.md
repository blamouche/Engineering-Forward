# Lovable in Talks to Double Valuation to $13.2B
**Source**: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/08/lovable-reportedly-in-talks-to-double-its-valuation-to-13-2b/
**Date**: 2026-07-08
**Author**: Marina Temkin (TechCrunch)
**Keywords**: Lovable, vibe coding, startup funding, valuation, AI coding, Menlo Ventures

## Elevator pitch
Swedish vibe-coding startup Lovable is in talks to raise $300M at a $13.2B valuation—exactly double its December 2025 valuation—driven by $500M in annualized revenue and a broader AI-funding wave that includes Cursor's $60B SpaceX acquisition and Replit's $9B round.

## Takeaways
- Lovable is raising $300M at a $13.2B post-money valuation, double its $6.6B December 2025 valuation
- The company hit $500M in annualized revenue run rate in June 2026 with approximately 146 employees
- Menlo Ventures—which just raised a $3B fund and backed Anthropic—is expected to lead the round
- Vibe coding (building software by describing it in natural language) is the most popular and lucrative AI use case, with users ranging from founders to enterprise clients like Workday, Asana, and Nvidia
- The broader vibe-coding market is consolidating rapidly: Replit hit $9B valuation in March, Factory raised at $1.5B in April, and Cursor was acquired by SpaceX for $60B in June

## Synthesis
Lovable's potential doubling of valuation in just seven months tells the story of the vibe-coding market in 2026: it's exploding faster than almost anyone predicted. The Swedish startup, barely three years old, has reached $500M in annualized revenue with roughly 146 people—an extraordinary revenue-per-employee ratio that underscores how AI-native companies can scale with dramatically leaner teams than traditional SaaS.

The valuation math is aggressive even by 2026 standards: $13.2B on $500M ARR implies a 26x revenue multiple, though this must be contextualized against the pace of growth. Lovable went from launch to $500M ARR in under three years, a trajectory that rivals the fastest-growing SaaS companies in history. The question isn't whether the business is real—the revenue is—but whether the multiple reflects durable competitive advantage or temporary market euphoria.

The competitive landscape adds urgency. Cursor's $60B SpaceX acquisition in June created a new benchmark for vibe-coding valuations, and Replit's $9B round demonstrated that even smaller players can command premium prices. But Lovable differentiates by targeting not just developers but also founders, designers, and salespeople who want to build websites and e-commerce storefronts without writing code. This broader market position—vibe coding for non-technical users—may prove more defensible than developer-only tools.

Menlo Ventures leading the round is significant. The firm's $3B fund and its successful early bet on Anthropic give it both the capital and the AI ecosystem positioning to add strategic value. The risk for Lovable is that vibe coding, as a category, faces unresolved security concerns—code generated by AI can be difficult to audit, and enterprise customers may demand more rigorous guarantees. But with $500M in revenue already flowing, Lovable has earned the right to argue that the market has spoken.
