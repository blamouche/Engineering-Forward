# Nvidia Backs OpenAI's $500B Data Center with $105B Guarantee
**Source**: https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/nvidia-invest-15-billion-sb-energy-under-openai-data-center-deal-2026-08-17/
**Date**: 2026-07-28
**Author**: Reuters / Bloomberg
**Keywords**: OpenAI, Nvidia, data center, Ohio, SoftBank, SB Energy, AI infrastructure, compute, $500 billion

## Elevator pitch
Nvidia agrees to provide up to $105 billion in credit support for OpenAI's massive $500 billion data center project in Ohio, marking the chipmaker's largest infrastructure financing commitment and cementing the vertical integration between AI compute providers and their biggest customers.

## Takeaways
- Nvidia will guarantee up to $105 billion for OpenAI's data center at the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Pike County, Ohio—its largest infrastructure financing commitment to date.
- The deal structure: SB Energy (SoftBank) builds, owns, and operates the data center under a 20-year lease to OpenAI; Nvidia provides credit support on land, power, and shell buildout; Nvidia is the exclusive AI compute infrastructure provider.
- The campus will deliver 8 IT-GW of AI factory capacity, with initial deployment of 4.25 IT-GW and an option for 3.75 IT-GW more. First 800MW expected online by 2028.
- Nvidia will invest $1.5 billion in SB Energy, joining existing investors SoftBank and OpenAI. SB Energy and SoftBank will build at least 10 GW of new energy generation and invest $4.2 billion in regional grid infrastructure.
- The deal represents a new model for AI infrastructure: rather than OpenAI building its own data centers (as originally planned under Stargate), it leases purpose-built capacity from infrastructure specialists backed by compute suppliers.

## Synthesis
The Nvidia-OpenAI data center deal in Ohio represents the largest single infrastructure financing commitment in AI history and signals a fundamental shift in how AI compute capacity gets built and financed. The structure is telling: rather than OpenAI bearing the full capital risk of building a $500 billion facility, the deal distributes risk across three parties. SB Energy takes the real estate and construction risk. Nvidia takes the compute supply risk (guaranteeing the lease means Nvidia is on the hook if OpenAI can't pay). OpenAI takes the demand risk (it must fill 8 GW of capacity with its own workloads).

The scale is staggering. Eight gigawatts is enough to power roughly 6 million US homes. The campus repurposes a decommissioned uranium enrichment plant (the Portsmouth Gaseous Diffusion Plant), turning Cold War nuclear infrastructure into AI infrastructure—a fitting symbol for the era. The initial 800MW coming online by 2028 means the first phase will take roughly two years to build, consistent with data center construction timelines.

For the broader market, this deal confirms that the "Stargate" concept—OpenAI building its own data centers—has evolved into a lease-and-guarantee model where infrastructure specialists own the buildings and compute providers back the financing. Nvidia's $105 billion guarantee is effectively a bet that OpenAI's demand for GPU compute will remain strong for the 20-year lease term. If demand shifts to custom silicon, different architectures, or different geographies, Nvidia carries the risk.

The community benefits are notable: $80 million in initial community benefits, with an additional $40 million from OpenAI, plus tens of thousands of construction and operational jobs. This is the kind of economic development story that makes AI infrastructure politically popular in the regions that host it—but the long-term question is whether the promised jobs are permanent or temporary, and whether the energy demands (10 GW of new generation) can be met without raising costs for local residents.
