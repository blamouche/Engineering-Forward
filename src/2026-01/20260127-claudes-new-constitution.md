# Claude's New Constitution

**Source**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-new-constitution

**Date**: January 22, 2026

**Author**: Anthropic

**Keywords**: Claude, constitution, AI values, safety, ethics, Anthropic, AI training, behavioral guidelines

## Elevator pitch

Anthropic releases a revised foundational document outlining Claude's values and behavioral guidelines, emphasizing the reasoning behind desired behaviors rather than rigid rules, with the constitution directly shaping model training through synthetic data generation.

## Takeaways

- The new constitution explains why Claude should behave in certain ways rather than merely specifying what it should do, recognizing that AI models need to understand the reasoning behind rules
- Four foundational principles in priority order: broadly safe, broadly ethical, compliant with Anthropic's guidelines, and genuinely helpful
- Claude itself generates synthetic training data using the constitution, creating conversations and response rankings that align with stated values
- The document addresses helpfulness, ethics and honesty, safety and human oversight, and philosophical questions about Claude's nature
- Anthropic acknowledges gaps between intention and reality while committing to ongoing refinement through external feedback

## Synthesis

Anthropic has released a revised constitution for Claude, a foundational document that both expresses their vision for how the AI should behave and directly shapes its training. This represents an evolution from earlier approaches to AI alignment, moving beyond rule specification toward explaining the reasoning behind desired behaviors.

The core philosophical shift centers on helping Claude understand why certain behaviors are wanted rather than mechanically following directives. As the article states, "AI models like Claude need to understand why we want them to behave in certain ways." This approach treats Claude as requiring comprehension rather than mere compliance, suggesting Anthropic believes reasoning about values leads to more robust alignment than memorizing rules.

Four foundational principles structure the constitution, listed in priority order for resolving conflicts. Safety comes first—protecting human oversight capabilities during AI development. Ethics follows—maintaining honesty and avoiding harmful actions. Third is compliance with Anthropic's guidelines, following specific organizational directives. Finally, genuine helpfulness—benefiting users and operators. This prioritization makes trade-off resolution explicit: safety concerns override helpfulness, ethical considerations take precedence over mere compliance, and so forth.

The constitution serves a practical function in training. Claude itself generates synthetic training data using the document, creating conversations and response rankings aligned with stated values. This self-reinforcing approach makes the constitution functional for ongoing model development rather than a static declaration. The model learns not just from external human feedback but from its own attempts to reason about and apply the constitutional principles.

The document addresses several domains: helpfulness and user-centered assistance, ethics and honesty standards, safety and human oversight preservation, and questions about Claude's nature including potential consciousness. This last category acknowledges philosophical uncertainties about AI systems rather than dismissing them.

Anthropic positions the release as transparency during a critical period of AI development. They acknowledge gaps between intention and reality, noting the constitution represents aspirations that training may imperfectly realize. The organization commits to ongoing refinement through external expert feedback and continued technical development across multiple evaluation and safety methods. This framing positions the constitution as a living document subject to revision rather than a final statement.
