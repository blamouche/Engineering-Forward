# OpenAI to Add Shopping Cart and Merchant Tools to ChatGPT

**Source**: https://www.testingcatalog.com/openai-to-add-shopping-cart-and-merchant-tools-to-chatgpt/

**Date**: January 24, 2026

**Author**: Alexey Shabanov

**Keywords**: ChatGPT, OpenAI, e-commerce, shopping cart, merchant tools, AI commerce, product feeds

## Elevator pitch

OpenAI is developing commerce features for ChatGPT including a shopping cart for in-conversation purchases and merchant integration tools that would allow sellers to upload product feeds directly into the platform.

## Takeaways

- A dedicated shopping section will allow users to collect products discussed during conversations, review items, select delivery addresses and payment methods, and complete purchases
- The feature mirrors Microsoft Copilot's existing shopping integration, suggesting convergence in AI assistant commerce capabilities
- Temporary chat sessions will leverage user data, chat history, and preferences to personalize shopping recommendations while balancing privacy
- A merchant submission portal will enable sellers to upload product feeds, potentially broadening reach for small business owners
- No public launch timeline exists, though leadership has signaled additional products are forthcoming

## Synthesis

OpenAI is developing commerce-focused features for ChatGPT that would transform the conversational AI into a transactional platform. The developments signal a strategic shift toward making ChatGPT practical for daily tasks beyond conversation, positioning the platform to compete more directly with search engines and e-commerce sites.

The core feature is a shopping cart within ChatGPT. Users will be able to collect products discussed during conversations, review selected items, specify delivery addresses and payment methods, and complete purchases without leaving the interface. This mirrors functionality already available in Microsoft Copilot, suggesting that AI-integrated shopping is becoming a competitive baseline rather than a differentiating feature.

The implementation balances personalization with privacy through what OpenAI calls personalized temporary chats. While these sessions are not saved long-term, the system will leverage user data, chat history, and preferences to tailor shopping recommendations. This approach attempts to provide customized assistance without the long-term data retention that raises privacy concerns.

For merchants, OpenAI is developing a submission portal where sellers can upload product feeds. This creates a pathway for merchandise to surface in ChatGPT's shopping flows, potentially offering small business owners a new channel to reach customers. The portal approach suggests OpenAI envisions a marketplace model rather than partnerships limited to major retailers.

The broader implication concerns the evolution of AI assistants from information providers to transaction facilitators. As conversational AI becomes capable of completing purchases, the user journey from discovery to transaction collapses into a single interface. This changes the competitive landscape for both search engines, which traditionally capture purchase intent, and e-commerce platforms, which manage the transaction itself.

No public launch timeline has been announced, though OpenAI leadership has indicated additional products are forthcoming. The feature's development alongside protocols like ACP and UCP for agentic commerce suggests a broader industry movement toward AI-mediated transactions.
