# Grok Imagine API

**Source**: https://x.ai/news/grok-imagine-api

**Date**: January 29, 2026

**Author**: xAI

**Keywords**: xAI, Grok, image generation, video generation, API, multimodal AI, text-to-image, text-to-video, creative AI

## Elevator pitch

xAI unveils Grok Imagine API, a unified bundle of APIs for end-to-end creative workflows combining image generation, animation, and sound effects into short video clips.

## Takeaways

- Grok Imagine is xAI's most powerful video-audio generative model, designed for complete creative workflows
- The API separates image creation from motion extension, allowing greater frame consistency in generated videos
- Generated video clips run up to approximately 15 seconds with synchronized sound effects
- The system outperforms competitors in P50 latency benchmarks for 720p, 8-second video generation as of January 28, 2026
- Third-party integrations like Pixazo are already providing access to the API on their platforms

## Synthesis

xAI has released the Grok Imagine API, positioning it as a unified solution for creative workflows that span from initial concept to finished animated content. The API represents a significant step in the integration of multimodal AI capabilities, combining text-to-image generation with motion synthesis and automatic sound effect creation.

The architectural approach taken by xAI separates the creative process into distinct phases. First, the system handles high-quality image generation from text prompts through the grok-imagine-image model, accessible via the standard API endpoint. Once a satisfactory base image is created or provided by the user, Grok Imagine applies motion, timing, and synchronized audio to produce short animated sequences. This separation of concerns allows for greater control over the final output and improved consistency across frames.

The instruction-following capabilities of Grok Imagine are marketed as best-in-class, enabling users to perform complex edits on generated content. Users can restyle entire scenes, add or remove objects, and control the motion patterns applied to their images. This level of control differentiates the offering from simpler text-to-video solutions that provide less granular manipulation options.

Performance benchmarks released by xAI show Grok Imagine leading competitors in key metrics, particularly P50 latency for 720p resolution videos at 8-second duration. While the maximum output length is currently capped at approximately 15 seconds per clip, this is sufficient for many social media and marketing use cases where brief, eye-catching content is the goal.

The pricing model follows the established pattern for large language model APIs, with costs calculated per million tokens. Large context inputs are charged at $6.00 per million tokens, while outputs cost $30.00 per million tokens, placing it in the premium tier of creative AI services.

Ecosystem development is already underway, with Pixazo announcing integration of the Grok Imagine API on January 30, 2026, just one day after the official release. This rapid third-party adoption suggests strong developer interest in building applications on top of xAI's multimodal capabilities.

The release continues xAI's strategy of competing directly with established players in the generative AI space. By offering a unified API that handles the entire creative pipeline from text prompt to animated video with sound, xAI is betting that developers and creators prefer integrated solutions over assembling multiple point solutions. The success of this approach will depend on whether the quality and consistency of outputs can match or exceed what users achieve through more specialized tools.
