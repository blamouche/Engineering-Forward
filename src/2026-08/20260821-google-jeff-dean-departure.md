# Google Chief Scientist Jeff Dean Leaving Company After 27 Years
**Source**: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/05/google-chief-scientist-jeff-dean-leaving-company-after-27-years.html
**Date**: 2026-08-05
**Author**: MacKenzie Sigalos, Kif Leswing
**Keywords**: google, jeff-dean, deepmind, ai-reorg, discovery-loop

## Elevator pitch
Google's AI divisions are being reshuffled: Jeff Dean leaves after 27 years to start Discovery Loop with Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals; Demis Hassabis steps down as DeepMind CEO to become chairman and Alphabet chief scientist; Koray Kavukcuoglu takes over day-to-day operations.

## Takeaways
- Jeff Dean, Google's chief scientist and one of the most influential engineers in AI history, is departing after 27 years to co-found Discovery Loop, a public benefit corporation focused on automating scientific research
- He's joined by three other senior AI leaders: Sanjay Ghemawat (Google Senior Fellow), Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals — representing an extraordinary concentration of AI talent leaving simultaneously
- Demis Hassabis transitions from DeepMind CEO to chairman, also assuming the new title of Alphabet chief scientist, signaling a move from operational to strategic oversight
- Koray Kavukcuoglu, previously DeepMind's CTO, now leads the unit as SVP reporting directly to Sundar Pichai, and will oversee development of Gemini 4
- Google will invest in Discovery Loop and provide Google Cloud compute, suggesting the departure is amicable but also that Google is losing its institutional memory in AI leadership

## Synthesis
The departure of Jeff Dean and three other top AI researchers represents the most significant talent exodus in Google's AI history. Dean, who joined Google in 1999 and built systems like MapReduce, BigTable, and TensorFlow, has been the engineering backbone of Google's AI efforts. His departure alongside Ghemawat (his frequent co-author), Le (who led visual and sequence modeling), and Vinyals (who led AlphaGo, AlphaStar, and Gemini) removes an extraordinary concentration of institutional knowledge.

The reorg also elevates Koray Kavukcuoglu, a DeepMind veteran, to lead the unit — a signal that Google is consolidating its AI efforts under leadership closer to the DeepMind tradition rather than the Google Brain tradition that Dean represented. The Brain-DeepMind merger in 2023 was the first step; this reshuffle completes the transition.

The timing is notable: Google's latest model, Gemini 3.5 Pro, has reportedly been delayed after a scrapped rebuild, and FutureSearch forecasts suggest Google is now 12 months behind the LLM frontier. Alphabet shares fell ~4-5% on the news. Discovery Loop's focus on automating scientific research aligns with Hassabis's own long-stated ambitions, and Google's investment suggests the company wants to maintain ties with its departing leaders while they pursue a different path.
