## Synthesis
Companies paid McKinsey, BCG, and Bain about $37 billion last year for advice. Most of the work on any engagement was done by someone who joined the firm less than two years ago. McKinsey has hired straight from graduate school since 1953, and it was the first firm to do so on purpose. That model only works if the associate is not what the client is buying. The client is buying a method: the same questions in the same order, the same test on every claim before it reaches a slide, the same structure on every document so a CEO reads page one and knows the answer.

The method is not secret. Minto's Pyramid Principle, Lafley and Martin's Playing to Win, Kaplan and Norton's Balanced Scorecard, and Heuer's declassified CIA handbook on competing hypotheses are all published and readable. Reading was never the constraint. The constraint was the two years it takes to turn those books into reflex, under deadline, with a partner checking your logic at every step. Nobody outside the firms had that, so everyone else bought it back at partner rates.

A frontier model like Claude Fable 5 can now run that method with more discipline than a second-year associate, provided someone writes it down in a form the model can execute: steps in order, a checkpoint at each one, the tests a partner applies, a worked example, and the mistakes that produce plausible nonsense. That is what this guide provides: twelve Claude Skills built on the published frameworks, written to one specification and verified against primary sources.

Two skills were tested head-to-head against the same model with no skill loaded. With the skills, the model passed 95-97 percent of a partner-level checklist. Without them, it scored 38-67 percent. The skills encode the consulting method as executable procedures: structured problem decomposition, hypothesis testing against competing explanations, pyramid-based communication structure, and strategic choice cascades. Install them, and Claude stops answering strategy questions like a well-read generalist and starts working like a firm. The guide includes instructions for running an engagement end to end with exact prompts, a proof section with build methodology and test results, and three installation methods for all twelve skills. All are free to download.
