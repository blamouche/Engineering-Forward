Sometimes, building the future means grieving what you love—and following a hunch into the unknown. For Josh Miller and Hursh Agrawal , cofounders of The Browser Company , that meant shifting focus away from Arc , a web browser with millions of users beloved in the tech community. But as they explored the potential of AI, it became clear that retrofitting the browser with the technology wasn’t enough.
