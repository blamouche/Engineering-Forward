# Harry Stebbings Rolls Back 996 Comments
**Source**: Sifted Daily (sifted@sifted.eu)
**Date**: 2026-10-02
**Author**: Tom Nugent
**Keywords**: Harry Stebbings, 996 work culture, Sifted Summit, European tech sovereignty, EU Inc, spacetech, AI compute, gigafactories, NVIDIA GTC Berlin, Sifted 250, 100 women in tech, Monzo, Nubank

## Elevator pitch
At the Sifted Summit, 20VC founder Harry Stebbings walked back his "996" work culture comments, Europe's tech sovereignty debate intensified across spacetech and AI compute, the Sifted 250 ranking and 100 Women in Tech list launched, and Monzo's Nubank acquisition talks collapsed.

## Takeaways
- Harry Stebbings walked back his commitment to "996" working culture (9am-9pm, 6 days/week), saying "I regret the literalness of it" — he meant willingness to go above and beyond, not literal hours
- Europe's sovereignty debate centered on owning more of the tech stack: spacetech (SpaceX reliance vs Isar Aerospace), AI compute (reducing reliance on US vendors), and gigafactory coordination
- Pascal Mazure (Multiverse Computing): "In Europe we have a lot of initiatives around gigafactories, the issue is we aren't coordinating... we need to consume locally and produce locally"
- Zuzanna Stamirowska (Pathway): sovereignty is really about "owning your data and your findings" rather than nationalism
- Sifted 250 ranking launched: wellness marketplace Healf topped with 1,403% two-year revenue CAGR, followed by Neko Health and Aikido Security
- Second annual 100 Women in Tech list published; female-founded companies still receive just 12% of venture funding, women make up 5-15% of angel and VC investors
- Monzo's potential acquisition by Nubank scrapped less than a week after talks emerged
- NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang's GTC Berlin keynote scheduled for October 21, covering agentic AI, physical AI, and robotics
- UK AI minister working "extremely rapidly" on non-compete clause changes
