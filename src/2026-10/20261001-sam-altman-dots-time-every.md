# How Sam Altman Uses Dots to Take Back His Time
**Source**: Every (hello@every.to)
**Date**: 2026-09-30
**Author**: Laura Entis
**Keywords**: Sam Altman, OpenAI DevDay, Dot agent, Astra Ultrafast, AI Renaissance, Dan Shipper, Every Podcast, always-on agent, agentic coding

## Elevator pitch
OpenAI CEO Sam Altman sits down with Dan Shipper at DevDay 2026 to discuss how his always-on agent "Dotty" triages his mornings, why he only prompts on Ultrafast mode, how he builds features by leaving voice notes for his Dot overnight, and why he believes AI will spark a new Renaissance.

## Takeaways
- OpenAI shipped 22 products and features at DevDay 2026, more than twice as many as last year; Altman teased "just wait for next year"
- Altman's Dot agent ("Dotty") triages morning issues, alerting him only when something is urgent, giving him back creative time and time with his kids — "a significant improvement to my life"
- Dan Shipper's own Dot ("Boo") tracks commitments across apps and has become his default way to use ChatGPT despite bugs, bringing context together from across his entire life
- Altman builds features with his Dot: leaves long voice notes with ideas before drifting off, wakes up to find the feature built plus the next seven steps completed
- Altman only prompts on Ultrafast mode, citing Bret Victor's principle that creators need an instant connection to what they're making — Codex Ultrafast gives that tight feedback loop
- He can't quit Astra's new Ultrafast mode and believes AI will bring on a new Renaissance
- The Every Podcast (formerly AI & I) is the new show name; Natalia Quintero joins Dan Shipper as co-host later this month
