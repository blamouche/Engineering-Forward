# ElevenLabs Doubles Valuation to $22bn
**Source**: Sifted Daily (sifted@sifted.eu)
**Date**: 2026-10-01
**Author**: Jonathan Sinclair, Maya Dharampal-Hornby
**Keywords**: ElevenLabs, Sifted 250, Sifted Summit, EU Scaleup Fund, UK non-compete reform, Legora, AI neocloud, Blackfuel, Oura IPO, Healf, Neko Health, Aikido Security, NVIDIA GTC Berlin

## Elevator pitch
Sifted Summit 2025 opens with the launch of the Sifted 250 ranking, ElevenLabs doubling its valuation to $22bn, the EU's €5bn Scaleup Fund cleared to back UK and US companies, UK AI minister promising rapid non-compete reform, and H Company cofounder Charles Kantor emerging from stealth with AI neocloud Blackfuel.

## Takeaways
- AI voice startup ElevenLabs doubled its valuation to $22bn following a tender offer, cementing its position as one of Europe's most valuable AI companies
- The 2026 Sifted 250 ranking launched: London wellbeing marketplace Healf took first place with 1,403% two-year revenue CAGR, followed by Swedish healthtech Neko Health and Belgium's Aikido Security; Notion Capital topped the investor ranking
- Exclusive: the EU's €5bn Scaleup Fund can back UK — and even US — companies, despite no British capital committed yet; fund COO Sebastian Frisk wants it to grow to €50bn over 5–10 years
- UK AI minister Kanishka Narayan said he is "working extremely rapidly" on non-compete clause reform, judged his success partly on the rate of founder exits from Google DeepMind
- Legora CEO Max Junestrand called UK stock options a "no-brainer" for expansion but Sweden "almost impossible"
- H Company cofounder Charles Kantor launched AI neocloud Blackfuel from stealth
- Smart ring maker Oura delayed its long-awaited IPO citing "market uncertainty"
- Nick Clegg, now at Hiro Capital, said LLMs are "monstrously inefficient technology" and Europe should focus on AI for the physical world — robotics, defence, world models
- General Catalyst's Zeynep Yavuz Wilson argued AI value will be captured at the application layer, favouring Europe's services-heavy economy
- Deals: Reverion (€154.2m Series B, modular power plants), Kuro (€10m, AI construction tenders), 52-North (£3.2m, chemotherapy blood test), Amara (€3.7m pre-seed, voice tech for older adults)
