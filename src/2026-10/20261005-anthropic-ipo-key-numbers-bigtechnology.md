# The Key Anthropic Numbers You Need to Know Ahead of Its IPO
**Source**: Big Technology (bigtechnology@substack.com)
**Date**: 2026-10-05
**Author**: Marty Swant and Alex Kantrowitz
**Keywords**: Anthropic IPO, Claude growth, ChatGPT competition, DAU market share, Similarweb, Apptopia, AI app analytics, enterprise AI, Claude website traffic, chatbot stickiness, Muse, Grok, Gemini

## Elevator pitch
Ahead of Anthropic's anticipated IPO, Big Technology analyzed data from Similarweb and Apptopia showing Claude's dramatic growth: DAU market share rising from 1.4% to 14.9% while ChatGPT's fell from 41.8% to 30.7%, website traffic hitting 950 million visits in August (up 540% year-over-year), enterprise SSO logins jumping 4x since March, and Claude users spending the most time per session (27 minutes) — though downloads are slowing and over 80% of users still actively use just one chatbot.

## Takeaways
- Claude's DAU market share across AI apps rose from 1.4% in January to 14.9% in September, while ChatGPT's fell from 41.8% to 30.7%
- Claude's website hit 950 million visits with 111 million unique visitors in August, marking 540% and 562% year-over-year growth respectively
- Claude's share of AI website traffic rose from 2% to 9.6% as ChatGPT's share fell from 79% to 57%
- ChatGPT users who downloaded Claude spent 13.5% less time in ChatGPT in the month after downloading Claude — Claude is actively winning time away from ChatGPT
- Claude's monthly US downloads peaked at 7.42 million in March (up from 670K in January) but dropped to 3.26 million in September — downloads are healthy but slowing
- Claude's March surge was so large it outgrew the entire market: Claude's +6.75M download gain exceeded the total gain across all AI apps (+6.46M)
- Claude users stay longest: 27 average minutes per DAU, ahead of Grok (23.4) and ChatGPT (23.1)
- Enterprise usage is surging: Claude's SSO login traffic (Similarweb's enterprise proxy) jumped roughly 4x since March
- Over 80% of users actively use just one chatbot, with another 10% using two — winner-take-most dynamics are at play
- Muse users open the app almost as much as ChatGPT and Claude but spend less time on it

## Synthesis
The data paints a clear picture: Claude is the only AI assistant meaningfully eroding ChatGPT's dominance. The DAU market share shift — from 1.4% to 14.9% in nine months, while ChatGPT fell from 41.8% to 30.7% — is the most striking metric. This isn't a marginal competitive dynamic; it's a structural reallocation of user attention. The fact that ChatGPT users who download Claude subsequently spend 13.5% less time in ChatGPT confirms that Claude isn't just adding new users — it's actively cannibalizing ChatGPT's existing base.

The enterprise signal is equally significant. SSO login traffic jumping 4x since March suggests that Claude is penetrating the enterprise market — the segment where revenue per user is highest and stickiness is strongest. This aligns with Anthropic's broader strategy of building Claude for specific job functions (financial advisors, commerce agents) rather than competing purely as a general-purpose chatbot. If enterprise adoption is the leading indicator of durable revenue, Claude's trajectory is more favorable than its slowing consumer downloads might suggest.

The 80% single-chatbot statistic is the most strategically important number in the entire analysis. It means the AI assistant market exhibits winner-take-most dynamics: most users commit to one bot and rarely switch. This makes early market share gains disproportionately valuable, because each user captured is likely to stay. ChatGPT's decline from 79% to 57% of web traffic and 41.8% to 30.7% of DAU share is therefore more alarming for OpenAI than the absolute numbers suggest — those users may not come back. The IPO will be the first public test of whether investors believe Claude's growth trajectory justifies the massive compute commitments Anthropic has signed.
