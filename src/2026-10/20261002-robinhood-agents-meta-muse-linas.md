**Source**: Linas's Newsletter (linas@substack.com)
**Date**: 2026-10-01
**Author**: Linas Sekeris
**Keywords**: Robinhood Agents, HOOD Summit, AI trading agent, OpenAI, Anthropic, GPT-6 Luna, token reselling, data marketplace, Meta Muse, Claude for Small Business, agentic commerce, liability, fintech

## Elevator pitch
Robinhood launched its in-app AI trading agent with 150,000+ agentic accounts — reselling OpenAI and Anthropic tokens at cost while making money on trades — while Meta's Muse for Small Business goes head-to-head with Anthropic's Claude for Small Business, leveraging 5M downloads and 200M existing business accounts.

## Takeaways
- Robinhood Agents launched at HOOD Summit with 150,000+ agentic accounts already open
- Robinhood resells OpenAI and Anthropic tokens at the models' public price; OpenAI is paying for GPT-6 Luna usage through year-end — revenue comes from trades, not AI subscriptions
- A data marketplace that only works with Robinhood's own agents pulls customers off Claude and ChatGPT, creating platform lock-in
- Liability gap: House Democrats are questioning who's on the hook when an agent loses money
- Incumbents funded by idle customer cash are most exposed by the shift to agentic finance
- Meta's Muse hit ~5M U.S. downloads in 3 weeks before being pointed at ~200M businesses already on Meta's apps
- Meta's advantage: Muse starts with brand and ad context that Claude for Small Business asks owners to write down themselves
- Bonus deep dives included: Robinhood's latest financials, How to Build an AI Agent from Scratch, How to Turn Claude Into a Financial Analyst That Never Sleeps
