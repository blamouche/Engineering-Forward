# Introducing Imagine Gallery
**Source**: https://blog.character.ai/imagine-gallery/
**Date**: March 23, 2026
**Author**: Unknown
**Keywords**: Character.ai, Imagine, gallery, generative images, social sharing

## Elevator pitch
Character.ai launches Imagine Gallery, a mobile feature that organizes and showcases all AI‑generated images from chats in one visual grid.

## Takeaways
- Imagine Gallery aggregates all Imagine‑generated images in the mobile app.
- Users can browse, filter by Persona, favorite, and share creations.
- The feature adds “Imagine Message,” generating images from specific chat messages.
- c.ai+ subscribers can set generated images as chat backgrounds.
- The roadmap includes future media types like video, comics, and books.

## Synthesis
Character.ai’s Imagine Gallery introduces a dedicated space in the mobile app to collect and organize all images generated with its Imagine feature. The goal is to give users a centralized, visual grid where their creations live, making it easier to rediscover, manage, and share content created in chats. This shifts the experience from ephemeral chat moments to a more persistent media library.

The feature adds standard gallery mechanics: browsing at a glance, filtering by Persona, favoriting standout images, and fast sharing options both inside and outside the app. For subscribers, the gallery also enables setting generated images as chat backgrounds, extending the creative output directly into the chat interface. These touches emphasize that Character.ai wants Imagine to be more than a novelty—it should become part of a user’s ongoing creative identity.

A notable addition is “Imagine Message,” which lets users generate images directly from a specific chat message by tapping an image icon. Users can swipe through variations within the chat before saving to the gallery. This integrates the image workflow more tightly with conversation, making it easier to turn standout moments into visual artifacts.

The post positions the gallery as the first step in a broader content hub. While the current release focuses on images, the roadmap mentions future support for videos, comics, books, and other media types derived from chat interactions. This suggests a longer‑term strategy to turn conversational content into a multi‑modal creative ecosystem.

Overall, Imagine Gallery is a product move that formalizes and elevates user‑generated media within Character.ai. By giving creations a structured home and adding share‑friendly workflows, the company aims to deepen engagement and encourage creative continuity across chats and Personas.
