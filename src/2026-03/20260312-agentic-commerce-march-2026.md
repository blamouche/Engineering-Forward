# Agentic Commerce | 2026.03
**Source**: https://robonomics.substack.com/p/agentic-commerce-202603
**Date**: 2026-03-12
**Author**: FD (Altimeter Capital)
**Keywords**: agentic commerce, Amazon Rufus, ChatGPT shopping, Shopify, OTAs, PayPal, AI shopping agents, B2B sourcing, discovery channels

## Elevator pitch
Agentic commerce is reshaping retail at three levels: ChatGPT pivoting to discovery-and-handoff, major platforms embedding agent features (Amazon's "Buy for Me"), and B2B procurement automating research-heavy supplier comparison workflows.

## Takeaways
- ChatGPT transitions from shopping destination to discovery entry point, routing transactions to specialized commerce platforms rather than completing them.
- Amazon's "Buy for Me" facilitates purchases outside Amazon's marketplace inventory—positioning Amazon as a shopping layer rather than just a marketplace.
- Amazon Rufus drove estimated $700M+ indirect operating profit in 2025; Shopify AI search orders increased 15x since January 2025.
- OTAs (Booking, Expedia) face structural risk: their supply aggregation moat may weaken as APIs enable direct hotel distribution to agents.
- PayPal faces disintermediation risk if dominant agents implement native payment rails.
- AI democratizes information, shifting power from sellers to buyers; control of discovery channels remains strategically valuable.

## Synthesis
The ChatGPT pivot from commerce destination to commerce router reflects a broader principle about where AI assistants create durable value in commerce: not as transaction processors but as discovery and matching services. An AI that finds the best product and routes to the right merchant creates value through selection quality; it doesn't need to own the transaction to capture value through recommendations, commissions, or search positioning.

Amazon's "Buy for Me" for off-Amazon inventory is strategically audacious. It explicitly uses Amazon's agent capabilities to complete purchases on competitor sites—potentially cannibalizing third-party sales on Amazon while expanding Amazon's role in e-commerce transactions beyond its marketplace. The bet is that becoming the shopping layer is more valuable than being any individual marketplace, even a dominant one.

The OTA risk is real and structural. Travel aggregators built their business on information asymmetry and booking friction: they made finding and comparing hotels easier than calling each hotel directly, and they built inventory relationships that created a flywheel. AI agents that can directly query hotel APIs, compare options, and book without human-readable UI eliminate both advantages. The question is whether hotels will invest in AI-accessible APIs that bypass aggregators, and how quickly.

The B2B sourcing adoption is the least discussed but potentially the most economically significant segment. Enterprise procurement involves substantial research labor—comparing supplier specifications, verifying compliance, managing multi-vendor RFP processes. Agent workflows that automate this research are already gaining traction and represent a large, high-value-per-transaction opportunity with lower consumer behavior change requirements than retail.
