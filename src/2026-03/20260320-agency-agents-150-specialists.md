# The Agency: 150+ AI Specialist Agents Ready to Transform Your Workflow
**Source**: https://github.com/msitarzewski/agency-agents
**Date**: 2026-03-20
**Author**: msitarzewski

## Elevator pitch
A GitHub repository of 150+ specialized AI agent personas organized across 13 organizational divisions—from frontend engineers to Reddit community managers—provides an instant AI agency compatible with Claude Code, Cursor, Aider, and Windsurf.

## Takeaways
- 150+ agents across 13 divisions: Engineering (24+), Marketing (30+), Sales (8), Design (8), Testing (8), and others including Spatial Computing and Multi-Agent Orchestration
- Each agent has a distinct personality, domain expertise, defined processes, and deliverable-focused outputs
- Compatible with Claude Code, Cursor, Aider, and Windsurf with automatic tool detection
- 55.9K GitHub stars and 8.4K forks indicate widespread adoption
- MIT licensed and fully customizable for organizational needs

## Synthesis
The Agency repository represents a mature answer to a question that every team adopting AI tools eventually encounters: how do you create specialized AI collaborators that go beyond generic assistance to deliver domain-expert-level outputs? The answer is through well-crafted, purpose-built agent personas with deep domain knowledge encoded in their instructions.

The organizational structure mirrors how human agencies are structured. Engineering agents cover the full technical stack—frontend, backend, mobile, AI, DevOps, security—each configured with the specific standards, terminology, and working patterns appropriate to their discipline. Marketing agents address the full spectrum from content to social platforms to growth and regional specialization. The sales division handles outbound prospecting, discovery conversations, deal strategy, and coaching separately because these require genuinely different expertise and communication approaches.

The 55.9K GitHub stars (one of the highest for any agent repository) confirms that this level of specialization resonates with practitioners. Generic AI assistance often produces outputs that feel competent but generic—missing the specific terminology, quality standards, and decision frameworks that characterize genuine domain expertise. Agents with deep specialization produce outputs that reflect how practitioners in those domains actually think and work.

The multi-tool compatibility with automatic detection is a practical quality of life feature. As the agentic coding ecosystem continues to fragment across Claude Code, Cursor, Aider, and Windsurf, repositories that require tool-specific configuration create adoption barriers. Automatic detection removes this friction, enabling teams that use multiple tools to share agent repositories without maintaining separate versions.

The Spatial Computing division—covering VR/AR, Vision Pro, and immersive experiences—reflects forward-looking domain inclusion. As spatial computing development grows, having pre-built agent personas with relevant expertise will become increasingly valuable. The Multi-Agent Orchestration specialists address meta-level coordination: agents whose job is to direct other agents.
