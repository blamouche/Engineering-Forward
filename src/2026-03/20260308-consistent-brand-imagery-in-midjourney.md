# How I AI: Jamey Gannon's Workflow for Consistent Brand Imagery in Midjourney
**Source**: https://www.chatprd.ai/how-i-ai/consistent-brand-imagery-in-midjourney
**Date**: 2026-03-08
**Author**: Claire Vo
**Keywords**: Midjourney, AI image generation, brand consistency, mood boards, style references, personalization codes

## Elevator pitch
An AI creative director shares a systematic, repeatable workflow for generating cohesive brand assets using mood boards, style references, and personalization codes rather than complex prompts.

## Takeaways
- Visual Communication Over Text: "A picture is worth a thousand words—and for an image model, that's literally true." Leverage mood boards and visual references instead of lengthy prompts.
- Style References (SREFs) Enable Precision: Individual style references from curated mood boards produce better results than feeding entire collections, allowing for iterative refinement.
- Personalization Codes Embed Taste: Training custom style preferences through rating exercises creates reusable codes that apply consistent aesthetic sensibilities across multiple generations.
- Strategic Simplification in Prompts: Effective prompting uses "cheat codes" like publication names (Vogue, Dazed), camera models (Sony RX100), and plain language rather than technical specifications.
- Refinement Tools Enhance Output: Secondary tools like Nano Banana enable selective image modification—replacing specific elements while preserving context—and upscaling without destroying original composition.

## Synthesis
Jamey Gannon's methodology challenges the assumption that AI image generation requires either extensive technical knowledge or exhaustingly detailed prompts. Instead, her approach positions creative direction as a systematic curation process that treats AI as a collaborator responding to visual language.

The foundation involves creating a mood board that captures the desired aesthetic's essence. This visual foundation serves a critical function: it communicates to the model through example rather than description, which proves more effective for complex or nuanced aesthetics.

When initial tests using entire mood boards produced averaged, generic results, Gannon shifted to individual style references. This granular approach revealed which specific images advanced or hindered the desired direction. Upon discovering that a dramatic green eye was pulling the palette in an undesirable direction, she simply removed that reference. This observational, iterative process demonstrates that consistency emerges through attention to which visual inputs influence output, not through exhaustive prompting.

Personalization codes represent another layer of systematization. By rating images in preference exercises, Gannon trains Midjourney to understand her aesthetic taste, creating a reusable code that can be applied across future generations. This approach allows creators to build multiple codes for different aesthetic directions and layer them strategically.

The prompting methodology itself emphasizes laziness and leverage. Rather than describing technical camera specifications, Gannon uses camera model names as shortcuts. Publication references convey grit and contrast without explicit description. When compositional elements prove problematic, the solution involves cropping rather than negation—essentially removing the distraction rather than fighting it through language.

Importantly, Gannon packages her aesthetic system for clients in Figma, delivering not just images but the reproducible framework itself. This represents a philosophical shift from gatekeeping creative assets to enabling ongoing in-house asset generation. Clients receive the exact style references, personalization codes, and proven prompts necessary to extend the aesthetic independently.

The self-portrait workflow demonstrates practical application: collecting varied selfies with different angles, expressions, and lighting creates a robust reference set. Using these as strong image references allows generating unlimited custom images while maintaining recognizable features and on-brand aesthetic.

This methodology ultimately democratizes consistent brand asset creation by replacing technical expertise with systematic visual curation, observation, and iterative refinement—skills that creatives intuitively possess but haven't previously applied to AI workflows.
