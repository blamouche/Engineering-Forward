# Introducing Forge
**Source**: https://mistral.ai/news/forge
**Date**: 2026-03-17
**Author**: Mistral AI
**Keywords**: Mistral, Forge, enterprise AI, custom models, training, fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, agentic AI

## Elevator pitch
Mistral Forge is an enterprise platform that enables organizations to build custom AI models grounded in proprietary knowledge, offering training, fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning pipelines for domain-specific deployment.

## Takeaways
- Forge bridges generic AI capabilities and domain-specific requirements through three training approaches: pre-training on internal datasets, post-training for specific tasks, and RL for policy alignment
- Supports both dense and mixture-of-experts architectures, plus multimodal capabilities
- Designed as agent-first so Mistral Vibe can handle model customization through natural language
- Early enterprise partners include ASML, Ericsson, European Space Agency, and Reply across government, finance, manufacturing, and software domains
- Organizations retain full control over proprietary data governance and model behavior

## Synthesis
Mistral AI's Forge platform addresses a fundamental tension in enterprise AI adoption: organizations need AI that deeply understands their proprietary context, domain terminology, and operational constraints, but generic models trained on public data provide only a superficial approximation of that understanding.

The platform offers three training approaches that can be applied individually or in combination. Pre-training on internal datasets builds foundational domain knowledge from the ground up using an organization's proprietary data. Post-training fine-tuning adapts general capabilities for specific tasks—transforming a capable but generic model into one that reliably follows company-specific workflows. Reinforcement learning aligns model outputs with organizational policies, ensuring the model reinforces rather than undermines existing rules and values.

Technical breadth is a deliberate feature. Forge supports both dense and mixture-of-experts architectures to accommodate different inference cost and capability requirements. Multimodal capabilities enable processing of the heterogeneous data types—documents, images, code—that characterize real enterprise environments.

The agent-first design philosophy reflects Mistral's conviction that AI deployment increasingly means autonomous operation rather than interactive chat. Mistral Vibe, their agentic system, can handle model customization through natural language, reducing the technical barrier to domain adaptation. Evaluation frameworks and reinforcement learning pipelines enable continuous improvement as organizational needs evolve and model drift is detected.

Early deployment partners span demanding professional domains: ASML in semiconductor manufacturing, DSO National Laboratories and HTX Singapore in defense, the European Space Agency in aerospace, and Ericsson and Reply in telecommunications and consulting. These use cases—policy analysis, financial compliance, manufacturing diagnostics, software development—share a common requirement for precision that generic models cannot meet. Forge positions Mistral to capture enterprise value beyond model API subscriptions, competing in the higher-margin layer of AI infrastructure where organizational data becomes a durable competitive moat.
