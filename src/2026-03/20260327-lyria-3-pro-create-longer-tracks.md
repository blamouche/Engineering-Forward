# Lyria 3 Pro: Create longer tracks in more Google products
**Source**: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/lyria-3-pro/
**Date**: Unknown
**Author**: Google
**Keywords**: music generation, audio, Vertex AI, Gemini, SynthID

## Elevator pitch
Google introduces Lyria 3 Pro, an advanced music‑generation model that supports up to 3‑minute tracks and rolls out across Vertex AI, Gemini, Google Vids, and other products.

## Takeaways
- Lyria 3 Pro generates longer tracks with better structural control (verse/chorus/bridge).
- Available in Vertex AI, Google AI Studio, Gemini API, Gemini app, and Google Vids.
- Supports creative tools like ProducerAI for iterative song creation.
- Outputs are watermarked with SynthID for AI‑generated audio detection.
- Emphasizes responsible use and partnerships with musicians.

## Synthesis
Lyria 3 Pro expands Google’s music‑generation capabilities by allowing longer, more structured compositions. The model supports explicit musical sections—intros, verses, choruses, bridges—making it easier to prompt for multi‑part songs and complex transitions. The focus is on giving creators finer control over structure rather than just producing short loops.

The rollout is broad. Lyria 3 Pro is available on Vertex AI for enterprise use, in Google AI Studio and the Gemini API for developers, in Google Vids for Workspace and consumer creators, and in the Gemini app for paid subscribers. ProducerAI, a collaborative music tool, also integrates Lyria 3 Pro for iterative composition.

Google emphasizes responsible deployment, noting SynthID watermarking on all outputs and safeguards against direct artist imitation. The company highlights collaborations with musicians and its Music AI Sandbox as part of shaping the product.

Overall, Lyria 3 Pro positions Google’s music generation as a scalable creative tool that fits into both enterprise production pipelines and consumer creativity workflows, while extending track length and compositional control.
