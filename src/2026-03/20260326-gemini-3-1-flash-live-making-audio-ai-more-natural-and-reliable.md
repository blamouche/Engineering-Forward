# Gemini 3.1 Flash Live: Making audio AI more natural and reliable
**Source**: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-1-flash-live/
**Date**: March 26, 2026
**Author**: Valeria Wu, Yifan Ding (Gemini team)
**Keywords**: audio, voice agents, Gemini Live, real-time, watermarking

## Elevator pitch
Google’s Gemini 3.1 Flash Live is a faster, more natural real‑time audio model that improves voice‑agent reasoning and is rolling out across Gemini Live, Search Live, and enterprise APIs.

## Takeaways
- Higher‑quality, lower‑latency audio model for real‑time dialogue.
- Leads audio function‑calling and multi‑challenge benchmarks.
- Improved tonal understanding for more natural conversations.
- Available via Gemini Live API, enterprise CX products, Search Live, and Gemini Live.
- All audio is watermarked with SynthID for AI‑generated audio detection.

## Synthesis
Gemini 3.1 Flash Live is positioned as Google’s best real‑time audio model, aimed at smoother, more reliable voice interactions. The model emphasizes both speed and naturalness, with improved tone recognition and better handling of interruptions typical in conversational audio.

On benchmarks, Google claims strong performance: 90.8% on ComplexFuncBench Audio for multi‑step function calling, and 36.1% on Scale’s Audio MultiChallenge with “thinking” enabled. These results frame the model as more capable for complex, long‑horizon voice tasks, not just short Q&A.

The rollout spans developer, enterprise, and consumer surfaces. Developers can access it via the Gemini Live API in Google AI Studio, enterprises via Gemini Enterprise for Customer Experience, and end‑users through Search Live and Gemini Live, now available in 200+ countries. Google also highlights that every generated audio output is watermarked with SynthID, signaling a safety focus on detectable AI audio.

Overall, the update underscores a push toward voice‑first agents that can reason and act in noisy, real‑time environments while maintaining a natural conversational feel.
