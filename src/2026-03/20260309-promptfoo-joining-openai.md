# Promptfoo is Joining OpenAI
**Source**: https://www.promptfoo.dev/blog/promptfoo-joining-openai/
**Date**: 2026-03-09
**Author**: Ian Webster, Michael D'Angelo
**Keywords**: Promptfoo acquisition, OpenAI acquisition, AI security, AI evals, open source AI security

## Elevator pitch
Promptfoo, a widely-adopted open-source AI security and evaluation platform with 350,000+ developers, has been acquired by OpenAI to accelerate its mission of helping teams build and deploy secure AI systems.

## Takeaways
- Acquisition Announcement: Promptfoo agreed to be acquired by OpenAI while maintaining its open-source status and continuing service to users and customers.
- Significant Market Traction: The platform achieved remarkable adoption with 350,000+ developers, 130,000 monthly active users, and deployment across 25%+ of Fortune 500 companies.
- Open-Source Commitment: The company will "continue to maintain the open-source suite as a best-in-class red teaming, static scanning, and evals tool" supporting diverse AI providers.
- Integration Strategy: OpenAI plans to integrate Promptfoo's technology into model and infrastructure layers, enabling earlier vulnerability detection in the development pipeline.
- Resource Enhancement: The acquisition provides access to OpenAI's research capabilities at the model and inference levels to strengthen AI security and compliance efforts.

## Synthesis
Promptfoo's acquisition by OpenAI represents a strategic consolidation in the growing AI security landscape. Founded in 2024, the startup rapidly established itself as essential infrastructure for teams deploying AI systems, identifying that "adversarial tests for security, safety, and other behavioral risks were the biggest blockers to shipping AI, especially at large enterprises."

The company's explosive growth—from startup to serving one-quarter of Fortune 500 companies within approximately two years—reflects genuine market demand for systematic AI evaluation and red-teaming tools. The founders emphasize this was not a forced exit but a strategic choice, noting that "OpenAI gives our work more resources and access to research at the model and inference layers."

A critical commitment maintained through the acquisition is Promptfoo's open-source status. The founders pledge to continue supporting multiple AI providers and models, avoiding vendor lock-in that could undermine developer trust. This approach acknowledges that enterprise AI development remains heterogeneous, with organizations standardizing on diverse infrastructure.

The integration strategy reveals OpenAI's vision for AI security: embedding safety and evaluation capabilities into foundational model layers rather than treating them as post-deployment concerns. By moving these capabilities upstream, teams can "catch vulnerabilities early and ship secure AI from the start."

The announcement also demonstrates strong investor confidence, with backing from Insight Partners and Andreessen Horowitz. Going forward, questions remain about maintaining open-source independence while operating within a major commercial entity. However, the founders' explicit commitment to community contribution and multi-provider support suggests deliberate preservation of Promptfoo's collaborative ethos.

The acquisition appears positioned as an acceleration rather than a pivot, deepening institutional resources behind work that already resonated with developers and enterprises worldwide. For the broader AI safety community, the move signals that major labs are recognizing evaluation and red-teaming as core infrastructure rather than afterthoughts.
