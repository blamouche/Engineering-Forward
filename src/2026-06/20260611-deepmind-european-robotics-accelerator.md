# Google DeepMind's European Robotics Bet: First Accelerator Cohort

**Source**: https://deepmind.google/accelerators/robotics/
**Date**: June 11, 2026
**Author**: Tom Nugent (Sifted) / Google DeepMind
**Keywords**: Google DeepMind, robotics accelerator, Europe, physical AI, Gemini Robotics Models, startup cohort, Neura Robotics, Zurich, London

## Elevator pitch
Google DeepMind announces the inaugural cohort of its first robotics accelerator, selecting 15 early-stage European startups across 10 countries for a three-month program with access to Google's AI stack, Gemini robotics models, and $350K in cloud credits.

## Takeaways
- The Google DeepMind Robotics Accelerator is a three-month, equity-free program running June–September 2026 at DeepMind's London headquarters
- 15 startups from 10 European countries were selected, building everything from AI agents that understand machines to microbots that navigate brain tissue
- Selected startups receive mentorship, strategic guidance, access to Google's AI stack, and Gemini Robotics Models
- Up to $350K in Google for Startups Cloud program credits are offered to each selected company
- European robotics startups raised €1.45bn across 125 deals in 2025; so far in 2026 they've raised €847m across 70 deals
- Zurich is currently the hottest city for robotics deals in Europe with 13 bets since start of 2025, followed by London and Paris
- Neura Robotics separately raised $1.4bn, Europe's biggest ever robotics round, backed by Amazon, Nvidia, and Qualcomm
- The program ends with a Demo Day showcasing achievements to mentors, partners, and Google teams

## Synthesis
Google DeepMind's first robotics accelerator signals the AI lab's strategic push to shape the next generation of robotics companies at an early stage. The 15_startup cohort spans 10 European countries and a remarkable breadth of use cases — from AI-driven welding automation (Norway's 3D-Components) to microbots that navigate brain tissue for diagnosing and treating neuropathology. Each company receives three months of mentorship, technical support, access to Google's AI stack and Gemini Robotics Models, and up to $350K in cloud credits.

The investment comes at a pivotal moment for European robotics. Startups in the sector raised €1.45bn across 125 deals in 2025, and the pace in 2026 suggests they'll reach €1.92bn across 159 deals by year-end if current rates hold. Zurich has emerged as the hottest hub with 13 VC bets since early 2025, followed by London and Paris, with Barcelona, Amsterdam, and Toulouse also seeing activity. Separately, Neura Robotics raised $1.4 billion — Europe's biggest ever robotics round — backed by Amazon, Nvidia, and Qualcomm, underscoring the sector's momentum.

The accelerator positions Google DeepMind at the center of this ecosystem, providing infrastructure and models to early-stage companies in exchange for ecosystem influence. By making the program equity-free, Google avoids direct ownership stakes while building dependency on its AI stack — a strategy that mirrors cloud providers' startup programs. The Demo Day format creates a natural funnel for future partnerships and investments. As AI labs increasingly extend beyond model development into venture support and ecosystem building, this move signals Google's intent to own the infrastructure layer for physical AI in Europe.
