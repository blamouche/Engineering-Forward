# TLDR Tech Roundup: iPhone 18 Leaks, Codex Micro, Rocket Lab Acquires Iridium

## Elevator pitch

TLDR's June 30 tech roundup covers iPhone 18 Pro motherboard leaks showing a thermally-managed A20 chip, OpenAI's Codex Micro keyboard hardware displayed at the AI Engineer World Fair, Rocket Lab's acquisition of Iridium to challenge SpaceX, and a non-invasive brain-to-text decoding model achieving 61% word accuracy.

## Takeaways

- iPhone 18 Pro motherboard leaks reveal the A20 chip with improved thermal management, memory, and NPU upgrades for better AI performance at roughly the same footprint as the A19 Pro
- OpenAI's Codex Micro is a physical keyboard device designed to supercharge Codex usage, developed with accessories company Work Louder, displayed at the AI Engineer World Fair
- Rocket Lab is acquiring Iridium Communications and its 66 LEO satellites to challenge SpaceX's satellite offerings, gaining valuable spectrum rights for device connectivity
- Brain2Qwerty v2 achieves 61% word accuracy in real-time sentence decoding from non-invasive brain recordings, significantly higher than the 8% from previous non-invasive methods
- The model was trained on ~22,000 sentences from nine volunteer participants using magnetoencephalography devices, with training code released on Hugging Face

## Synthesis

The June 30 TLDR tech roundup captures several converging hardware trends. The iPhone 18 Pro leaks show Apple continuing to optimize for on-device AI: the A20 Pro chip features improved thermal management and NPU upgrades, suggesting Apple is betting heavily on local AI inference rather than cloud dependency. The motherboard design reportedly helps Apple save production costs while maintaining the A19 Pro's footprint — a balance between performance and manufacturing economics.

OpenAI's Codex Micro represents a different hardware bet: a dedicated keyboard designed to supercharge Codex usage, built in partnership with Work Louder. Displayed at the AI Engineer World Fair, it signals that AI companies are exploring physical input devices as a way to deepen developer integration — reminiscent of specialized hardware like Stream Decks but purpose-built for AI coding workflows.

Rocket Lab's acquisition of Iridium is a strategic consolidation in the satellite space. By purchasing Iridium's 66-satellite constellation and spectrum rights, Rocket Lab gains immediate scale to compete with SpaceX's Starlink — not by building from scratch, but by acquiring existing infrastructure. The plan to develop an upgraded replacement fleet suggests this is about spectrum and market position, not just the current satellites.

Brain2Qwerty v2 is the most scientifically significant item: a non-invasive brain-to-text model achieving 61% word accuracy using magnetoencephalography. This is a 7.5x improvement over previous non-invasive methods (8%), bringing practical brain-computer interfaces closer to reality without surgical implants. The release of training code and v1 dataset on Hugging Face indicates an openness to accelerate research in this space.
