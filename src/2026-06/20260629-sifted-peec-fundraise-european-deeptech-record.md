## Synthesis
Sifted Daily's June 29 edition captures the state of European tech amid political and funding shifts. The lead story is Berlin-based AI search startup Peec, which is in talks to raise new funding at a pre-money valuation of $200m — roughly doubling its previous valuation. The raise signals continued investor appetite for European AI startups with proven traction, even as broader market conditions remain selective.

European deeptech investment has hit an annual record in just six months, according to Sifted data. This suggests that the AI boom is not confined to US labs but is translating into real capital flows into European deeptech, with sectors including AI, climate tech, cybersecurity, robotics, and B2B software all showing momentum.
