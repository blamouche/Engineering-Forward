## Elevator pitch
Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness fund expanded 148% to $13.7B in Q1 2026 — but the headline is that the book flipped from predominantly long to 62% short, with massive single-name puts on every Tier-1 semiconductor company including NVDA, TSM, ASML, and AMD.

## Takeaways
- Gross notional jumped from $5.52B to $13.68B in 90 days, a 148% expansion, with disclosed positions growing from 29 to 37
- The fund went net short for the first time: 38.1% long, 61.9% short, with the largest position being SMH semiconductor ETF puts at 14.94%
- The short side targets every major semiconductor name: NVDA, AVGO, TSM, ASML, INTC, plus ORCL puts
- Despite the dramatic portfolio shift, YTD return on disclosed positions is +39.45% vs SPY's +8.40%
- The original thesis — "buy the bottlenecks of AGI buildout, short what AI eats" — is unchanged; only the expression has shifted

## Synthesis
Linas Beliūnas breaks down the latest 13F filing from Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness LP, and the numbers are startling. In just 90 days, the fund's disclosed notional exposure more than doubled from $5.5 billion to $13.7 billion, while its direction flipped from predominantly long to 62% short. The former OpenAI researcher who built a reputation with a concentrated bet on AI infrastructure bottlenecks now holds single-name puts on virtually every company in the semiconductor supply chain.

The core thesis hasn't changed — "buy the bottlenecks of the AGI buildout, short what AI eats" — but the expression has undergone a complete inversion. Where the fund previously expressed the AI infrastructure thesis through long equity positions in power, compute, memory, optics, and fabs, it's now expressing the same thesis through short positions: betting that the semiconductor names that benefited from the AI infrastructure buildout are overpriced relative to the next phase. The fund now holds positions on both sides of eight tickers simultaneously, suggesting a sophisticated overlay strategy rather than a simple directional bet.

The timing is significant. This filing arrives as Coatue's May 2026 report frames the AI market as "sellers of scarcity vs buyers of scarcity" — a framework that validates Aschenbrenner's approach. The YTD return of +39.45% versus the S&P's +8.4% — delivering 31 percentage points of alpha in 4.5 months — suggests the strategy is working. But the piece also notes an important caveat: 13F filings disclose put and call notional without confirming whether the fund is long or short those options. The interpretation that these are directional bearish puts fits the fund's pattern but cannot be confirmed from filings alone.
