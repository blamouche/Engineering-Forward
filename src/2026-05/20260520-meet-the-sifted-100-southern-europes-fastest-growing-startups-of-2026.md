# Meet the Sifted 100: Southern Europe's Fastest-Growing Startups of 2026
**Source**: https://sifted.eu/articles/sifted-100-southern-europe-2026
**Date**: May 20, 2026
**Author**: Éanna Kelly
**Keywords**: Southern Europe, startups, Sifted 100, leaderboard, Milan, Barcelona, B2B SaaS, AI, venture capital, revenue growth

## Elevator pitch
The Sifted 100 Southern Europe leaderboard ranks the region's fastest-growing startups, topped by Milan-based Hotiday (hotel subletting, 1331% CAGR), Lexroom (AI legal tools, 1130% CAGR), and Enginy (AI sales platform, 890% CAGR).

## Takeaways
- The top three companies — Hotiday, Lexroom, Enginy — are all based in Italy or Spain, confirming Milan and Barcelona as Southern Europe's startup hubs.
- B2B software dominates the ranking at 32% of companies, continuing a three-year trend, followed by climate tech (9 companies) and AI-native startups (13 companies).
- Milan leads with 27 companies, Barcelona has 14, Madrid has 7.
- Six companies in the top 100 raised funding in 2026, with Lexroom's $50M Series B being the largest.
- The leaderboard methodology uses two-year revenue CAGR with minimum revenue thresholds of €10k in base year and €100k in the most recent year.

## Synthesis
The third edition of the Sifted 100 Southern Europe leaderboard reveals a regional startup ecosystem that is maturing rapidly and developing distinct specializations. While Northern Europe has historically dominated European tech rankings, Southern Europe is producing companies with genuinely impressive growth metrics — Hotiday's 1331% CAGR rivals the best growth rates seen anywhere in Europe.

The top three companies illustrate different entrepreneurial playbooks: Hotiday found an asset-light model in hospitality (subletting hotel rooms rather than buying buildings), Lexroom rode the legaltech AI wave with tools for lawyers, and Enginy's CEO built a customer acquisition engine through personal LinkedIn presence. This diversity of approaches — marketplace, vertical SaaS, and AI platform — shows the ecosystem isn't dependent on a single model.

The geographic concentration in Milan (27 companies) and Barcelona (14) mirrors the broader European pattern of startup activity clustering in a few cities with deep talent pools and investor density. The presence of 13 AI-native companies and 9 climate tech startups reflects global capital allocation trends, suggesting Southern European founders are building in the same hot sectors as their counterparts in London or Berlin.

The Sifted 100 data provides a counter-narrative to the pessimism sometimes associated with European tech: these are real companies with verified revenue growth, not just funding announcements. The challenge, as always, is whether they can sustain this trajectory into Series C and beyond, where Southern Europe has historically had fewer exits than its northern neighbors.
