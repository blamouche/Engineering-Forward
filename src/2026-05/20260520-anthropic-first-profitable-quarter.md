# Mind-Blowing Growth Is About to Propel Anthropic Into Its First Profitable Quarter
**Source**: https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/mind-blowing-growth-is-about-to-propel-anthropic-into-its-first-profitable-quarter-7edbf2f4
**Date**: 2026-05-20
**Author**: Berber Jin
**Keywords**: Anthropic, revenue, profitability, AI boom, Dario Amodei, funding round, valuation

## Elevator pitch
Anthropic is projecting a 130% quarterly revenue surge to $10.9 billion and its first-ever operating profit in the June 2026 quarter, defying skeptics who argued that AI companies' massive infrastructure spending would prevent near-term profitability — all while raising a funding round likely to push its valuation above OpenAI's.

## Takeaways
- Revenue projected to more than double from Q1 to Q2 2026, reaching $10.9 billion (130% quarterly growth)
- The company expects its first operating profit in the June quarter, a milestone that challenges the "AI companies can't make money" narrative
- Disclosed to investors as part of an ongoing funding round expected to push Anthropic's valuation above OpenAI's
- The growth rate was described as "mind-blowing" and defies conventional wisdom about AI startup economics
- The projections come as Anthropic simultaneously locks in massive compute deals (SpaceX $45B) — suggesting revenue growth is outpacing infrastructure costs

## Synthesis
The Wall Street Journal has obtained investor disclosures showing that Anthropic is on the cusp of a financial milestone that many industry observers thought was years away: a profitable quarter. The company projects Q2 2026 revenue of $10.9 billion, representing a 130% quarter-over-quarter increase, and — critically — expects this to translate into its first operating profit.

This is significant for several reasons. First, it challenges the dominant narrative that frontier AI labs are money furnaces that will burn cash indefinitely. Anthropic's numbers suggest that enterprise adoption of Claude is generating real, accelerating revenue that can outpace the enormous infrastructure costs (including the $1.25B/month SpaceX compute deal). Second, the timing coincides with an ongoing funding round that could push Anthropic's valuation past OpenAI's, creating a financial horse race alongside the technological one.

The "mind-blowing" characterization isn't hyperbole. A 130% quarterly growth rate at a $10B+ revenue base is virtually unprecedented in enterprise software. It implies Anthropic is not just winning enterprise deals but expanding them rapidly — likely through a combination of API usage growth, enterprise Claude deployments, and possibly the joint ventures with cloud providers that both Anthropic and OpenAI have been pursuing.

The broader implication: if Anthropic can turn profitable while spending $1.25B/month on compute and raising $30B+ in additional funding, the unit economics of frontier AI may be stronger than skeptics assumed. This has IPO implications for both Anthropic and OpenAI — profitability makes the public market story substantially easier to sell.
