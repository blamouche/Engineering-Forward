# ElevenLabs Music v2: Genre-Switching AI Music Generation
**Source**: https://elevenlabs.io/blog/introducing-music-v2
**Date**: 2026-05-26
**Author**: Imogen Mulliner, Juliette Rolnick (ElevenLabs)
**Keywords**: elevenlabs, ai-music, music-generation, multilingual, genre-switching, creative-tools

## Elevator pitch
ElevenLabs' Music v2 model can generate songs that switch from opera to heavy metal mid-track, handle fast rap and dense lyrical delivery, embed non-musical sound effects, and build full songs section by section — all trained on licensed data and cleared for commercial use.

## Takeaways
- Music v2 supports mid-track genre transitions — a single song can move from opera to heavy metal and back while maintaining musical coherence
- Long-form, section-by-section composition lets users build full songs with intro, verse, chorus, and beyond, maintaining structure and continuity throughout
- The model handles fast rap and complex vocal delivery patterns, plus non-musical sound effects embedded directly within tracks
- Improved multilingual support means lyrics, vocals, and arrangements perform more reliably across a growing list of languages including English, Spanish, German, and Japanese
- Audio Reference lets users upload a short track (up to 30 seconds) to guide generation style — influencing sound, mood, and instrumentation without copying or remixing
- The model is trained only on licensed data and cleared for commercial use, with every generated track owned by the creator — no sync fees or clearance delays
- Available across ElevenMusic, ElevenCreative, and coming soon to ElevenAPI

## Synthesis
ElevenLabs' Music v2 represents a significant leap in AI music generation, moving beyond short clip generation to full-song composition with professional-grade control. The headline capability — mid-track genre switching that lets a single song transition from opera to heavy metal while maintaining compositional coherence — demonstrates a level of musical understanding that was not possible with the previous generation of models.

The model's architecture supports long-form composition through section-by-section building, allowing creators to define intro, verse, chorus, breakdown, and outro sections while maintaining structural continuity. This transforms AI music from a novelty into a practical creative tool. Vocal performance has also improved substantially, with the model handling fast rap, dense lyrical phrasing, and complex delivery patterns that previously broke AI generation. Non-musical sound effects can be embedded directly within tracks, blurring the line between music generation and sound design.

The Audio Reference feature adds a new dimension of creative control: uploading a short reference track guides the generation toward a similar sound, mood, and instrumentation profile. The system is careful to distinguish this from remixing or genre transfer — it influences characteristics rather than copying source material, and all uploads are screened for copyright compliance. Multilingual support has improved meaningfully, with lyrics, vocals, and arrangements performing more reliably across English, Spanish, German, and Japanese. Critically, the model is trained only on licensed data and cleared for commercial use, addressing the copyright concerns that have shadowed AI music generation. For brands and content teams, ElevenCreative handles licensed music at scale, briefed like a creative director rather than prompted with text.
