# Greg Brockman Officially Takes Control of OpenAI's Products in Latest Shake-Up
**Source**: https://www.wired.com/story/openai-reorg-greg-brockman-product/
**Date**: May 15, 2026
**Author**: Maxwell Zeff
**Keywords**: OpenAI, Greg Brockman, reorganization, ChatGPT, Codex, super app, executive changes, IPO, product strategy

## Elevator pitch
OpenAI cofounder Greg Brockman has formally taken control of all product strategy as the company consolidates ChatGPT, Codex, and its API into a single unified product experience, part of a sweeping reorganization ahead of a planned IPO later this year.

## Takeaways
- Greg Brockman is now officially leading OpenAI's product strategy, moving from an interim role while Fidji Simo remains on medical leave
- OpenAI is merging ChatGPT, Codex, and its developer API into one core product team to simplify offerings
- Thibault Sottiaux (head of Codex) will lead core product/platform teams; Nick Turley moves from ChatGPT consumer to enterprise products
- Ashley Alexander (former Instagram VP) takes over consumer product unit; several execs departed including Kevin Weil and Bill Peebles
- The reorganization reflects pressure from Anthropic (coding) and Google (chatbots) and aims to streamline ahead of a potential 2026 IPO

## Synthesis
OpenAI is undergoing its most significant organizational restructuring in recent memory, with cofounder and president Greg Brockman formally taking the reins of the company's entire product strategy. WIRED reports that the changes, announced to staff on May 15, 2026, are designed to "consolidate product efforts to execute with maximum focus toward the agentic future."

The strategic logic is convergence. Brockman's internal memo argues that OpenAI's products are naturally merging, and the company is officially folding ChatGPT, its AI coding agent Codex, and its developer-facing API into one core product team. This makes practical sense: Codex is increasingly powering both consumer and enterprise offerings, and the lines between chatbot, coding assistant, and autonomous agent are blurring. The end goal is a unified "super app" that combines ChatGPT, Codex, and the Atlas web browser into a single desktop experience.

The personnel changes are extensive. Thibault Sottiaux, who built Codex into one of OpenAI's fastest-growing products, is elevated to lead core product and platform teams. Nick Turley, who has led ChatGPT since launch and grown it to over 900 million weekly active users, moves to enterprise products exclusively. Ashley Alexander, a former Instagram VP who had been leading health products, takes over the consumer unit. These moves suggest a clear specialization: separate leaders for consumer and enterprise, with a unified platform underneath.

Notably, the reorganization follows a wave of executive departures. Kevin Weil (head of AI workspace for scientists), Bill Peebles (head of Sora), and Srinivas Narayanan (CTO of enterprise applications) all left the company last month. Fidji Simo, CEO of AGI deployment, remains on medical leave but the company expects her return and notes she worked with Brockman on these changes.

The timing is critical. OpenAI faces mounting competitive pressure — Anthropic is strong in coding, Google is aggressive in consumer chatbots — and the company is positioning for an IPO that could happen later in 2026. Simplifying the product portfolio makes the company more legible to public market investors. It also signals that OpenAI sees its future not as a collection of standalone products but as an integrated platform where coding, conversation, and autonomous task execution converge into one experience. Whether this consolidation proves to be strategic genius or premature convergence will depend on execution in the coming quarters.
