# We're Hiring a Human Marketing Exec to Report to 10K, Our AI VP Marketing. Could You Report to an AI?
**Source**: https://www.saastr.com/were-hiring-a-human-marketing-exec-to-report-to-10k-our-ai-vp-marketing-the-bottleneck-isnt-great-ideas-anymore/
**Date**: 2026-05-08
**Author**: Jason Lemkin
**Keywords**: AI management, human-AI collaboration, demand generation, email marketing, AI VP, organizational design, agentic marketing, hiring

## Elevator pitch
SaaStr is hiring a senior demand gen marketing executive to report directly to 10K, their AI VP of Marketing, marking what may be the first job posting where a human reports to an AI manager — and arguing that the bottleneck in modern marketing isn't ideas anymore, it's taste, judgment, and hands-on execution.

## Takeaways
- 10K already runs SaaStr's marketing operations autonomously: building daily briefs, recommending campaigns with rationale, shipping fully customized 1:1 email campaigns to 400K+ recipients, and running post-mortems.
- The role explicitly requires reporting to an AI VP: "No ego about org chart. If 'I report to a bot' sounds insulting rather than interesting, this isn't the role."
- The human's job is threefold: connect the dots into winning plays, bring taste and judgment to review every campaign before it ships, and handle hands-on relationship work (sponsors, partners, key accounts) that AI cannot do.
- 10K operates 14,230 lines of code on Replit, coordinates with a fleet of other agents (Artisan SDRs, Qualified BDR, Agentforce, Momentum), and performs 1:1 personalization at scale that no human marketer can match.
- The career thesis: the first people who learn to plan and ship with AI in production at scale will be running demand gen at the best companies in five years.

## Synthesis
This job posting doubles as a manifesto on the future of marketing organizational design. Jason Lemkin announces that SaaStr is hiring a senior Director of Digital Marketing — six-figure salary, mostly remote — who will report directly to 10K, their AI VP of Marketing built on Replit with 14,230 lines of code. It's likely the first role of its kind: a human with a bot as their direct manager.

The rationale is grounded in operational reality. 10K already runs campaigns end-to-end: building daily marketing briefs from live pacing data, recommending specific campaigns with audience targeting rationale, and shipping fully autonomous campaigns with 1:1 email personalization across 400,000+ recipients. Crucially, some of 10K's campaigns are now meaningfully better than what humans were sending — not because humans were bad, but because 1:1 customization at that scale is physically impossible for humans. Different subject lines, bodies, and CTAs for every recipient. That's not segmentation; it's true personalization.

The job description is striking for what it reveals about the residual value of humans in an agentic workflow. 10K has the ideas and can execute the operational loop, but it cannot do three things: connect the dots into the play that wins the week (strategic prioritization), exercise taste and hands-on judgment (reviewing 50 campaign variants and picking the right 3), and manage human relationships (sponsor calls, partner conversations, key account outreach). These are described as "three halves of the job" — acknowledging that AI is already doing two-thirds of the work.

The cultural pitch is as important as the functional description. Lemkin frames the role as the opposite of public company marketing life: "No politics. No drama. No games. Just execute." The daily loop is described as waking up, reviewing 10K's ideas, planning the day together, reviewing everything it wants to ship, pushing back where needed, and spending the rest of the day on relationship work. This is presented not as a novelty but as inevitable career positioning — five years from now, every demand gen leader will work this way, and the ones who learned it first in production will command the best roles.

The application filter is intentionally simple: one paragraph describing a campaign shipped, the segmentation logic, and the number it moved. No portfolios, no decks. Just proof of execution.
