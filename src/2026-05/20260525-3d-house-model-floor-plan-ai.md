# How to Create an Interactive 3D House Model from a Floor Plan Using AI
**Source**: https://www.chatprd.ai/how-i-ai/workflows/how-to-create-an-interactive-3d-house-model-from-a-floor-plan-using-ai
**Date**: May 25, 2026
**Author**: ChatPRD (from Felix Rieseberg interview)
**Keywords**: Claude Cowork, 3D modeling, floor plan, furniture, email parsing, Gmail, interactive

## Elevator pitch
A step-by-step workflow for turning a realtor's floor plan into an interactive 3D walkthrough by using Claude to find reference dimensions in permits, extrude walls into 3D, and populate the model with actual furniture parsed from email receipts.

## Takeaways
- Step 1: consolidate all house documents in one folder; Claude finds a reference (e.g., garage permit dimensions) to calculate the entire floor plan.
- Step 2: instead of asking Claude to place furniture, ask it to build an interactive 2D planner — automate the outcome, not the task.
- Step 3: Claude converts the 2D plan to 3D by analyzing wall positions and extruding them, creating a walkthrough-like experience.
- Step 4: connect Gmail to Claude Cowork; Claude parses furniture purchase receipts for dimensions and adds actual items to the 3D model.

## Synthesis
This workflow guide is the practical companion to the Felix Rieseberg interview, providing the concrete steps for the 3D house planner project. The four-step process demonstrates the "abstraction ladder" in action: from finding dimensions in building permits, to building an interactive planner rather than a static image, to extruding walls into 3D, to populating the model with actual furniture from email data. The workflow turns an unstructured collection of documents and emails into a structured, interactive personal inventory system — a pattern applicable well beyond interior design. The approach requires no 3D modeling or CAD expertise; Claude handles the geometry, the rendering, and the data extraction.
