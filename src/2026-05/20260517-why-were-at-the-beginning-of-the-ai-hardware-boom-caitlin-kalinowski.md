# Why We're at the Beginning of the AI Hardware Boom — Caitlin Kalinowski

**Source:** [Lenny's Podcast](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/why-were-at-the-beginning-of-the)
**Date:** 2026-05-17
**Host:** Lenny Rachitsky
**Guest:** Caitlin Kalinowski (ex-OpenAI, Meta, Apple)

## Summary

Caitlin Kalinowski, most recently at OpenAI building robotics and hardware teams from scratch, discusses supply chain vulnerabilities, why consumer VR failed, humanoid robots, and what she learned building products at Apple, Meta, and OpenAI.

## Topics Covered

- **VR — what happened?** Why consumer VR failed despite massive investment
- **Coming memory price shock** — why she's telling startups to pre-buy now
- **VR technologies → modern warfare foundation** — how VR tech became military-essential
- **Humanoid robots** — still just prototypes; what's gating mass deployment
- **Leadership lessons** — from Steve Jobs, Mark Zuckerberg, and Sam Altman
- **Why she left OpenAI**

## Background

- Former head of AR glasses and VR hardware at Meta (every generation of Quest, Rift, Orion)
- Meta's first consumer electronics hire
- Technical lead on MacBook Air and Mac Pro at Apple
- Helped engineer the original unibody MacBook Pro
- Most recently at OpenAI building robotics/hardware from scratch

## Key Insight

The AI hardware boom is just starting — supply chain vulnerabilities (especially memory) are the near-term pinch point. Meanwhile, the infrastructure built for consumer VR has found unexpected applications in defense and robotics.
