# OpenAI Has Already Created 300+ Decamillionaires. More Than a Decade of B2B IPOs Combined. Before Going Public.
**Source**: https://www.saastr.com/openaidecamillionaires/
**Date**: 2026-05-15
**Author**: Jason Lemkin
**Keywords**: OpenAI, wealth creation, secondary equity, decamillionaires, AI talent, compensation, Anthropic, IPO, B2B

## Elevator pitch
OpenAI has minted 300+ employee decamillionaires through secondary tender offers before even going public, creating an unprecedented wealth event that is fundamentally reshaping B2B AI compensation, talent retention, and the startup ecosystem.

## Takeaways
- A single OpenAI tender offer last October saw 600+ employees sell $6.6B in stock with 75 hitting the $30M cap, yielding 300-500+ decamillionaires cumulatively across all tenders since 2021.
- This pre-IPO liquidity has no precedent: Google and Facebook produced paper millionaires; OpenAI produces wired cash before any IPO at an $852B valuation.
- Early employees from 2019 have seen 100x equity appreciation, with staff engineers now sitting on $50M-$200M+ in realizable equity.
- Anthropic is following a similar path with a $380B post-money valuation and IPO targeted for Q4 2026, while employees chose to hold rather than sell.
- The downstream effects include uncapped comp inflation ($500K+ base floors), liquidity cliffs replacing vesting cliffs as retention tools, and hundreds of newly-minted angel investors seeding the next wave of AI startups.

## Synthesis
Jason Lemkin analyzes the WSJ's reporting on OpenAI's October tender offer to paint a picture of wealth creation without precedent in tech history. The numbers are staggering: 600+ employees sold $6.6 billion in stock in a single transaction, with 75 hitting the $30 million individual cap. Cumulatively, OpenAI has likely created 300-500+ decamillionaires across multiple secondary tenders since 2021.

What makes this unique is the liquidity timeline. Google's 2004 IPO made roughly 1,000 employees paper millionaires who had to endure lockups before any cash. Facebook followed the same pattern. OpenAI employees are receiving $30M wire transfers while the company is still private. This flips the traditional startup wealth narrative on its head — the payoff comes before the public market debut, not after.

The equity appreciation is equally extreme: employees from 2019 have seen 100x returns while the Nasdaq roughly tripled. A staff engineer who joined at market rates now holds $50M-$200M+ with active liquidity windows. This isn't paper wealth being tracked on Carta; it's cash being wired to bank accounts, donor-advised funds, real estate purchases, and angel investments.

The comparison with Anthropic sharpens the analysis. Anthropic's April 2026 tender at a $380B post-money valuation saw an interesting inversion: investors wanted to buy more shares than employees were willing to sell. Secondary markets now price Anthropic at $500-595B, with an IPO potentially set for Q4 2026. The two AI labs represent different employee sentiment about the future but converge on the same outcome: massive concentrated wealth.

For B2B founders, the implications are sobering. The compensation floor for serious AI/ML talent is now $500K+ base with liquid equity — not options, not RSUs with a future IPO condition, but equity that converts to cash within 24 months through regular secondary tenders. The "we'll IPO eventually" pitch stopped working two years ago. As Lemkin notes, being a non-founder operator at the right AI lab now beats founder economics at most B2B companies.

The flywheel effects are already visible: hundreds of newly-minted decamillionaires in San Francisco are angel-investing, starting companies, and seeding the next generation of AI startups with OpenAI alumni capital. When OpenAI and Anthropic actually IPO at what could be $1.5T+ valuations, they will produce thousands of decamillionaires, hundreds of centimillionaires, and potentially dozens of new billionaires — from single companies. This is the largest concentrated wealth creation event in B2B history, and it's just getting started.
