# Anthropic Has Acquired the Dev Tools Startup Used by OpenAI, Google, and Cloudflare
**Source**: https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/18/anthropic-has-acquired-the-dev-tools-startup-used-by-openai-google-and-cloudflare/
**Date**: May 18, 2026
**Author**: Kirsten Korosec (TechCrunch)
**Keywords**: Anthropic, Stainless, acquisition, SDK, developer tools, AI infrastructure, consolidation, Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz

## Elevator pitch
Anthropic's acquisition of Stainless — the SDK-generation startup backed by Sequoia and a16z, used by OpenAI, Google, Replicate, Runway, and Cloudflare — takes a critical piece of shared AI developer infrastructure private and represents a consolidation play in the increasingly competitive AI platform wars.

## Takeaways
- Deal reported at over $300 million, backed by Sequoia Capital and Andreessen Horowitz, with founder Alex Rattray (ex-Stripe) joining Anthropic
- Stainless automates SDK generation from API specs across Python, TypeScript, Kotlin, Go, Java — automatically updating SDKs as APIs change, eliminating manual maintenance
- Anthropic will wind down all hosted Stainless products; customers retain ownership of generated SDKs but lose the automated update service
- The acquisition is strategically significant because AI agents increasingly depend on SDKs to connect to external software — controlling this layer gives Anthropic an agent connectivity advantage
- This marks consolidation of shared AI developer infrastructure into platform-specific tooling, potentially forcing competitors to build in-house SDK tooling

## Synthesis
TechCrunch's Kirsten Korosec reports on the acquisition with additional detail beyond the official announcement. The deal, reportedly valued at over $300 million, represents a significant consolidation move in the AI developer tooling space. Stainless's platform — which automates SDK creation and maintenance from API specifications — had become essential infrastructure for multiple AI companies, including Anthropic's direct competitors OpenAI, Google, and Cloudflare.

The strategic calculus is clear: Stainless's technology automates what is traditionally a labor-intensive process of maintaining SDKs across multiple languages. For AI companies building agents that connect to external software, these SDKs are not just developer conveniences — they're the connective tissue that enables agentic behavior. By acquiring Stainless and winding down its hosted products, Anthropic is simultaneously strengthening its own developer platform and forcing competitors to either build their own SDK tooling or find alternatives.

The deal also reflects broader consolidation dynamics in AI. As the frontier shifts from models that answer to agents that act, the tooling layer between models and the systems they connect to becomes increasingly strategic. Stainless wasn't just an SDK generator — it had expanded into MCP server tooling, the protocol Anthropic created for agent connectivity.

For the startup ecosystem, the acquisition sends a signal: shared AI developer infrastructure companies that achieve broad adoption may face acquisition pressure as platforms seek to control critical tooling layers. The $300M+ valuation reflects the strategic premium of being the industry's default SDK layer, even as that very default status makes independence unsustainable when platforms are willing to pay to take it private.

The wind-down of hosted products creates immediate practical challenges for companies like OpenAI and Cloudflare, who must now either build in-house SDK tooling or seek alternatives — at a time when maintaining developer relationships through quality tooling is more important than ever.
