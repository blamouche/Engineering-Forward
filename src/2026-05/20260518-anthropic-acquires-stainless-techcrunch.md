# Anthropic has acquired the dev tools startup used by OpenAI, Google, and Cloudflare
**Source**: https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/18/anthropic-has-acquired-the-dev-tools-startup-used-by-openai-google-and-cloudflare/
**Date**: May 18, 2026
**Author**: Kirsten Korosec (TechCrunch)
**Keywords**: Anthropic, Stainless, acquisition, SDK generation, developer tools, Alex Rattray, Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, OpenAI, API infrastructure

## Elevator pitch
Anthropic acquires Stainless — the SDK-generation startup used by OpenAI, Google, and Cloudflare — in a deal reportedly worth over $300 million, winding down all hosted services and taking a critical piece of developer infrastructure off the market for competitors.

## Takeaways
- The deal is reportedly worth over $300 million, according to The Information; Stainless was backed by Sequoia Capital and Andreessen Horowitz.
- Stainless founder Alex Rattray, a former Stripe engineer, built software that turns API specifications into production-ready SDKs across Python, TypeScript, Kotlin, Go, and Java — automatically updating them as APIs change.
- Anthropic will wind down all hosted Stainless products; customers retain ownership and full modification rights to their existing SDKs but lose ongoing automated maintenance.
- The acquisition takes a key infrastructure supplier out of competitors' hands — OpenAI, Google, Replicate, Runway, and Cloudflare all relied on Stainless for SDK generation and maintenance.
- The deal reflects the growing strategic importance of developer tooling in the AI platform race, where the quality and reliability of SDKs directly impacts API adoption and agent connectivity.

## Synthesis
TechCrunch's coverage of Anthropic's acquisition of Stainless provides additional detail beyond Anthropic's own announcement, including the reported deal price and competitive implications. According to The Information, the acquisition is valued at over $300 million — a significant sum for a developer tools startup backed by Sequoia Capital and Andreessen Horowitz.

The article emphasizes the competitive dimension of the deal. Stainless's technology became critical infrastructure for the AI industry by solving a persistent problem: manually maintaining SDKs across multiple programming languages is time-consuming, error-prone, and a poor use of engineering resources. Stainless automated this by taking API specifications and generating production-ready SDKs that update automatically as APIs evolve. This made it particularly valuable to AI companies building agent platforms, where reliable API connectivity is foundational.

By acquiring Stainless and winding down its hosted services, Anthropic removes this capability from competitors. Companies like OpenAI, Google, Replicate, Runway, and Cloudflare — all listed as Stainless customers — will need to find alternatives or build SDK maintenance tooling in-house. An Anthropic spokesperson confirmed that existing customers retain ownership of their generated SDKs and "full rights to modify and extend them however they wish," but the ongoing automated maintenance service will no longer be available.

The deal price and competitive dynamics reflect the growing strategic importance of developer experience in the AI platform race. As AI shifts from chatbot interfaces to agent-based interactions, the quality, reliability, and breadth of SDK support directly impacts which platforms developers choose to build on. Controlling the SDK generation pipeline gives Anthropic an advantage in making Claude the platform with the most seamless developer experience — a moat that becomes more valuable as agent connectivity becomes the primary battleground in AI.
