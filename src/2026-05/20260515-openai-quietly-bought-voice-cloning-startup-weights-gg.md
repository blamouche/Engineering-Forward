# OpenAI Quietly Bought Voice-Cloning Startup Weights.gg, Then Folded the Team

**Source:** [Implicator.ai](https://www.implicator.ai/openai-quietly-bought-voice-cloning-startup-weights-gg-then-folded-the-team/)
**Date:** 2026-05-15
**Author:** Marcus Schuler

## Summary

OpenAI acquired Weights.gg, a six-person voice-cloning startup whose Replay catalog hosted voice models for Taylor Swift, Samuel L. Jackson, President Trump, and others. The team was dispersed across OpenAI groups rather than kept together, and the company says it's unlikely to release a similar product. This looks less like an acqui-hire than a takedown — removing a public catalog of unauthorized celebrity voices ahead of OpenAI's expected IPO.

## Key Details

- Deal reported by NYT's Mike Isaac (May 15); Weights.gg shut down services March 31, weeks before public reporting
- Weights.gg raised ~$4M in VC (per PitchBook); terms of the deal not learned
- OpenAI's Voice Engine remains in limited preview on safety grounds since March 2024
- Meanwhile, multiple vendors ship voice cloning: xAI Custom Voices, ElevenLabs, open-weight Voxtral, F5-TTS

## The Pre-IPO Context

- OpenAI targeting public company listing by end of 2026 (NYT, April 24)
- Company made Hollywood-facing moves: hired Instagram's celebrity partnerships lead Charles Porch (February)
- Sora consumer app shut down April 26
- S-1 filing could require disclosure of material acquisitions and IP risks

## Key Insight

Capability isn't the constraint — the constraint is catalogs, what a company controls and what it has retired. By acquiring and shuttering Weights.gg, OpenAI removed a public repository of unauthorized celebrity voices while keeping its own Voice Engine under lock. The Realtime API (GPT-Realtime-2, Translate, Whisper) operates under usage policies requiring consent and disclosure.
