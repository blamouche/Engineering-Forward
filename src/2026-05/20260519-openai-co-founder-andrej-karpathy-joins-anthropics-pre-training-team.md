# OpenAI co-founder Andrej Karpathy joins Anthropic's pre-training team
**Source**: https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/19/openai-co-founder-andrej-karpathy-joins-anthropics-pre-training-team/
**Date**: May 19, 2026
**Author**: Rebecca Bellan, Lorenzo Franceschi-Bicchierai
**Keywords**: Andrej Karpathy, Anthropic, OpenAI, pre-training, Claude, AI talent, frontier models, Chris Rohlf, red team

## Elevator pitch
Andrej Karpathy, the AI researcher who co-founded OpenAI and led Tesla's Autopilot, has joined Anthropic's pre-training team to build a group using Claude to accelerate pre-training research — signaling that AI-assisted research, not just raw compute, is Anthropic's competitive strategy.

## Takeaways
- Karpathy joins Anthropic's pre-training team under Nick Joseph, focused on using Claude to accelerate pre-training research — a meta-application of AI to improve AI development.
- His hire signals Anthropic's belief that AI-assisted research, rather than pure compute scaling, is the key to staying competitive with OpenAI and Google.
- Karpathy is one of the rare researchers who bridges LLM theory and large-scale training practice, having co-founded OpenAI, led Tesla's FSD/Autopilot, and created educational AI content.
- His education startup Eureka Labs appears paused; Karpathy says he "remains deeply passionate about education and plan to resume my work on it in time."
- Separately, Anthropic hired cybersecurity veteran Chris Rohlf (ex-Yahoo Paranoids, Meta) for its frontier red team to stress-test advanced models against severe threats.

## Synthesis
In a significant talent acquisition for the AI industry, Andrej Karpathy — OpenAI co-founder, former Tesla Autopilot lead, and creator of the educational startup Eureka Labs — has joined Anthropic's pre-training team. Karpathy announced the move on X, stating that "the next few years at the frontier of LLMs will be especially formative" and expressing excitement to "get back to R&D."

Under team lead Nick Joseph, Karpathy will build a team specifically focused on using Claude to accelerate pre-training research. This is a strategically revealing assignment: pre-training is the most expensive, compute-intensive phase of frontier model development, responsible for imbuing models with their core knowledge and capabilities. Having Karpathy — one of the few researchers with deep experience spanning both theoretical foundations and massive-scale training — lead an effort to use AI to improve that very process suggests Anthropic sees software and research methodology innovation as a critical lever, not just hardware scaling.

The move also raises questions about Eureka Labs, Karpathy's education startup launched in 2024. Karpathy stated he plans to resume education work "in time," but provided no timeline, suggesting the venture is effectively on hold. His popular online course "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero" and YouTube lectures on LLMs have made him one of AI's most influential educators.

In a parallel hire, Anthropic brought on Chris Rohlf, a 20-year cybersecurity veteran who previously worked at Yahoo's elite "Paranoids" team and Meta, to join its frontier red team. Rohlf will stress-test advanced AI models against severe threats, framing the opportunity as a chance to "dramatically improve cyber security with AI." The dual hires — one focused on accelerating capabilities, the other on securing them — reflect Anthropic's signature approach of pushing frontier performance while investing heavily in safety infrastructure.
