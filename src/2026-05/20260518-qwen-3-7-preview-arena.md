# Qwen3.7 Preview Lands on Arena

**Source:** Thread Reader App (Twitter/X thread by @Alibaba_Qwen)
**Author:** Qwen (Alibaba)
**Date:** May 18, 2026
**URL:** https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/2056403591464984753.html

## Summary

Alibaba's Qwen team announced Qwen3.7-Max-Preview and Qwen3.7-Plus-Preview landing on the Chatbot Arena. Alibaba now ranks #6 among AI labs in Text and #5 in Vision categories, signaling continued momentum for China's leading open-weight model family.

## Key Points

- **Qwen3.7 Preview** models now live on Arena
- **Alibaba ranks #6 in Text, #5 in Vision** on the Arena leaderboard
- **Qwen3.7 series** release imminent
- **Context:** Follows the Qwen3.5 series (397B-A17B, hybrid linear attention + sparse MoE, Apache 2.0) and Qwen3.5-Omni (first end-to-end omni-modal model)

## Why It Matters

Alibaba's Qwen has become the leading open-weight alternative from China, consistently releasing competitive models under permissive licenses. Ranking in the top 5-6 globally shows that the gap between Chinese and Western frontier labs continues to narrow. The Qwen3.7 release will be a key data point for whether open-source models can keep pace with proprietary systems.
