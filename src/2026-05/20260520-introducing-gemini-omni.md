# Introducing Gemini Omni
**Source**: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-omni/
**Date**: May 20, 2026
**Author**: Koray Kavukcuoglu
**Keywords**: Gemini, multimodal, video generation, AI, Google DeepMind, video editing, conversational AI

## Elevator pitch
Google DeepMind unveils Gemini Omni, a natively multimodal model that creates and edits video from any combination of text, image, and audio inputs using conversational natural language, starting with Gemini Omni Flash.

## Takeaways
- Gemini Omni is a multimodal generative model that creates and edits video from text, image, audio, and video inputs through conversational natural language
- The first model, Gemini Omni Flash, is rolling out to Gemini app, Google Flow, and YouTube Shorts
- Omni maintains consistency across edits — characters, physics, and scene memory persist across conversational turns
- The model combines reasoning abilities with generative creation, grounding outputs in Gemini's real-world knowledge of physics, history, and science
- Future versions will expand output modalities to include image and audio generation

## Synthesis
Google DeepMind has introduced Gemini Omni, a significant evolution of its multimodal AI capabilities that merges the company's reasoning models with generative creation. Building on the foundation of Nano Banana, the image generation model released last year, Omni represents a shift toward truly unified multimodal generation. The first model in the family, Gemini Omni Flash, launches with video as the primary output modality, capable of accepting text, images, audio, and video as inputs and producing high-quality video grounded in Gemini's understanding of the real world.

The defining feature of Gemini Omni is its conversational approach to video creation and editing. Unlike traditional video generation tools that require starting fresh with each new prompt, Omni maintains consistency across multiple turns of conversation — characters remain recognizable, physics behave predictably, and scenes retain continuity. Users can film a video and then ask Omni to transform it, whether by changing the environment, adding characters, altering camera angles, or even making objects invisible, all while preserving the thread of the original scene.

What sets Omni apart from competing models is its integration with Gemini's broader knowledge systems. The model doesn't just produce visually plausible outputs — it reasons about what should happen next based on an intuitive understanding of physics, including gravity, kinetic energy, and fluid dynamics, combined with knowledge of history, science, and cultural context. This bridges the gap between photorealistic generation and meaningful storytelling. The demonstrations shared by Google showcase capabilities ranging from transforming sculptures into bubbles to creating infinite recursive visual loops, pointing toward a model that could reshape how creative professionals, content creators, and everyday users approach video production. Google plans to expand Omni's output modalities to include image and audio in the future, positioning it as a comprehensive creative platform rather than a single-purpose tool.
