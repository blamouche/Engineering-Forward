# 3 AI PM Archetypes + 1

**Source:** https://itamargilad.com/ai-pm-future
**Date:** 2026-05-19

## Summary

Itamar Gilad outlines three emerging product management archetypes in the AI era, plus a fourth often overlooked:

1. **The AI PM** - Deep expertise in LLM training, inference, fine-tuning, RAG, evals. Fastest growing PM category (+465% since 2023) but unlikely to become universal. More likely a specialty like security or fintech.

2. **The Developer PM** - Uses coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor) for product work. Vibe-codes prototypes. Gilad is less convinced about PMs submitting production code — product management is a full-time upstream role.

3. **No PM** - Claims that PM role is becoming obsolete. Gilad dismisses this as mostly from people who misunderstand product management. However, the "delivery PO" role (translating roadmaps into backlogs/stories) is vulnerable — AI can already produce such artifacts at scale, similar to how CAD displaced draftsmen in the 1980s.

4. **(The overlooked one)** - PMs who help companies navigate AI-driven disruption itself. Not just building AI products, but helping their organizations survive the transformation.
