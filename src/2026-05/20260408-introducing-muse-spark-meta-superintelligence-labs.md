# Introducing Muse Spark: Meta's Most Powerful Model Yet
**Source**: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/04/introducing-muse-spark-meta-superintelligence-labs
**Date**: April 8, 2026 (updated May 12, 2026)
**Author**: Meta Newsroom
**Keywords**: Meta, Muse Spark, Meta Superintelligence Labs, MSL, AI model, personal superintelligence, multimodal, Meta AI, voice AI, AI glasses, shopping AI

## Elevator pitch
Meta unveiled Muse Spark, the first model from its newly formed Meta Superintelligence Labs, purpose-built to power a radically upgraded Meta AI assistant with multimodal perception, voice conversations, shopping features, and deep integration across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, Threads, and AI glasses.

## Takeaways
1. Muse Spark is Meta's most powerful model yet, rebuilt from the ground up by Meta Superintelligence Labs over nine months, capable of complex reasoning across science, math, and health
2. The model powers a new "Thinking mode" with parallel subagents that can tackle multiple aspects of a problem simultaneously (e.g., trip planning with itinerary, comparison, and activity-finding agents)
3. Multimodal perception lets Meta AI understand images — identifying food nutrition from photos, comparing products, analyzing health charts, and generating visual websites and mini-games from prompts
4. Muse Spark is rolling out across all Meta surfaces: the Meta AI app, Ray-Ban Meta glasses, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger, and Threads
5. Meta plans to open-source future versions and is already developing larger models — this is positioned as the first step toward "personal superintelligence"

## Synthesis

Meta has made its most ambitious AI move yet with the introduction of Muse Spark, the inaugural model from Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL). The announcement, originally made on April 8 and updated on May 12 with expanded rollout details, marks a strategic pivot for Meta's AI efforts — from building general-purpose models to creating deeply integrated, product-native intelligence.

MSL rebuilt Meta's entire AI stack from scratch over nine months, moving faster than any previous development cycle. Muse Spark is deliberately small and fast by design, yet capable enough to reason through complex questions in science, math, and health. The company frames this as a deliberate, scientific approach to model scaling where each generation validates and builds on the last before going bigger — with larger models already in development.

The product integration is where Meta's strategy diverges most sharply from competitors. Muse Spark powers a new Meta AI experience with two modes: Instant for quick answers and Thinking for complex reasoning tasks. The Thinking mode can launch multiple subagents in parallel — for example, when planning a family trip, one agent drafts an itinerary while another compares destinations and a third finds kid-friendly activities. This parallel-agent architecture is a notable technical differentiator.

Multimodal capabilities are central to the value proposition. Meta AI can now analyze photos — identifying the most protein-rich snacks from an airport shelf photo, comparing products visually, and even helping interpret health-related images and charts. Visual coding lets users generate custom websites and mini-games from text prompts. Voice conversations, rolling out in the Meta AI app, support natural interruption, topic-switching, and language-swapping, while live AI (pointing your camera at the world and asking about what you see) extends capabilities already available on AI glasses.

The distribution advantage is enormous. Muse Spark is being deployed across Meta's entire ecosystem: the standalone Meta AI app, meta.ai, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger, Threads, Ray-Ban Meta glasses, and Oakley Meta glasses. New features like shopping mode (searching Facebook Marketplace alongside web results), side chats in group conversations, and @meta.ai mentions in Threads demonstrate how deeply Meta intends to weave AI into its social fabric.

Meta's framing of "personal superintelligence" is distinct from competitors' AGI narratives. The emphasis is on an AI that "understands your world because it is built on it" — grounded in the relationships, content, and communities already central to users' lives. This is AI not as a standalone product but as a layer that makes Meta's existing products more intelligent and useful. The company is also opening API access via private preview to select partners and plans to open-source future versions, continuing its established pattern of using openness as a competitive strategy.
