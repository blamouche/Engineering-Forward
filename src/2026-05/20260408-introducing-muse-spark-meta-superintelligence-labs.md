# Introducing Muse Spark: Meta's Most Powerful Model Yet
**Source**: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/04/introducing-muse-spark-meta-superintelligence-labs
**Date**: April 8, 2026 (updated May 12, 2026)
**Author**: Meta (press release)
**Keywords**: Meta, Muse Spark, LLM, Meta AI, Meta Superintelligence Labs, multimodal, agentic AI, personal superintelligence, open source

## Elevator pitch
Meta unveiled Muse Spark, the first model from its new Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL), a purpose-built multimodal LLM that powers a rebuilt Meta AI assistant across all Meta platforms, with ambitions toward "personal superintelligence" — an AI that understands your world because it's built on it.

## Takeaways
- Muse Spark is the first release from Meta Superintelligence Labs, a nine-month ground-up rebuild of Meta's AI stack with a "deliberate and scientific approach to model scaling"
- The model powers Meta AI with multimodal perception (vision + text), multiple subagents running in parallel, and separate "Instant" and "Thinking" modes for different task complexity
- Key features include visual coding (websites/games from prompts), shopping mode across Marketplace and the web, and health advice developed with physician input
- The May 12 update expanded access to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger, and Ray-Ban/Oakley Meta glasses, with live AI camera features
- Meta plans to offer API access to select partners and aims to open-source future versions of the Muse model series

## Synthesis
Meta's announcement of Muse Spark represents a significant escalation in the consumer AI platform war. Built by the newly formed Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL) over nine months, Muse Spark is positioned as the foundation for Meta's vision of "personal superintelligence" — an assistant that doesn't just answer questions but understands the user's world through deep integration with Meta's ecosystem of apps and devices.

The technical strategy is notably pragmatic. Rather than racing to build the largest possible model, MSL describes a "deliberate and scientific approach to model scaling where each generation validates and builds on the last before we go bigger." This initial model is "small and fast by design, yet capable enough to reason through complex questions in science, math, and health." It's a measured approach that contrasts with the arms-race rhetoric from other labs, suggesting Meta is optimizing for deployment at massive scale rather than benchmark supremacy.

The integration strategy is where Meta's advantage becomes clear. Muse Spark is rolling out across WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger, Threads, and AI glasses — reaching billions of users through surfaces they already use daily. The multimodal perception features are particularly powerful on AI glasses, where the assistant can see what the user sees in real time. The "side chats" feature in group conversations and "@meta.ai" mentions in Threads embed the AI directly into social contexts, making it ambient rather than requiring users to open a separate app.

The shopping features are strategically significant. By combining Facebook Marketplace listings with web-wide options in a single interface, Meta is positioning Meta AI as a commerce layer that could compete with Google Shopping and Amazon. The ability to @mention specific brands or creators for browsing their public content directly ties commerce to the creator economy Meta has been cultivating.

On the open-source front, Meta's commitment to eventually open-source future Muse models continues its strategy of commoditizing AI infrastructure while differentiating through distribution. API access in private preview to select partners creates an enterprise on-ramp while the consumer products generate the data flywheel. The model is also explicitly built with safeguards for safety and privacy, with a strengthened risk framework — a necessary investment given Meta's history of public trust challenges. The "personal superintelligence" framing is ambitious, but the phased rollout suggests Meta is playing a long game: get the AI onto billions of devices first, then make it indispensable.
