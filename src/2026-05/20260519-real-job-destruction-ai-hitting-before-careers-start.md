# The Real Job Destruction from AI Is Hitting Before Careers Can Start

**Source:** https://insights.som.yale.edu/insights/the-real-job-destruction-from-ai-is-hitting-before-careers-can-start
**Date:** 2026-05-04

## Summary

Yale's Jeffrey Sonnenfeld and co-authors argue that AI's impact on employment is unfolding not through mass layoffs but through the disappearance of entry-level positions. Key points:

- Unemployment among recent college graduates has climbed to nearly 6%, rising twice as fast as the rest of the workforce since 2022
- AI is reducing US employment by roughly 16,000 jobs/month (Goldman Sachs estimate)
- Computer science majors now have more trouble finding jobs than humanities majors (NY Fed research)
- Agentic AI is shifting from task automation to workflow automation, which further erodes entry-level roles
- BCG estimates 10-15% of existing jobs could be eliminated by 2031
- The deeper question: without entry points into the workforce, how will young people develop skills and leadership capacity?

The article highlights a "quiet crisis" where the labor market looks healthy on the surface but entry-level opportunities are being hollowed out.
