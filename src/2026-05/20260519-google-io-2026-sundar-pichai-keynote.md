# I/O 2026: Welcome to the agentic Gemini era
**Source**: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/sundar-pichai-io-2026/
**Date**: May 19, 2026
**Author**: Sundar Pichai
**Keywords**: Google I/O 2026, Gemini, agentic AI, AI Mode, Search, Gemini app, infrastructure, tokens

## Elevator pitch
Sundar Pichai's Google I/O 2026 keynote declares the "agentic Gemini era," revealing that Google processes 3.2 quadrillion tokens monthly — a 7x increase — with 900M Gemini app users and 1B+ AI Mode users.

## Takeaways
- Token processing surged from 480 trillion to 3.2 quadrillion monthly, with 8.5 million developers building on Google's models and APIs processing 19 billion tokens per minute.
- AI Overviews hit 2.5 billion monthly active users; AI Mode surpassed 1 billion monthly users in just one year, becoming the "biggest upgrade to Search ever."
- The Gemini app doubled to 900 million monthly users, and daily requests grew 7x year-over-year.
- New consumer features: Ask YouTube (AI-powered video navigation), Docs Live (voice-powered document creation), and natural conversational AI in Maps and other products.
- Google's full-stack approach — custom silicon, research, models, products — is positioned as a competitive moat enabling faster iteration across billions of users.
- Pichai framed the shift from AI as a feature to AI as a persistent agent, with 50 billion images generated via Nano Banana models reflecting latent creative demand.

## Synthesis
Sundar Pichai's I/O 2026 keynote is less about new model announcements and more about scale and integration. The headline numbers tell the story: 3.2 quadrillion tokens processed per month, up 7x from last year; 900 million Gemini app users; AI Mode crossing 1 billion monthly users in its first year. These aren't vanity metrics — they represent real-world adoption of AI across Google's entire product surface.

The keynote's strategic message is that Google's full-stack advantage — from TPUs and infrastructure to models and products — is compounding. While competitors focus on model performance benchmarks, Google is quietly embedding AI into the products people already use: Search, Maps, YouTube, Docs. The introduction of Ask YouTube and Docs Live reflects a push toward ambient, voice-driven AI that doesn't require users to learn new interfaces.

Pichai's framing of the "agentic Gemini era" marks a rhetorical shift. The narrative is no longer about AI as a chatbot or a search enhancement — it's about Gemini as a persistent, proactive agent woven into daily life. The 24/7 Gemini Spark personal agent, built on Gemini 3.5 Flash, represents the concrete product embodiment of this vision.

The developer story is equally significant: 8.5 million monthly active developers and 375+ enterprise customers each processing over a trillion tokens annually signals that Google's AI platform is gaining real enterprise traction, not just consumer adoption. This positions Google to compete with both OpenAI's API business and cloud providers bundling AI services.

The keynote's subtext is competitive positioning. By emphasizing scale and integration rather than raw model capabilities, Pichai is making the argument that AI leadership in 2026 is about distribution and infrastructure — areas where Google has structural advantages over pure-play AI companies.
