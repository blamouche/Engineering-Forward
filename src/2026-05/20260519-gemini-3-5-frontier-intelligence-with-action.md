# Gemini 3.5: frontier intelligence with action
**Source**: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-5/
**Date**: May 19, 2026
**Author**: Koray Kavukcuoglu, Jeff Dean, Oriol Vinyals, Noam Shazeer
**Keywords**: Gemini 3.5, Google DeepMind, agentic AI, Flash, coding benchmarks, Antigravity, multimodal, enterprise AI

## Elevator pitch
Google releases Gemini 3.5 Flash, a frontier model combining top-tier intelligence with exceptional speed, purpose-built for complex agentic workflows and coding tasks at scale.

## Takeaways
- Gemini 3.5 Flash matches or exceeds larger flagship models on coding (Terminal-Bench 76.2%) and agentic benchmarks (MCP Atlas 83.6%) while being 4x faster than other frontier models.
- Designed for long-horizon agentic tasks: can autonomously plan, build, and iterate on multi-step workflows at half the cost of competitors.
- Deep integration with Antigravity platform enables collaborative subagents for the most demanding enterprise use cases.
- Already adopted by Shopify, Macquarie Bank, Salesforce, Ramp, Xero, and Databricks for real-world automation.
- Gemini 3.5 Pro is in internal use and expected to roll out next month.

## Synthesis
At Google I/O 2026, Google DeepMind unveiled Gemini 3.5 Flash, a model that sits in the top-right quadrant of the Artificial Analysis index — combining frontier intelligence with Flash-series speed. The announcement frames 3.5 Flash not just as a better language model, but as an engine purpose-built for the agentic era.

The performance numbers are striking. On Terminal-Bench 2.1, a challenging coding benchmark, 3.5 Flash scores 76.2%. On MCP Atlas, measuring agentic capabilities, it reaches 83.6%. On CharXiv Reasoning for multimodal understanding, it hits 84.2%. And crucially, it delivers these results at 4x the output token speed of competing frontier models — placing it in a sweet spot where quality and latency no longer trade off against each other.

What makes 3.5 Flash distinct is its integration with Antigravity, Google's agent-first development platform. Under Antigravity, the model can deploy multiple collaborative subagents that tackle problems in parallel. The demos are impressive: synthesizing the AlphaZero paper and coding a fully playable game in six hours, transforming messy legacy codebases to Next.js, or generating interactive web UIs from plain text descriptions. This is not just a chat model — it's an execution engine for complex, multi-step work.

The real-world traction is already visible. Shopify is running parallel subagents to forecast merchant growth globally. Macquarie Bank is piloting customer onboarding automation that reasons over 100+ page documents. Salesforce is integrating it into Agentforce for enterprise task automation. Ramp uses it for smarter OCR on complex invoices, Xero for multi-week tax form workflows, and Databricks for data science diagnostics.

The model is available immediately across the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search, Google AI Studio, Android Studio, and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. Meanwhile, Gemini 3.5 Pro — already used internally at Google — is slated for release next month. This dual-release cadence suggests Google is serious about competing at every tier of the AI stack: consumer, developer, and enterprise.

The broader signal: the AI race is shifting decisively from raw benchmark scores to real-world agentic execution. Speed, cost, and the ability to orchestrate complex workflows over long time horizons are becoming the metrics that matter — and Google is positioning Gemini 3.5 Flash as the model optimized for exactly that.
