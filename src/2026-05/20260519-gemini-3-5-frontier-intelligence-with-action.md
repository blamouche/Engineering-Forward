# Gemini 3.5: frontier intelligence with action
**Source**: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-5/
**Date**: May 19, 2026
**Author**: Koray Kavukcuoglu, Jeff Dean, Oriol Vinyals, Noam Shazeer
**Keywords**: Gemini 3.5, Google DeepMind, agentic AI, Flash, Antigravity, coding, multimodal

## Elevator pitch
Google DeepMind unveils Gemini 3.5 Flash, a frontier model that combines top-tier reasoning with exceptional speed, purpose-built for long-horizon agentic workflows and coding at scale.

## Takeaways
- Gemini 3.5 Flash matches flagship-level intelligence while being 4x faster in output tokens per second than other frontier models, landing in the top-right quadrant of the Artificial Analysis index.
- The model excels at agentic and coding benchmarks: Terminal-Bench 2.1 (76.2%), MCP Atlas (83.6%), and multimodal understanding via CharXiv Reasoning (84.2%).
- Paired with Google's Antigravity harness, 3.5 Flash can deploy collaborative subagents for multi-step workflows like codebase migrations, game development, and financial document preparation.
- Enterprise partners (Shopify, Macquarie Bank, Salesforce, Ramp, Xero, Databricks) are already using it for production workloads ranging from customer onboarding to autonomous tax form processing.
- 3.5 Flash powers the new Gemini Spark personal AI agent and AI Mode in Search, while 3.5 Pro is in internal use and rolling out next month.

## Synthesis
At Google I/O 2026, Google DeepMind introduced Gemini 3.5, a new model family designed explicitly around the marriage of frontier intelligence and agentic action. The initial release, 3.5 Flash, represents a strategic bet: that the next frontier isn't just smarter models, but models that can act reliably over long time horizons without sacrificing speed or cost.

The performance claims are substantial. 3.5 Flash achieves scores that rival or exceed recent large flagship models on key coding and agentic benchmarks — Terminal-Bench 2.1 (76.2%), MCP Atlas (83.6%), and GDPval-AA (1656 Elo) — while delivering output tokens at four times the speed of competing frontier models. This speed-intelligence combination lands it in the top-right quadrant of the Artificial Analysis index, a position no previous Flash-tier model has occupied.

The architectural insight is less about raw model scale and more about system-level design. 3.5 Flash's capabilities are amplified by Antigravity, Google's updated agent harness, which enables multi-agent orchestration: builder and player agents that self-improve in loops, subagents that parallelize complex analysis, and workflows that transform legacy codebases or synthesize research papers into playable games over hours rather than days.

Enterprise adoption signals are concrete. Shopify runs parallel subagents for global merchant growth forecasting. Macquarie Bank pilots customer onboarding by reasoning over 100+ page documents. Salesforce integrates it into Agentforce for multi-turn enterprise task automation. Ramp uses its multimodal understanding for smarter OCR on invoices. Xero deploys agents for autonomous multi-week tax workflows. Databricks leverages it for real-time data science monitoring and remediation.

On the consumer side, 3.5 Flash becomes the default model for the Gemini app and powers Gemini Spark, a 24/7 personal AI agent. This shift — from on-demand chat to always-on agent — marks a significant evolution in how Google positions Gemini as an ambient, persistent assistant rather than a query-response tool.

The announcement also previews 3.5 Pro, already in internal use and slated for release next month, suggesting Google is compressing its release cadence. The broader message is clear: the agentic era isn't coming — it's here, and Google intends to compete on both intelligence and the operational infrastructure that makes agents reliable in production.
