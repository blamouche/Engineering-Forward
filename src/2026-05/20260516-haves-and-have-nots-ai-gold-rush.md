# The haves and have-nots of the AI gold rush

**Source:** TechCrunch
**Author:** Anthony Ha
**Date:** May 16, 2026
**URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/16/the-haves-and-have-nots-of-the-ai-gold-rush

## Summary

Menlo Ventures partner Deedy Das described the current AI boom as producing the worst wealth-divide he's ever seen in tech. An estimated ~10,000 people (founders and employees at OpenAI, Anthropic, Nvidia, etc.) have hit retirement wealth of $20M+, while layoffs are accelerating and many software engineers feel their skills are becoming obsolete. The same technology creating lottery-ticket wealth is simultaneously eating the fallback options for everyone else.

## Key Points

- **~10K people** have achieved $20M+ wealth from AI companies
- **Layoffs in full swing** across the tech industry
- **Software engineers** feel their "life's skill is no longer useful"
- **Deep malaise** about work and its future
- **Critical response:** Some argue these people are "incredibly fortunate" and can simply choose to be happy
- **Novel dynamic:** AI is simultaneously the lottery ticket and the thing destroying fallback careers
- **SF described as "frenetic"** with worst outcome divide ever seen

## Why It Matters

The AI gold rush is creating an unprecedented wealth concentration problem within tech itself. The dynamic is unique: the technology making a small group fabulously wealthy is the same one undermining the employability of the broader engineering workforce. This internal contradiction could shape political and labor dynamics around AI for years.
