# Gemini app rolling out 'Extended' thinking level, new 3rd-party app integrations
**Source**: https://9to5google.com/2026/05/17/gemini-app-thinking-level
**Date**: May 17, 2026
**Author**: Abner Li
**Keywords**: Gemini, Google, thinking level, AI, Canva, Instacart, OpenTable, app integrations

## Elevator pitch
Google is rolling out an "Extended" thinking level in the Gemini app model picker for Flash and Pro models, while preparing third-party app integrations with Canva, Instacart, and OpenTable ahead of Google I/O 2026.

## Takeaways
- The new "Thinking level" option appears in the Gemini app model picker when selecting Gemini 3 Flash or 3.1 Pro, with "Standard" and "Extended" options.
- Extended thinking mirrors Google AI Studio's existing Low/Medium/High thinking levels, now coming to the consumer app for deeper reasoning on complex queries.
- New third-party app integrations are being prepared: @Canva (design creation and asset management), @Instacart (grocery shopping via natural language), and @OpenTable (restaurant reservations).
- Canva integration allows generating designs from prompts, accessing existing designs, replying to comments, and editing AI-generated images.
- Instacart integration lets users add ingredients to carts, find stores carrying specific products, and combine recipe discovery with shopping.
- OpenTable enables booking, changing, and canceling reservations through natural language conversation with Gemini.
- These features are rolling out ahead of Google I/O 2026, suggesting a broader push toward making Gemini an action-oriented assistant beyond just information retrieval.

## Synthesis
Google's Gemini app is evolving from a pure information tool into an action-oriented assistant. Abner Li at 9to5Google reports two significant feature rollouts ahead of Google I/O 2026: an "Extended" thinking level for deeper reasoning, and integrations with third-party apps that let Gemini take real-world actions on behalf of users.

The thinking level option appears in the Gemini app's model picker when selecting Gemini 3 Flash or 3.1 Pro (not available in Thinking mode itself, which already runs at maximum depth). Users can toggle between "Standard" and "Extended" — a simplified version of Google AI Studio's Low/Medium/High thinking controls. This brings the power of extended reasoning (which lets the model "think longer" about complex problems) to the consumer app, making it accessible without switching to the developer-focused AI Studio interface.

The third-party app integrations represent a more strategic shift. Beyond existing integrations with GitHub, OpenStax, Spotify, and WhatsApp, support documents reveal Canva, Instacart, and OpenTable are coming. The Canva integration is particularly deep: users can generate designs from prompts, access existing templates and project files, move assets between folders, reply to comments, and even generate AI images in Gemini then edit them in Canva. Instacart lets users add groceries to their cart by simply describing a recipe or listing ingredients. OpenTable handles the full reservation lifecycle — finding, booking, modifying, and canceling — all through natural language.

These integrations position Gemini as a genuine assistant that bridges information and action, competing in the same space as ChatGPT's plugins and Claude's tool use. The timing ahead of Google I/O 2026 suggests this is part of a broader strategy to make Gemini the central hub for digital tasks.
