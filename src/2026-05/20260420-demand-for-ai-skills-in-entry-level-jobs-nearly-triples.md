# Demand for AI Skills in Entry-Level Jobs Nearly Triples Since Fall 2025

**Source:** [NACE](https://www.naceweb.org/job-market/trends-and-predictions/demand-for-ai-skills-in-entry-level-jobs-nearly-triples-since-fall-2025)
**Date:** 2026-04-20
**Author:** Mary Gatta, Ph.D.
**Organization:** National Association of Colleges and Employers (NACE)

## Key Findings (Job Outlook 2026 Spring Update)

- **More than one-third** of entry-level jobs now require AI skills — nearly triple the share from fall 2025
- AI skills are more prevalent in job descriptions now than just 6 months ago
- 28% of employers seek early career talent who can use AI; nearly 60% assign interns projects using AI tools

## AI's Effect on Entry-Level Work

- Majority of employers are exploring AI technology, considering ethics, and discussing productivity/task-level changes
- ~20% are discussing AI's impact on job design and staffing
- **67%+** are considering how AI may be used within tasks; just 11% are discussing AI replacing positions
- **Over half** report AI is NOT reducing tasks entry-level workers perform; ~27% say it has
- AI skills sought enable workers to **complement, not replace** human work

## Implications

- **Higher education:** Must incorporate AI into curriculum across all disciplines, consult regularly with employers
- **Recruiters:** Work with career services to communicate organization-specific AI needs, identify skills gaps
- **Career services:** Guide students on AI skills employers seek, connect to career readiness, help demonstrate critical thinking + technical skills for effective AI use

## Key Insight

AI is largely reshaping — not replacing — early career talent and the skills needed to succeed. The tripling demand in just 6 months signals a structural shift in what's expected of new graduates.
