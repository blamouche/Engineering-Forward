# Nvidia partners with Ineffable Intelligence to build 'next frontier' of AI
**Source**: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/13/google-deepmind-alumni-startup-partners-nvidia-superintelligence.html
**Date**: May 13, 2026
**Author**: Kai Nicol-Schwarz
**Keywords**: Nvidia, Ineffable Intelligence, David Silver, reinforcement learning, superintelligence, DeepMind, Jensen Huang, AI chips

## Elevator pitch
Nvidia announces an engineering partnership with Ineffable Intelligence — the reinforcement learning startup founded by DeepMind's David Silver that raised a record $1.1B seed round — to co-design infrastructure for AI systems that learn continuously from experience rather than human data.

## Takeaways
- Ineffable Intelligence, founded in late 2025 by UCL professor and former DeepMind RL lead David Silver, focuses on reinforcement learning instead of training on human data
- The record $1.1B seed round was co-led by Sequoia and Lightspeed, with Nvidia, DST Global, Index, Google, and the UK Sovereign AI Fund participating
- Jensen Huang: "The next frontier of AI is superlearners — systems that learn continuously from experience"
- Ineffable joins a wave of Big Tech alumni startups: Recursive Superintelligence ($650M), AMI Labs ($1B), Periodic Labs, Humans&
- The partnership will use Nvidia's Grace Blackwell and Vera Rubin platforms for large-scale RL infrastructure

## Synthesis
The partnership between Nvidia and Ineffable Intelligence signals a strategic bet on reinforcement learning as the next paradigm shift beyond current LLM approaches. David Silver, the researcher behind AlphaGo and DeepMind's RL breakthroughs, is arguing that the "easier problem" — building systems that know what humans already know — has been largely solved. The harder problem is systems that discover new knowledge autonomously through experience.

Nvidia's involvement is both financial (participating in the $1.1B seed round) and deeply technical (engineering-level collaboration to co-design RL infrastructure at scale). For Nvidia, this is strategic positioning: if RL becomes the dominant training paradigm for next-generation AI, the chipmaker wants its Grace Blackwell and Vera Rubin platforms to be the default hardware. Jensen Huang's framing — "superlearners" — is a deliberate rebranding of RL for the enterprise era.

Ineffable is part of a broader trend of elite AI researchers leaving Big Tech to found independent labs. David Silver (DeepMind), Tim Rocktäschel (Recursive Superintelligence, $650M), Yann LeCun (AMI Labs, $1B) — the pattern is consistent. These researchers are betting that the next architectural breakthrough won't come from inside the large labs, which are increasingly focused on productization and scale rather than fundamental research.

The UK angle is notable: Ineffable is London-based, backed by the UK's Sovereign AI Fund, reflecting Britain's ambition to remain a pole in the global AI race despite lacking the compute scale of the US or China.
