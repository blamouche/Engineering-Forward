# Polymarket Launches Private Company Trading for AI Unicorns
**Source**: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/19/polymarket-launches-private-company-trading-so-investors-can-speculate-on-anthropic-openai.html
**Date**: May 19, 2026
**Author**: Contessa Brewer
**Keywords**: polymarket, prediction-markets, private-companies, openai, anthropic, ipo, valuation, nasdaq

## Elevator pitch
Polymarket is launching prediction markets tied to private company milestones — valuations, IPO timing, secondary-market activity — for companies like OpenAI and Anthropic, letting ordinary investors speculate on unicorns they can't buy shares in, with Nasdaq Private Market serving as the exclusive resolution data provider.

## Takeaways
- Polymarket's new contracts let traders take positions on events like whether OpenAI will IPO above $1 trillion before 2027 or whether Anthropic reaches a $500B valuation in 2026
- Nasdaq Private Market (NPM) provides resolution data and will make private company valuation data publicly available for free for the first time
- Over 1,600 companies are unicorns valued at $1B+, but ordinary investors cannot invest directly — these contracts provide exposure without equity ownership
- NPM data shows Anthropic's estimated share price at $477 as of May 5, up over 1,500%, with a last trade at $234
- Kalshi offers some IPO event contracts but doesn't cover private company valuations, giving Polymarket an edge in this category

## Synthesis
Polymarket's expansion into private company prediction markets represents a significant evolution in how retail investors can engage with the AI boom. The fundamental tension it addresses is well-known: OpenAI, Anthropic, and hundreds of other unicorns have created enormous value and brand recognition while remaining completely inaccessible to ordinary investors. Only accredited investors, institutions, or the exceptionally well-connected can invest directly. Polymarket's contracts don't offer equity — no shares, no voting rights — but they provide a way to take a financial position on specific private-market outcomes.

The partnership with Nasdaq Private Market is the key infrastructure play. NPM will serve as the exclusive resolution data provider, determining whether contracts pay out based on actual private-market activity. In a notable move, NPM will make valuation data publicly available for free — no subscription required. This transparency could itself become a market signal, as secondary-market prices, funding rounds, and IPO timing have historically been fragmented and opaque compared to public equities.

The specific contracts listed illustrate the zeitgeist: will OpenAI have a $1 trillion-plus IPO before 2027? Will Anthropic reach a $500 billion valuation in 2026? Will Anthropic be valued higher than OpenAI at any point this year? These are the questions that dominate AI industry discourse, and Polymarket is financializing them. NPM's data shows Anthropic's estimated share price at $477 as of early May, up more than 1,500%, with bid-ask spreads wide enough ($261 bid, $188 offer) to reflect genuine uncertainty. For institutional investors, liquid prediction markets around private-company events could become another real-time signal — particularly valuable in AI, where public comps are imperfect and fresh private-market data is scarce.
