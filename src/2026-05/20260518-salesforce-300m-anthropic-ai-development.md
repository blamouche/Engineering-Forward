# Salesforce Sets Aside $300M for AI Development with Anthropic
**Source**: https://www.techzine.eu/news/devops/141412/salesforce-sets-aside-300m-for-ai-development-with-anthropic/
**Date**: May 18, 2026
**Author**: Mels Dees
**Keywords**: Salesforce, Anthropic, Claude, AI tokens, enterprise AI, Marc Benioff, Slack, Agentforce, AI coding

## Elevator pitch
Salesforce plans to spend approximately $300 million on Anthropic AI tokens this year, primarily for AI-assisted software development, making it one of Anthropic's largest customers while also building new coding capabilities into Slack.

## Takeaways
- CEO Marc Benioff revealed the $300M token spend on the All-In podcast, emphasizing AI's impact on faster, cheaper software development and customer service
- The investment builds on Salesforce's existing equity stake in Anthropic — acquired partly because Salesforce couldn't get a position in OpenAI
- Slack is getting new AI coding capabilities, building on dozens of AI features already launched for Slackbot (meeting summarization, task execution, CRM integration), all running on Claude
- Benioff advocates for an AI routing layer: complex tasks go to powerful models like Claude, simple tasks to smaller/cheaper models — a strategy to manage operational costs
- Agentforce, Salesforce's AI agent division, is growing strongly; Slack is projected to generate ~$3 billion in revenue this year

## Synthesis
Salesforce's $300 million commitment to Anthropic tokens represents the maturing of enterprise AI from experimental line item to fixed operational cost. CEO Marc Benioff disclosed the figure on the All-In podcast, framing it primarily as a software development investment — AI coding tools are now so impactful that Salesforce can build software "faster and more cost-effectively" than before.

The spending is centered on tokens — the processing units that large language models consume — making it a recurring operational expense rather than a one-time capital investment. An annual $300 million token bill immediately positions Salesforce as one of Anthropic's largest customers, deepening a relationship that already includes an equity stake. Benioff noted that stake was partly motivated by Salesforce's inability to secure a position in OpenAI.

The announcement comes with a product roadmap reveal: Slack, the collaboration platform Salesforce acquired in 2021, will gain coding capabilities. This builds on an extensive AI expansion earlier in 2026 that added dozens of Claude-powered features to Slackbot, including automatic meeting summarization, task execution via external tools, and CRM process support. Slack is projected to generate approximately $3 billion in revenue this year.

Benioff's commentary on AI cost management reveals a maturing operational philosophy. He expects organizations to deploy an "additional software layer" that routes AI requests intelligently — complex tasks directed to powerful models like Claude, simpler tasks to cheaper alternatives. "Large models are more expensive to use than lighter variants or open-source alternatives such as Meta's Llama," he noted, signaling that enterprise AI strategy is entering its cost-optimization phase.

The broader context is Salesforce's ongoing AI-driven restructuring. Benioff previously noted AI agents enabled significant downsizing of support teams, and now similar efficiency gains are materializing in engineering. Agentforce, the company's AI agent division, posted strong recurring revenue growth. The $300M token commitment, combined with Slack's AI integration and Agentforce's growth trajectory, paints a picture of a company betting heavily that AI isn't just a feature — it's the new cost structure of enterprise software development and delivery.
