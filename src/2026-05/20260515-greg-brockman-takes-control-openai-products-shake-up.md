# Greg Brockman Officially Takes Control of OpenAI's Products in Latest Shake-Up
**Source**: https://www.wired.com/story/openai-reorg-greg-brockman-product/
**Date**: May 15, 2026
**Author**: Maxwell Zeff
**Keywords**: OpenAI, Greg Brockman, reorganization, ChatGPT, Codex, executive shake-up, Fidji Simo, super app, IPO, product strategy

## Elevator pitch
OpenAI announced a major executive reorganization consolidating ChatGPT, Codex, and its developer API under cofounder Greg Brockman's product leadership, as the company races toward an IPO and a unified "super app" experience.

## Takeaways
1. Greg Brockman, previously interim product lead during Fidji Simo's medical leave, now officially leads all product strategy in addition to his AI infrastructure responsibilities
2. ChatGPT, the coding agent Codex, and developer APIs are being merged into one core product team, with Codex increasingly powering both consumer and enterprise offerings
3. Codex head Thibault Sottiaux now leads core product and platform teams, including the "super app" combining Codex, ChatGPT, and the Atlas browser
4. ChatGPT's longtime head Nick Turley moves to enterprise products; former Instagram VP Ashley Alexander takes over consumer products
5. The restructuring comes ahead of OpenAI's planned IPO later this year, amid mounting competition from Anthropic (coding) and Google (consumer chatbots)

## Synthesis

OpenAI is undergoing its most significant executive reorganization in recent memory, as the company positions itself for an IPO while facing intensifying competition on multiple fronts. The changes, announced to staff on May 15, cement cofounder and president Greg Brockman's control over all of OpenAI's product strategy.

The reorganization was set in motion when Fidji Simo, OpenAI's CEO of AGI deployment, went on medical leave last month. Brockman stepped in on an interim basis, and the arrangement has now been made permanent. Simo remains on leave and reportedly worked with Brockman on these organizational changes; OpenAI expects her return.

At the heart of the restructuring is a recognition that OpenAI's products are naturally converging. ChatGPT, Codex, and the developer API are being folded into one core product team. The rationale is clear: Codex is increasingly powering both consumer and enterprise offerings, which are gaining the ability to perform digital tasks autonomously on behalf of users. As Brockman put it in a staff memo, the goal is to "execute with maximum focus toward the agentic future, to win across both consumer and enterprise."

The personnel moves reflect these priorities. Thibault Sottiaux, who led Codex to become one of OpenAI's fastest-growing products ever, now oversees core product and platform teams — including the much-anticipated "super app" that aims to combine Codex, ChatGPT, and the Atlas web browser into a unified desktop experience. Nick Turley, who guided ChatGPT from launch to over 900 million weekly active users, moves to enterprise products. Ashley Alexander, a former Instagram VP who had been leading health products, takes over consumer products.

These changes follow a turbulent period for OpenAI's executive ranks. Last month saw the departures of Kevin Weil (head of AI workspace for scientists), Bill Peebles (head of Sora), and Srinivas Narayanan (CTO of enterprise applications). The company is clearly attempting to streamline its leadership structure and focus resources on the products that matter most for its growth story.

The competitive context is equally important. Anthropic has been gaining ground in coding domains, while Google continues to push hard in consumer chatbots. OpenAI's leaders hope that simplifying the product lineup — converging everything toward a unified agentic experience — will sharpen the company's edge ahead of what could be one of the largest tech IPOs in history. The reorganization is not just about internal efficiency; it is about crafting a coherent narrative for public markets.
