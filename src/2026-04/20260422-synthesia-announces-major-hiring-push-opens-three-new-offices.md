# Synthesia announces major hiring push, opens three new offices
**Source**: https://sifted.eu/articles/synthesia-hiring-push-new-offices
**Date**: Unknown
**Author**: Unknown
**Keywords**: Synthesia, hiring, Europe, AI video, enterprise software

## Elevator pitch
Synthesia says it will expand headcount by 70% and open offices in Austin, Paris and Berlin as enterprise demand pushes the company toward $200m ARR.

## Takeaways
- Synthesia plans to increase headcount by roughly 70% this year.
- The company says it has more than 600 employees today and expects to add more than 400.
- It is opening new offices in Austin, Paris and Berlin and expanding in Zurich.
- The company says it is on track to reach $200m ARR by the end of 2026.
- Growth is being driven by enterprise demand, especially in Europe and the US.

## Synthesis
This Sifted article presents Synthesia’s expansion as a sign that enterprise AI video tooling is moving from fast growth into scaled execution. The company says it will grow headcount by 70% this year, adding more than 400 people to a workforce that already exceeds 600, while opening offices in Austin, Paris and Berlin and increasing its presence in Zurich.

The operational logic is clear. Synthesia is not only investing in product development, it is also building a larger field organisation around enterprise customers. The company says many of the hires in the new hubs will focus on go-to-market and customer success. That matters because AI video generation for enterprise communications is no longer being framed as a speculative category. It is being sold as software that needs regional coverage, customer support and local commercial teams.

The article also ties this hiring push to concrete business momentum. Synthesia says it is on track to reach $200m in annual recurring revenue by the end of 2026 and that it has tripled the number of contracts worth more than $100k over the past year. Those numbers suggest the company is moving upmarket with larger customers and longer-term commitments, not just adding volume at the low end.

Another useful signal is geographic. The new offices in Paris and Berlin point to continued confidence in continental European demand, while Austin strengthens the company’s US footprint. Management frames the move as a way to attract local talent and stay close to customers in regions where usage is growing quickly. For an enterprise AI company, that is a sign of category maturity: expansion follows adoption density.

Overall, the article shows Synthesia behaving less like an experimental AI startup and more like a late-stage software company scaling distribution around a product that has already found enterprise fit. The key takeaway is not just that the company is hiring aggressively. It is that enterprise AI video appears to be generating enough repeatable demand to justify a much broader commercial and customer-facing footprint.
