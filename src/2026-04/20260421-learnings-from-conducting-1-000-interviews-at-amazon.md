- Technical interviews are going through a big change, thanks to AI tools: some companies are bringing in new, AI-assisted types of interviews, while others are trying to make “pre-AI” type interviews work.

Steve Huynh was an engineer at Amazon for 17 years – I previously did a podcast episode with him on the reality of being a principal engineer at Amazon. During this time, Steve conducted nearly 1,000 interviews, of which around 600 were Bar Raiser ones. Bar Raiser interviews are unique to Amazon: it’s an interview conducted by someone outside of the hiring team, with the goal of ensuring that the new hire raises the company’s talent bar. After leaving the e-commerce giant, Steve spent 2 years researching and writing the book Technical Behavioral Interview: An Insider’s Guide .
