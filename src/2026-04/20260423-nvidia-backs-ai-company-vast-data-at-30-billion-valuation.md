# Nvidia backs AI company Vast Data at $30 billion valuation

**Source**: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/22/nvidia-backs-ai-company-vast-data.html
**Date**: April 22, 2026
**Author**: CNBC
**Keywords**: Vast Data, Nvidia, AI infrastructure, funding, storage, CoreWeave

## Elevator pitch
Vast Data has raised $1 billion at a $30 billion valuation, showing that investors see AI data infrastructure, not just model labs, as one of the biggest leverage points in the current boom.

## Takeaways
- Vast raised a huge round led by Drive Capital and Access Industries, with Nvidia participating.
- The company positions itself as infrastructure for managing AI data at very large scale.
- Customers include CoreWeave, Cursor, Mistral, and the U.S. Air Force.
- Vast says it passed $4 billion in cumulative bookings and more than $500 million in committed ARR.
- The round underscores how much value is accruing to the data and storage layer of AI infrastructure.

## Synthesis
Vast Data’s new financing is a reminder that AI infrastructure winners will not only be model providers and GPU vendors. Vast sits lower in the stack, focused on the data infrastructure needed to feed and manage large-scale AI systems, and investors are now valuing that layer like a strategic chokepoint. A $1 billion round at a $30 billion valuation signals that the market believes AI storage, data movement, and system architecture are becoming core bottlenecks rather than commodity plumbing.

That reading makes sense. Training and serving large AI systems is not just a compute problem. It is a data problem: how quickly clusters can ingest inputs, checkpoint models, retrieve context, and keep accelerators busy instead of waiting on storage. Companies that solve those constraints can capture leverage across many customers at once. Vast’s customer list, including CoreWeave, Cursor, Mistral, and the U.S. Air Force, suggests its pitch is resonating across both commercial and government AI environments.

Nvidia’s presence in the round is also meaningful. The company has increasingly used investments to reinforce its role at the center of the AI stack, and backing infrastructure partners helps extend that influence beyond chips. If AI clusters become more complex and capital-intensive, the ecosystem around Nvidia hardware becomes more valuable too.

The wider lesson is that the AI boom keeps rediscovering old systems truths. Breakthrough applications often depend on unglamorous layers becoming good enough and scalable enough. Vast is being priced like one of those layers. Whether that valuation proves durable is another question, but the direction of travel is clear: the market increasingly believes that data infrastructure is one of the highest-leverage positions in AI.
