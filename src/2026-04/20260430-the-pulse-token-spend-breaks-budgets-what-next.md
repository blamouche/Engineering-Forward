Last week, we covered the slightly perverse trend of “tokenmaxxing” across the industry, where devs run agents with the sole aim of boosting their personal “token stats” in an effort to rank higher on internal token leaderboards, and not be seen as a Luddite who doesn’t use AI tools enough compared to peers. This week, I spoke with a software engineer at a large company and another at a seed-stage place. Both shared almost identical stories: at their latest all-hands, company leadership expressed concerns about the fast-rising costs of tokens.
