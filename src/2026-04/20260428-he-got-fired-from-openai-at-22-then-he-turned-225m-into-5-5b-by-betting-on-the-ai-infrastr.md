Leopold Aschenbrenner is either the most important investor no one on Wall Street has heard of, or the most dangerous kind of genius: one who might actually be right . In the twelve months following the launch of his hedge fund, Situational Awareness LP , in September 2024, he grew its disclosed U.S. equity exposure from roughly $225 million to $5.52 billion 😳

He did it without owning a single share of Nvidia, Microsoft, Amazon, Google, or Meta. His largest position was in a fuel-cell company . His second-largest was call options on a GPU cloud startup most investors had never heard of. His third-largest was call options on a chipmaker Wall Street had largely written off .

This is not a story about luck . It is a story about what happens when a mind trained in growth economics, existential risk theory, and AI scaling laws is given access to public markets and the conviction to act on conclusions everyone else considers too extreme to take seriously.

Leopold Aschenbrenner didn’t set out to run a hedge fund. He set out to understand how the world ends, and then figure out how to invest in the companies that would prevent it.

Born in Germany around 2001 to two medical doctors, he enrolled at Columbia University at fifteen. In 2021, at nineteen, he graduated as valedictorian with a double major in economics and mathematics-statistics, the same year he won a grant from Tyler Cowen’s Emergent Ventures. He did research at Oxford’s Global Priorities Institute alongside Philip Trammell, co-authoring a growth-economics paper that would later become the philosophical licence for his entire fund.

He interned at the FTX Future Fund, the philanthropic arm of Sam Bankman-Fried’s empire, and watched from the inside as one of the most spectacular coordination failures in recent financial history imploded. He resigned before the collapse. He then joined OpenAI’s Superalignment team in 2023, co-authored a paper on AI safety presented at a leading machine learning conference, and wrote an internal memo to the OpenAI board warning about Chinese espionage and the company’s security posture. OpenAI fired him in April 2024.

Two months later, he published Situational Awareness: The Decade Ahead , a 165-page essay series making a single sweeping claim: AGI is coming by 2027, the world is not ready, and the companies building the physical infrastructure to make it possible are the most undervalued assets in financial markets .
