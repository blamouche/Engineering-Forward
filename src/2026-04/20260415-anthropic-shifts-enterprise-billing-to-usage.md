## Takeaways
- Anthropic has unbundled Claude Code usage from enterprise seat fees, moving customers to per-token billing amid a compute crunch.
- Retool's founder already switched to OpenAI over uptime.
- Session caps, cache cuts, and OpenClaw meters show the subsidy bleeding out.

- Organizations on older seat-based plans with fixed usage allowances must migrate by their next contract renewal or lose the grandfathered terms.
