# Build with Veo 3.1 Lite, Our Most Cost-Effective Video Generation Model

**Source**: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/veo-3-1-lite/
**Date**: 2026-03-31
**Author**: Alisa Fortin, Guillaume Vernade
**Keywords**: Veo, video generation, Google DeepMind, Gemini API, AI models, developers

## Elevator pitch
Google launches Veo 3.1 Lite, a cost-effective video generation model available via the Gemini API at less than 50% of the cost of Veo 3.1 Fast.

## Takeaways
- Veo 3.1 Lite is Google's most cost-effective video generation model, under 50% the cost of Veo 3.1 Fast
- Supports Text-to-Video and Image-to-Video with flexible framing (16:9 and 9:16)
- Available in 720p and 1080p resolutions with customizable duration (4s, 6s, or 8s)
- Available to developers via paid tier on Gemini API and Google AI Studio
- Veo 3.1 Fast pricing will also be reduced on April 7

## Synthesis
Google DeepMind has introduced Veo 3.1 Lite, rounding out the Veo 3.1 model family with a price-conscious option targeting high-volume video applications. This model is positioned as the cost-efficient tier for developers who want practical video generation capabilities without the full cost of Veo 3.1 Fast, while maintaining the same generation speed.

The model supports both Text-to-Video and Image-to-Video workflows, making it versatile for different use cases. Developers can choose between landscape (16:9) and portrait (9:16) aspect ratios, and select from 720p or 1080p resolutions. Video duration is customizable at 4, 6, or 8 seconds, with pricing that adjusts accordingly — giving builders fine-grained cost control for their applications.

The release signals Google's commitment to making video generation economically viable at scale. By offering a full model family (Lite, Fast, and presumably more capable tiers), Google is applying a classic cloud platform strategy: let developers start cheap, then upgrade as needs grow.

For engineering teams, the key insight is that this positions Gemini API as a serious competitor in the generative video space — one that can now serve both prototyping budgets and production-grade use cases. The upcoming price reduction on Veo 3.1 Fast further underscores a strategy to grow developer adoption through accessible pricing rather than premium-only positioning.

Veo 3.1 Lite is available immediately via the Gemini API paid tier and Google AI Studio. This move mirrors how cloud providers have historically expanded markets by offering tiered entry points, democratizing access to capabilities previously reserved for large enterprises with significant compute budgets.
