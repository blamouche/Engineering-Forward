After more than a month since its release, customers continue to purchase the $599 laptop faster than Apple can produce them. MacBook Neo inventory has remained constrained since launch Apple unveiled MacBook Neo on March 4, opening pre-orders shortly after the announcement. MacBook Neo arrived in stores one week later on March 11. Launch inventory quickly sold out , and Apple hasn’t caught up with demand since. MacBook Neo comes in four colors (silver, blush, citrus, and indigo) and two configurations (256GB or 512GB with Touch ID). Each option shows delivery times between May 1 and May 8 when ordered today. Where to find MacBook Neo stock Shoppers may have a different experience at Apple retail store locations.
