Agentic AI is an advanced form of automation, capable of achieving complex goals with limited human direction. With agentic AI, machine learning (ML) models simulate human-style decision-making to solve complex problems. Instead of requiring a sequence of prompts to complete multiple actions, agentic AI operates automatically, interacting with traditional programming in fundamental systems, configuring operations in real time to optimize business outcomes. Using ML agents as tools to coordinate the completion of multiple subtasks to achieve larger objectives, agentic AI can accomplish specific goals with minimum human intervention.
