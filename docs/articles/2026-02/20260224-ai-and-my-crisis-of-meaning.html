AI and My Crisis of Meaning

2026-02-24 AI, software engineering, freelancing, productivity, career anxiety
Read the source article

Quick summary

An independent developer describes how AI coding tools made custom software absurdly fast to deliver—and why that speed is triggering a personal “meaning” crisis about long-term value, income, and identity.

Key takeaways

  • AI coding tools are compressing “weeks of work” into hours, especially for CRUD-style apps and internal tools.
  • The short-term opportunity is an information asymmetry: builders who understand agents can arbitrage speed and ship faster than clients.
  • That asymmetry feels temporary; the author fears clients will soon self-serve with agents.
  • Psychological impact matters: productivity gains can translate into anxiety, not relief, when your value proposition is tied to the old effort model.
  • There’s a subtle trust boundary: using AI in high-stakes compliance/audit situations can backfire if it erodes credibility.
