CoderPad State of Tech Hiring 2026

2026-02-11 Russell Heimlich tech hiring, AI tools, developer skills, recruitment, technical assessment, workforce trends, 2026
Read the source article

Quick summary

A survey of 650+ global participants finds that AI has shifted from optional productivity tool to essential infrastructure for developers, while reshaping the skills companies value most and the methods used to evaluate candidates.

Key takeaways

  • 82% of developers find generative AI useful (up from 76% in 2025); 54% would face significant productivity drops without it
  • Writing new code is declining as a critical skill; debugging, fine-tuning, and system design are growing in importance
  • Industry remains divided on AI in assessments: 34% ban it entirely, 46% allow it with varying constraints
  • 53% of hiring leaders anticipate budget increases (up from 37% in 2025), with quality of hire as the top priority for 60%
  • AI hasn't eliminated developer demand—it has intensified competition for talent proficient in AI-augmented workflows
