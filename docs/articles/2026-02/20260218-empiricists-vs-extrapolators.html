Empiricists vs. Extrapolators

Feb 18, 2026 Samuel Hammond AI forecasts, scaling laws, epistemology, uncertainty, policy
Read the source article

Quick summary

Samuel Hammond describes a divide in AI forecasting between empiricists who wait for evidence and extrapolators who project from scaling trends.

Key takeaways

  • The “empiricist vs extrapolator” split shapes debates about AI timelines and impact.
  • Empiricists emphasize uncertainty and complex-system unpredictability.
  • Extrapolators argue that scaling laws make rapid progress forecastable.
  • The key disagreement is about how much evidence is enough to act on.
  • Institutions move slowly, so forecasting is necessary even when imperfect.
