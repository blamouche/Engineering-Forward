Anthropic updates Claude Cowork tool built to give the average office worker a productivity boost

2026-02-24 CNBC / Reuters (via CNBC) enterprise AI, knowledge work, connectors, plugins, Anthropic
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic expands Claude Cowork from a research preview toward an enterprise product by adding connectors (Drive, Gmail, DocuSign, FactSet) and customizable plugins meant to embed company workflows and knowledge into everyday office tasks.

Key takeaways

  • Connectors are the product wedge: real-time access to internal tools is framed as what makes Cowork meaningfully different from chat.
  • Plugins encode institutional workflows across functions (finance, engineering, HR), pushing Cowork toward “enterprise app platform.”
  • Anthropic positions Cowork as the knowledge-worker analogue to Claude Code for engineers.
  • The company claims it isn’t yet seeing broad labor displacement, but expects uneven impacts across roles.
  • Market reaction is sensitive: even anticipated announcements can swing software stocks.
