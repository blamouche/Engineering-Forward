OpenAI's New Codex App Is Gold for Senior Engineers

February 2, 2026 Dan Shipper; Katie Parrott Codex app, GUI, coding agents, workflows, automations
Read the source article

Quick summary

A hands‑on review of OpenAI’s Codex desktop app, arguing that its GUI makes agentic coding more manageable for serious workflows.

Key takeaways

  • The Codex app is positioned as a command center that replaces terminal‑only use.
  • A sidebar enables multi‑agent orchestration with less workflow friction.
  • Built‑in skills, automations, and cloud‑to‑local sync expand how agents are used.
  • The UI makes diffs and state visible, reducing the cost of oversight.
  • The app is competitive but still a matter of preference versus CLI‑native tools.
