You Should Never Go Viral With Your AI App

February 27, 2026 Victor Stepanov growth, product strategy, AI apps, virality, retention
Read the source article

Quick summary

The article warns that AI apps often break when they go viral, arguing for deliberate, slower growth so teams can stabilize infrastructure, improve retention, and avoid reputational damage.

Key takeaways

  • Viral spikes can overwhelm infrastructure and degrade user experience.
  • Early demand can mask retention and product-market fit problems.
  • Rapid growth attracts misuse and increases moderation burdens.
  • Sustainable growth requires reliability, onboarding, and clear use cases.
  • Teams should design for durability before pursuing distribution.
