This Is How the Every Editorial Team Uses AI

2026-02-23 Kate Lee editorial workflow, AI writing, Spiral, Monologue, Claude Projects, Every.to, AI tools, editorial independence, workflow personalization
Read the source article

Quick summary

Every.to's editorial team integrates AI throughout their content workflow while preserving editorial independence—with each team member developing personalized AI practices rather than following prescriptive rules.

Key takeaways

  • Workflows are personal: writers, editors, and producers each develop individualized AI integration methods rather than following a single prescribed approach.
  • Kate Lee uses Spiral for subjective editorial decisions, requesting "rewrite options" to find language that "sounds like a specific human wrote it"—treating AI as a language palette.
  • Custom skills built to identify pattern-based issues: vague pronouns, unsourced claims, and formulaic constructions typical of AI-generated writing.
  • Managing Editor Eleanor Warnock uses AI for initial manuscript evaluation—assessing structural soundness and alignment with Every's editorial voice before deeper editing.
  • Key tools: Spiral (writing refinement), Monologue (voice drafting), and Claude Projects.
