When AI Becomes the Sales Floor

February 24, 2026 agentic commerce, product imagery, visual catalogs, 3D pipelines, retail distribution
Read the source article

Quick summary

AI shopping agents surface brands with complete visual catalogs, turning image coverage into distribution infrastructure—and Glossi pitches a 3D+AI workflow to generate that coverage at scale.

Key takeaways

  • Agentic commerce systems reward rich, structured product data, especially imagery.
  • Visual coverage across every SKU, colorway, and angle becomes a ranking signal.
  • Traditional photo shoots are too slow for continuously updating AI feeds.
  • 3D “source of truth” models can generate consistent assets at scale.
  • Content infrastructure is positioned as a long‑term competitive moat for brands.
