OpenAI's acquisition of OpenClaw signals the beginning of the end of the ChatGPT era

2026-02-17 Sam Witteveen openai, agents, acquisition
Read the source article

Quick summary

OpenAI’s move to bring OpenClaw’s creator into the company signals a shift from chatbots to autonomous agents that act in the world.

Key takeaways

  • OpenAI is hiring OpenClaw’s creator and sponsoring the project’s foundation.
  • The acquisition reflects a strategic pivot from chat interfaces to agentic systems.
  • OpenClaw’s rapid adoption raised enterprise security concerns.
  • Agents emphasize execution—browsing, clicking, running code—over conversation.
  • The shift pressures enterprises to rethink AI governance and risk models.
