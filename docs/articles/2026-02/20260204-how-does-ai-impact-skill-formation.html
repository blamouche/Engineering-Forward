How Does AI Impact Skill Formation?

2026-02-03 Sean Goedecke AI, skill formation, learning, coding agents, GPT-4o, Anthropic Fellows, software engineering, productivity
Read the source article

Quick summary

A critical analysis of the Anthropic Fellows paper on AI and skill formation finds that the "AI doesn't speed you up" conclusion relies on 50% of participants manually retyping AI code, while the real insight — that AI reduces per-task learning — is obvious but may be offset by higher task volume.

Key takeaways

  • When excluding participants who manually retyped AI code (50% of the cohort), AI users were 25% faster at the task
  • AI users who used AI for questions but wrote their own code performed nearly as well on knowledge quizzes as non-AI users
  • Software engineers are paid to ship, not to learn — avoiding AI to learn more actually makes you worse at your job
  • Moving faster with AI means more opportunities to learn through breadth, potentially offsetting lower per-task depth of learning
  • The study used GPT-4o despite being funded by Anthropic and conducted in 2025, raising questions about whether better models would change results
