How will OpenAI compete?

2026-02-19 Benedict Evans ai, strategy, business
Read the source article

Quick summary

Benedict Evans argues that OpenAI’s lead is fragile because models are commoditizing, engagement is shallow, and the real leverage will come from new experiences that may be built elsewhere.

Key takeaways

  • Frontier model performance is converging across several labs, with no durable lead.
  • OpenAI’s user base is enormous but engagement remains shallow and fragile.
  • Distribution advantages favor incumbents like Google and Meta over pure AI labs.
  • OpenAI’s capex and platform ambitions may secure a seat at the table, not defensible leverage.
  • The next wave of value likely comes from new experiences built on top of models, not from chatbots alone.
