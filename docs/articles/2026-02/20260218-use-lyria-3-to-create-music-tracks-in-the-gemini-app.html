Use Lyria 3 to create music tracks in the Gemini app

Feb 18, 2026 Joël Yawili, Myriam Hamed Torres Gemini app, Lyria 3, music generation, audio AI, creative tools
Read the source article

Quick summary

Google introduces Lyria 3 inside the Gemini app, enabling users to generate music tracks with prompts and iterate on compositions quickly.

Key takeaways

  • Lyria 3 is integrated into the Gemini app to generate music from text prompts.
  • The workflow emphasizes rapid iteration: tweak prompts and regenerate variations.
  • The feature targets creators who need quick drafts, stems, or mood explorations.
  • AI-generated music is positioned as a complement to human creativity, not a replacement.
  • Product rollout highlights accessibility and ease of use within Gemini.
