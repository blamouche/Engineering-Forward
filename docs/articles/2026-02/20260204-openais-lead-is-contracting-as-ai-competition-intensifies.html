New Data: OpenAI's Lead Is Contracting as AI Competition Intensifies

2026-02-03 Alex Kantrowitz, Big Technology OpenAI, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Claude, market share, AI competition, chatbot market
Read the source article

Quick summary

New data shows ChatGPT's mobile market share dropped from 69.1% to 45.3% in one year while Gemini surged to 25.1% and Grok rocketed to 15.2%, signaling a tightening chatbot race — though Claude's engagement metrics tell a different story of depth over breadth.

Key takeaways

  • ChatGPT's daily U.S. mobile app market share fell from 69.1% to 45.3% between January 2025 and January 2026
  • Gemini rose from 14.7% to 25.1% and Grok from 1.6% to 15.2% in the same period
  • Web visits to Gemini grew 647% year-over-year (267.7M to 2B), while ChatGPT grew 50% (3.8B to 5.7B)
  • The chatbot race is not zero-sum: 20% of users now use at least two chatbot apps, up from 5% at end of 2023
  • Claude's average daily session time surged from ~10 minutes in June 2025 to over 30 minutes today, suggesting deeper engagement despite fewer users
