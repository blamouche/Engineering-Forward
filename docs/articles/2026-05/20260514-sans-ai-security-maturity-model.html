Quick summary

The SANS AI Security Maturity Model provides a practical, evidence-based 5-stage framework to help organizations assess their AI security posture, align with global regulations, and progress from ad-hoc adoption to AI-native security maturity.

Key takeaways

  • The model is structured across three pillars—Protect, Utilize, and Govern—providing a comprehensive lens on AI security
  • Five maturity stages with defined controls, metrics, and actions allow organizations to benchmark and advance systematically
  • The framework maps to major global standards including NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, ISO 42001, and OWASP guidance for real-world compliance readiness
  • The model emphasizes evidence-based scoring, moving beyond checklist-style assessments to measurable security outcomes
  • Authored by Chris Cochran (SANS Field CISO), a Marine Corps veteran with deep expertise in threat intelligence and security operations leadership
