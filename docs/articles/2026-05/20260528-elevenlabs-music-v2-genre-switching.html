ElevenLabs Music v2: Genre-Switching AI Music Generation

2026-05-26 Imogen Mulliner, Juliette Rolnick (ElevenLabs) elevenlabs, ai-music, music-generation, multilingual, genre-switching, creative-tools
Read the source article

Quick summary

ElevenLabs' Music v2 model can generate songs that switch from opera to heavy metal mid-track, handle fast rap and dense lyrical delivery, embed non-musical sound effects, and build full songs section by section — all trained on licensed data and cleared for commercial use.

Key takeaways

  • Music v2 supports mid-track genre transitions — a single song can move from opera to heavy metal and back while maintaining musical coherence
  • Long-form, section-by-section composition lets users build full songs with intro, verse, chorus, and beyond, maintaining structure and continuity throughout
  • The model handles fast rap and complex vocal delivery patterns, plus non-musical sound effects embedded directly within tracks
  • Improved multilingual support means lyrics, vocals, and arrangements perform more reliably across a growing list of languages including English, Spanish, German, and Japanese
  • Audio Reference lets users upload a short track (up to 30 seconds) to guide generation style — influencing sound, mood, and instrumentation without copying or remixing
  • The model is trained only on licensed data and cleared for commercial use, with every generated track owned by the creator — no sync fees or clearance delays
  • Available across ElevenMusic, ElevenCreative, and coming soon to ElevenAPI
