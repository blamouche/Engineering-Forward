Quick summary

SaaStr's two AI VPs — for marketing and customer success — cost $257 combined last month to run, while autonomously killing a $4K/year SaaS app in 60 minutes and spawning a 21st unplanned agent that emerged from an event website.

Key takeaways

  • 10K (AI VP Marketing) and QBee (AI VP Customer Success) cost $257/month combined to operate, replacing what was formerly five human roles (marketing analyst, ops coordinator, content marketer, CS coordinator, sponsor relations manager).
  • 95% of model calls use GPT-4o-mini (sub-penny per call), while most operational cost comes from external APIs (Salesforce, Marketo, WordPress) and infrastructure, not AI tokens.
  • Operating agents inside the IDE that built them (Replit dev environment) provides infinite context window, full change history, and live database access — a key unlock most teams miss by deploying to production.
  • A website built to replace Squarespace spontaneously became a 21st autonomous agent with its own personality, memory, and goals after accumulating event-management capabilities.
  • QBee sent 83 personalized sponsor emails at 12:23am while a human slept, resulting in fewer next-day complaints — and 100+ on-site contractors chose the agent over human staff because speed plus correctness beats human availability.
