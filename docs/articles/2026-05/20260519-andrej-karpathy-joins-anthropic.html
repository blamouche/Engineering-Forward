Andrej Karpathy, Tesla Alum and OpenAI Co-Founder, Joins Anthropic

May 19, 2026 Elias Schisgall anthropic, karpathy, openai, tesla, ai-talent, pretraining, llm
Read the source article

Quick summary

Andrej Karpathy, an OpenAI co-founder and former Tesla AI chief, has joined Anthropic to lead a new group focused on using Claude to accelerate pretraining research — marking one of the highest-profile talent moves in the AI industry.

Key takeaways

  • Karpathy joins Anthropic's pretraining team, which oversees the data training process for Claude models
  • He described the next few years at the LLM frontier as "especially formative" and expressed excitement about returning to R&D
  • The move brings together one of AI's most prominent researchers with the company that has been gaining on OpenAI in enterprise adoption
  • Karpathy was a founding member of OpenAI, later led Tesla's Autopilot AI, and most recently has been an independent researcher and educator
  • The hire signals Anthropic's continued investment in fundamental research even as it expands its platform and product offerings
