Quick summary

A practical, battle-tested guide for finance professionals on using Claude Opus 4.7 with XML-structured prompts to dramatically accelerate core financial workflows — from DCF modeling and LBO analysis to cash forecasting and monthly close — cutting hours-long tasks to minutes while producing auditable first drafts.

Key takeaways

  • Claude Opus 4.7 can reduce a 13-week cash forecast from four hours to 40 minutes when configured with the right prompt structure
  • XML-structured instructions outperform conversational prompts for financial outputs, producing tighter, more reliable models
  • The guide covers seven specific prompt templates: DCF, LBO, three-statement model, 13-week cash forecast, unit economics, credit analysis, and monthly close
  • Key failure modes include confident but incorrect calculations — prompting discipline is essential for auditable outputs
  • The playbook integrates with broader AI automation workflows: scheduled deal scoring, autonomous research, and self-generating morning briefings
