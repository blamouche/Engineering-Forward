Jury Rejects Elon Musk's Lawsuit Against OpenAI and Its CEO Sam Altman

May 18, 2026 Unknown (paywalled) Elon Musk, Sam Altman, OpenAI, lawsuit, jury verdict, nonprofit, breach of trust, statute of limitations
Read the source article

Quick summary

A federal jury in Oakland, California rejected Elon Musk's lawsuit against OpenAI and Sam Altman, finding that Musk's claims of betrayal of the company's nonprofit mission were filed too late under the statute of limitations.

Key takeaways

  • The jury found Musk's breach of charitable trust claims were time-barred under California's three-year statute of limitations
  • Musk had sued in 2024, alleging Altman and OpenAI violated a founding promise to keep the AI company a nonprofit
  • The verdict was rendered after a three-week trial featuring testimony from both Altman and Musk
  • The court also dismissed Musk's claims against Microsoft, which had invested in OpenAI and was named as a co-defendant
  • Musk immediately criticized the ruling and indicated he would appeal the decision
