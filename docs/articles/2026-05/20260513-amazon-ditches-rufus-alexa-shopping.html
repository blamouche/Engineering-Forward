Amazon Ditches Rufus AI Chatbot in Favor of Alexa Shopping Agent

2026-05-13 Jennifer Pattison Tuohy (The Verge), CNBC staff Amazon, Alexa, Rufus, shopping agent, Alexa Plus, e-commerce, AI assistant, agentic commerce, Buy for Me, price alerts, Echo Show
Read the source article

Quick summary

Amazon is retiring its Rufus AI shopping chatbot and replacing it with Alexa for Shopping—an Alexa Plus-powered assistant deeply integrated into the Amazon search bar, website, and Echo devices—capable of price tracking, auto-purchasing, cross-website shopping, and cross-device continuity.

Key takeaways

  • Alexa for Shopping replaces Rufus entirely and moves front-and-center into the Amazon search bar, making AI shopping assistance the default rather than an optional feature
  • Capabilities include setting price alerts with automatic purchase triggers, tracking a full year of price history, comparing items, auto-reordering, and agentic shopping on external websites via Buy for Me
  • Cross-device continuity links Echo smart speakers and Show displays with the website and app—a conversation started on an Echo can inform shopping results on Amazon.com
  • The assistant leverages multiple models, pulling from Amazon.com data, web-wide information, and personal customer knowledge to generate "shopping guides" with feature comparisons and AI-generated review summaries
  • Amazon is simultaneously suing to keep external AI agents like Perplexity's Comet off its marketplace, revealing the strategic tension between building its own agentic commerce while blocking competitors
