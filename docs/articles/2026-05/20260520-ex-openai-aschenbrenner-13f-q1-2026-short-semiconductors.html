Ex-OpenAI's Aschenbrenner Bet $5.5B on AI Chips. Now He's Betting $8.5B Against Them

May 20, 2026 Linas Beliūnas ai, semiconductors, hedge-fund, aschenbrenner, investing, nvidia, shorts, 13f
Read the source article

Quick summary

Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness fund expanded 148% to $13.7B in Q1 2026 — but the headline is that the book flipped from predominantly long to 62% short, with massive single-name puts on every Tier-1 semiconductor company including NVDA, TSM, ASML, and AMD.

Key takeaways

  • Gross notional jumped from $5.52B to $13.68B in 90 days, a 148% expansion, with disclosed positions growing from 29 to 37
  • The fund went net short for the first time: 38.1% long, 61.9% short, with the largest position being SMH semiconductor ETF puts at 14.94%
  • The short side targets every major semiconductor name: NVDA, AVGO, TSM, ASML, INTC, plus ORCL puts
  • Despite the dramatic portfolio shift, YTD return on disclosed positions is +39.45% vs SPY's +8.40%
  • The original thesis — "buy the bottlenecks of AGI buildout, short what AI eats" — is unchanged; only the expression has shifted
