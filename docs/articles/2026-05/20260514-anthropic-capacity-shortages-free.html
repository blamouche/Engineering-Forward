The Pulse: Did capacity shortages turn Anthropic hostile to devs? (Free Edition)

2026-05-14 Gergely Orosz Anthropic, capacity shortages, Claude Code, SpaceX, xAI, Grok, AI competition, compute infrastructure
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic's pattern of degrading developer experience was likely driven by hidden compute capacity shortages, now addressed through a major data center deal with SpaceX/xAI's Colossus 1 facility.

Key takeaways

  • Anthropic's "dumber" model, Claude Code nerfing, and account bans correlate with undisclosed capacity constraints, not malice
  • Anthropic experienced 80x revenue and usage growth versus a planned 10x, overwhelming infrastructure
  • SpaceX/xAI is renting its entire Colossus 1 data center (220,000+ NVIDIA GPUs, ~45% of xAI capacity) to Anthropic
  • Following the deal, Anthropic immediately doubled Claude Code limits, removed peak-hour restrictions, and raised API rate limits
  • Musk's deal with Anthropic may be strategic: weakening OpenAI (his litigation target) by strengthening a rival
