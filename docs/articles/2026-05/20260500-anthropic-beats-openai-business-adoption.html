Anthropic beats OpenAI on business adoption

May 2026 Ara Kharazian, Lead Economist, Ramp AI adoption, Anthropic, OpenAI, business spend, AI Index, competition, token costs, Claude
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic has surpassed OpenAI in business adoption for the first time (34.4% vs 32.3%), but faces headwinds from misaligned incentives, declining model quality, and rising token costs that could reverse its lead.

Key takeaways

  • Anthropic reached 34.4% business adoption in April 2026, overtaking OpenAI at 32.3%, after quadrupling its share over the past year while OpenAI grew only 0.3%
  • Anthropic's incentives are misaligned — driving users to expensive models when cheaper ones suffice; Uber already blew through its 2026 AI budget
  • Users report Claude getting worse with frequent outages, rate limits, and declining satisfaction, though Anthropic has reset usage limits and secured compute via a SpaceX deal
  • Anthropic's latest model update 3x token costs for prompts containing images, raising questions about its product roadmap priorities
  • Fastest-growing vendors on Ramp are AI inference platforms providing cheap, open-source models — suggesting cost sensitivity may drive the next competitive shift
