Meta's AI Chief Alex Wang on Muse Spark and the AI Wars

2026-05-13 Ashlee Vance (Core Memory podcast) Meta, Alex Wang, Alexandr Wang, Muse Spark, Scale AI, Mark Zuckerberg, AI competition, Meta Superintelligence Labs, OpenAI, Anthropic, closed-source, AI recruiting
Read the source article

Quick summary

In his first public interview since joining Meta in a $14 billion deal, former Scale AI CEO Alex Wang discusses leading Meta's AI turnaround, the launch of the Muse Spark model, personal tensions with Sam Altman, and Meta's controversial pivot from open-source to closed-source frontier models.

Key takeaways

  • Meta acquired Alex Wang from Scale AI in a deal valued at $14 billion in June 2025, with Mark Zuckerberg positioning him as the "rescue plan" for Meta's fading position in the AI race
  • Wang's first major deliverable, Muse Spark, launched in April 2026 as a ground-up overhaul—a closed-source model built from scratch in nine months by the new Meta Superintelligence Labs unit
  • The podcast reveals Wang's personal "beef" with Sam Altman, extraordinary compensation packages Meta has been handing out to AI talent, and Zuckerberg personally delivering soup to AI recruits
  • Muse Spark marks a strategic departure from Meta's traditional open-source Llama approach—it's purpose-built for Meta's products (Instagram, Facebook, Threads) rather than released as open weights
  • The Meta AI hierarchy now includes "all-stars" like Nat Friedman, Daniel Gross, and Shengjia Zhao, assembled under Wang's leadership at the newly formed lab
