Anthropic Beats OpenAI on Business Adoption

May 13, 2026 Ara Kharazian (Lead Economist, Ramp) AI market share, Anthropic vs OpenAI, business AI adoption, Ramp AI Index, Claude, token economy, AI inference
Read the source article

Quick summary

Ramp's AI Index shows Anthropic has overtaken OpenAI in business adoption for the first time (34.4% vs 32.3%), but the lead is fragile amid rising costs, performance complaints, and the emergence of cheaper alternatives.

Key takeaways

  • Anthropic's business adoption reached 34.4% in April 2026, surpassing OpenAI's 32.3% — a 4x increase for Anthropic over the past year versus near-flat growth for OpenAI.
  • Overall AI adoption among businesses crossed 50.6%, up only 0.2 percentage points month-over-month, suggesting the market is maturing.
  • Three headwinds for Anthropic: incentive misalignment (token-based pricing encourages expensive model usage), declining Claude performance/outages, and a model update that 3x token costs for image prompts.
  • Cheaper alternatives are gaining traction: AI inference platforms giving access to open-source models were among the fastest-growing vendors on Ramp's platform.
  • The cost to switch between models is minimal, making vendor stickiness unusually weak in the AI model market — a dynamic unprecedented in software history.
