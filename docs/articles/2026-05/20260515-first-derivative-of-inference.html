The First Derivative of Inference

May 15, 2026 Tomasz Tunguz AI inference, SaaS, power law dynamics, Datadog, Twilio, Anthropic, Google Cloud, AI economics, Saaspocalypse, venture capital
Read the source article

Quick summary

The fastest-growing companies in tech today are either selling AI inference directly or are "first derivatives" of it — companies whose revenue grows as their customers buy more inference, and for pre-AI companies, finding this derivative relationship may be the only escape from the "Saaspocalypse."

Key takeaways

  • The AI inference market is projected to reach $250 billion within seven years, surpassing the database market and becoming the largest technology market
  • Companies selling inference directly are exploding: Anthropic booked $9-10B in consecutive months, Google Cloud is growing 63% at an $80B run rate
  • Datadog and Twilio exemplify "first derivative" companies — they don't sell inference but their revenue correlates directly with AI adoption growth
  • Power law dynamics dominate: at Datadog, 20% of customers using AI integrations represent 80% of ARR, showing extreme concentration
  • The board-level strategic question for every pre-AI company: "How do we resell inference or benefit from our customers buying huge volumes of it?"
