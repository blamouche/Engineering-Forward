Alibaba Unveils the Zhenwu M890 as China's NVIDIA Alternative Push Hardens

2026-05-20 Reuters / Allison Steffens Herrera (TNW) Alibaba, T-Head, Zhenwu M890, AI chip, GPU, NVIDIA alternative, China, export controls, semiconductor
Read the source article

Quick summary

Alibaba's T-Head chip unit has unveiled the Zhenwu M890, its highest-spec GPU-class AI chip to date, positioned as a domestic alternative to NVIDIA's H100 — with the company claiming scaled mass production and planning a T-Head IPO, all while US export controls and the Trump-Xi summit on AI chips create a procurement vacuum that Chinese customers are rapidly filling with domestic chips.

Key takeaways

  • The Zhenwu M890 is positioned against NVIDIA's H100 generation (not the newer Blackwell), with a meaningful but closing performance gap
  • The chip is manufactured at process nodes Chinese foundries can produce without US-controlled lithography equipment — the binding constraint on China's chip cycle
  • T-Head claims "scaled mass production," an unusual operational disclosure that signals supply-chain confidence
  • T-Head is planning an IPO to fund more aggressive infrastructure investment, putting it in direct competition with Huawei Ascend and Cambricon
  • Chinese hyperscalers are ramping domestic chip purchases even as some H200 chips have been cleared for sale to select Chinese customers — they want optionality on both sides
