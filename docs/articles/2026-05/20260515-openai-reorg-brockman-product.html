Greg Brockman Officially Takes Control of OpenAI's Products in Latest Shake-Up

2026-05-15 Maxwell Zeff OpenAI, Greg Brockman, reorganization, ChatGPT, Codex, super app, executive changes, Fidji Simo, IPO, product strategy
Read the source article

Quick summary

OpenAI is reorganizing its executive ranks, making co-founder Greg Brockman the permanent head of product strategy and merging ChatGPT and Codex into one unified experience, as the company streamlines ahead of its planned IPO amid mounting competition from Anthropic and Google.

Key takeaways

  • Greg Brockman, previously interim product head during Fidji Simo's medical leave, now officially leads product strategy while also overseeing AI infrastructure, with a stated goal of executing "with maximum focus toward the agentic future"
  • ChatGPT and Codex are being merged into a single product experience under one core team, reflecting their growing convergence as Codex increasingly powers both consumer and enterprise offerings
  • Codex head Thibault Sottiaux has been promoted to lead core product and platform teams; ChatGPT's longtime leader Nick Turley moves to enterprise products only, and former Instagram VP Ashley Alexander now leads consumer products
  • The reorganization follows last month's departure of several senior executives including Kevin Weil (AI workspace for scientists), Bill Peebles (Sora), and Srinivas Narayanan (CTO of enterprise applications)
  • These changes come as OpenAI faces mounting competitive pressure — Anthropic in coding, Google in consumer chatbots — and prepares to file for an IPO later this year, with ChatGPT now at over 900 million weekly active users
