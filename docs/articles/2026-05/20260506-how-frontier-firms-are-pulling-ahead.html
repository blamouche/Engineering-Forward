How Frontier Firms Are Pulling Ahead

May 6, 2026 OpenAI enterprise AI, AI adoption, frontier firms, agentic workflows, Codex, B2B signals, AI depth
Read the source article

Quick summary

OpenAI's B2B Signals report reveals that frontier firms now use 3.5x more intelligence per worker than typical firms, with the gap driven by deeper, more complex AI use and agentic workflows rather than simple message volume.

Key takeaways

  • Frontier firms (95th percentile of usage) consume 3.5x more intelligence per worker than typical firms, up from 2x a year ago — the gap is compounding.
  • Message volume explains only 36% of the frontier advantage; most of the gap comes from deeper, more substantive AI interactions.
  • Agentic workflows are the new maturity marker: frontier firms send 16x more Codex messages per worker, with similar patterns for ChatGPT Agent, Deep Research, and GPTs.
  • AI use is broadening across functions but increasingly specialized: IT/Security favor how-to guidance, developers lean on coding, Finance uses AI for analysis.
  • Leading firms don't just deploy AI — they measure depth, build production governance, invest in enablement, and move from chat to delegated agent-based workflows.
