AI's Public Relations Emergency

May 22, 2026 Alex Kantrowitz AI public perception, commencement protests, data center opposition, techlash, job displacement, AI messaging, Gallup polling
Read the source article

Quick summary

Alex Kantrowitz warns that AI faces a public relations emergency as college graduates boo AI mentions at commencements, seven in ten Americans oppose data centers in their area, and the industry's own leaders have spent years warning about mass job loss — all while marching toward trillion-dollar IPOs.

Key takeaways

  • College graduates across multiple US universities have booed or jeered mentions of AI at commencement ceremonies
  • AI leaders have spent years publicly warning about mass job loss, focusing on entry-level roles — while their companies head toward IPOs
  • Marc Andreessen told Joe Rogan's audience AI is great because "bots never get frustrated with you" and "never file HR complaints" — messaging unlikely to land with unemployed grads
  • Gallup: 70% of Americans oppose AI data centers in their area, with 48% strongly opposed and only 7% strongly in favor
  • Unlike the 2018 "techlash" against Amazon/Facebook/Google, AI polls below all major political candidates, lowering the political cost of restrictive action
  • A Maine data center moratorium was only stopped by a governor's veto; 2028 presidential races could feature data center bans
