How to Use Codex for Knowledge Work: A Power User's Guide

May 26, 2026 Katie Parrott Codex, OpenAI, knowledge work, AI agents, workflows, productivity, Claude Code, Dan Shipper
Read the source article

Quick summary

A practical introduction to using OpenAI's Codex as an operating system for knowledge work, covering workspace setup, five levels of use, and 13 workflow templates for email, research, writing, planning, and reporting.

Key takeaways

  • Codex functions as a collaborative workspace where humans and AI agents work side-by-side across email, documents, data sources, and connected tools.
  • The core loop is: Connect, contextualize, delegate or collaborate, review, and compound — progressively turning repeated processes into reusable workflows.
  • Five levels of Codex use progress from one-off tasks through multi-source workflows, recurring chores, small tools, to compounding systems.
  • 13 workflow templates are provided: inbox review queues, unanswered message sweeps, research briefs, weekly reports, GTM plans, customer support routing, recruiting research, and planning agents.
  • Dan Shipper has become an evangelist for Codex, calling it his "daily driver" and achieving inbox zero for 10 days straight — an unusual feat for him.
