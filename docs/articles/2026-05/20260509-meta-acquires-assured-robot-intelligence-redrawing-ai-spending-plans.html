Meta Acquires Assured Robot Intelligence, Redrawing AI Spending Plans

May 9, 2026 Emily Thompson Meta, robotics, humanoid AI, capex, acquisition, AI spending
Read the source article

Quick summary

Meta's May 2026 acquisition of humanoid robotics startup Assured Robot Intelligence, paired with a $10 billion capex increase to $125-145 billion, signals a concrete pivot from metaverse optics toward physical AI systems.

Key takeaways

  • Meta acquired San Diego-based Assured Robot Intelligence on May 1, 2026, folding its team into internal robotics AI model optimization
  • The acquisition was immediately followed by Meta raising its 2026 capital spending forecast by $10 billion to $125-145 billion, explicitly citing additional AI data-center costs
  • The move represents a tangible funding shift from AR/metaverse projects toward robotics and core AI infrastructure
  • Founders framed the deal as team integration rather than shutdown, with the startup's co-founder publicly expressing excitement about joining Meta's humanoid intelligence efforts
  • The small acquisition size suggests a talent and model play rather than a massive product acquisition, but signals strategic commitment to the physical AI domain
  • For investors and engineers, the key question is whether this accelerates real-world robot capabilities or simply centralizes talent and model IP within a tech giant
