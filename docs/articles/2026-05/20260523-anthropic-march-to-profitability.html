Anthropic's March to Profitability

May 23, 2026 Contrary Research Anthropic, profitability, revenue growth, IPO, Claude Code, enterprise AI, AI economics, quantum computing, South Korea, prediction markets
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic projects $10.9B in Q2 revenue with its first profitable quarter ($559M profit), driven by enterprise adoption of Claude's coding tools, as the company races toward an October IPO with quarterly revenue growth faster than Zoom's during the pandemic.

Key takeaways

  • Anthropic Q2 revenue projection: $10.9B, more than double Q1's $4.8B, with $559M in profit — the company's first profitable quarter.
  • Compute costs dropped from 71 cents per dollar of revenue in Q1 to 56 cents in Q2, showing improving unit economics at scale.
  • Claude Code alone generates over $2.5B, making enterprise coding tools the primary growth driver.
  • Anthropic warned investors against unauthorized SPV stock sales, calling them "void and will not be recognized."
