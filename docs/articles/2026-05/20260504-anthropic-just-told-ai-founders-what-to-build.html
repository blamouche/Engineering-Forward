Anthropic Just Told AI Founders Exactly What to Build in 2026

2026-05-04 Linas Beliūnas Anthropic, AI startups, consumer AI, product-market fit, Claude Marketplace, personal guidance, vertical AI, founder playbook, VC funding
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic's study of 1 million Claude conversations revealing 6% seeking personal guidance doubles as a product roadmap for AI founders, identifying nine consumer domains where Anthropic is staying out while building enterprise verticals—creating a clear line between platform risk and open opportunity.

Key takeaways

  • Anthropic analyzed 1 million Claude conversations and found ~6% involved personal guidance across health, careers, relationships, money, parenting, legal rights, and more—representing tens of millions of users using AI in lieu of professionals they can't access or afford.
  • Cal AI, built by two high school students targeting just one subtopic (calorie tracking), grew to $50M+ annualized revenue with 7 employees and $0 venture funding, proving the data converts to venture-scale outcomes.
  • Anthropic's job postings reveal it's building vertical AI products in healthcare, financial services, legal, and life sciences—all enterprise-focused, leaving the consumer layer wide open for startups.
  • The study cross-references the guidance study with Anthropic's careers page to produce a domain-by-domain analysis across all nine verticals, including funded competitors, willingness to pay, and specific founder playbooks.
  • VCs have stopped funding thin wrappers, but the Claude Marketplace offers a distribution channel for startups that build real depth in specific domains.
