Pope approves creation of Interdicasterial Commission on Artificial Intelligence

May 16, 2026 Isabella H. de Carvalho Vatican, Pope Leo XIV, Artificial Intelligence, Catholic Church, AI ethics, Dicastery, human dignity, Praedicate Evangelium
Read the source article

Quick summary

Pope Leo XIV has approved the creation of a Vatican Interdicasterial Commission on Artificial Intelligence, coordinated by the Dicastery for Promoting Integral Human Development, to address AI's effects on human dignity and societal development.

Key takeaways

  • The Commission involves representatives from seven Vatican bodies including Doctrine of the Faith, Culture and Education, and three Pontifical Academies
  • Coordination rotates among participating Dicasteries on one-year renewable terms, with the Pope deciding the next coordinating body
  • The move follows the 2025 document "Antiqua et Nova" on the relationship between artificial and human intelligence
  • Pope Leo XIV has made AI a central theme of his pontificate, drawing parallels to Pope Leo XIII's response to the Industrial Revolution
  • The Commission's mandate includes facilitating collaboration on AI activities, projects, and policies within the Holy See
