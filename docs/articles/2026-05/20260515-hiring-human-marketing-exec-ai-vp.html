We're Hiring a Human Marketing Exec to Report to 10K, Our AI VP Marketing. Could You Report to an AI?

2026-05-08 Jason Lemkin AI management, human-AI collaboration, demand generation, email marketing, AI VP, organizational design, agentic marketing, hiring
Read the source article

Quick summary

SaaStr is hiring a senior demand gen marketing executive to report directly to 10K, their AI VP of Marketing, marking what may be the first job posting where a human reports to an AI manager — and arguing that the bottleneck in modern marketing isn't ideas anymore, it's taste, judgment, and hands-on execution.

Key takeaways

  • 10K already runs SaaStr's marketing operations autonomously: building daily briefs, recommending campaigns with rationale, shipping fully customized 1:1 email campaigns to 400K+ recipients, and running post-mortems.
  • The role explicitly requires reporting to an AI VP: "No ego about org chart. If 'I report to a bot' sounds insulting rather than interesting, this isn't the role."
  • The human's job is threefold: connect the dots into winning plays, bring taste and judgment to review every campaign before it ships, and handle hands-on relationship work (sponsors, partners, key accounts) that AI cannot do.
  • 10K operates 14,230 lines of code on Replit, coordinates with a fleet of other agents (Artisan SDRs, Qualified BDR, Agentforce, Momentum), and performs 1:1 personalization at scale that no human marketer can match.
  • The career thesis: the first people who learn to plan and ship with AI in production at scale will be running demand gen at the best companies in five years.
