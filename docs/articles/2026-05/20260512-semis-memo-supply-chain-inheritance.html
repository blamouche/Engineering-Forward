Semis Memo: Supply Chain Inheritance

May 12, 2026 Citrini (Zephyr and Jukan, semis analysts) semiconductors, analog, power semis, MLCC, capacitors, AI infrastructure, supply chain, EVs, neoclouds, CPUs, inference, Korea, materials bottlenecks
Read the source article

Quick summary

Citrini Research's Semis Memo argues that AI data center buildout is "inheriting" the electric vehicle supply chain, with analog and power semiconductor manufacturers choosing to raise prices rather than add capacity — creating a compelling investment setup as AI demand overwhelms non-AI headwinds.

Key takeaways

  • AI data center demand is now the primary driver for analog and power semiconductors (MOSFETs, diodes, capacitors, inductors), which were previously dragged down by EV/automotive weakness and Chinese competition
  • Manufacturers like Texas Instruments and NXP are choosing to raise ASPs rather than expand capacity, a pattern Citrini calls "Post-Traumatic Supply Disorder" — they've been burned too many times by supply gluts
  • Nvidia itself credited "the electric vehicle and solar industries" as the foundation for its 800V DC rack architecture, explicitly linking the EV supply chain to AI infrastructure
  • The MLCC (Multilayer Ceramic Capacitors) shortage is already driving performance in names like Murata, Vishay, and Samsung Electro-Mechanics
  • The memo also covers CPUs in the agentic era, an inference shortage at neoclouds, AI materials bottlenecks, and the Korea semiconductor opportunity
