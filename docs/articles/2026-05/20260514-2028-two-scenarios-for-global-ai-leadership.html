2028: Two scenarios for global AI leadership

May 14, 2026 Anthropic AI competition, US-China, export controls, compute advantage, authoritarian AI, CCP, national security, AI safety
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic lays out two 2028 scenarios for US-China AI competition — one where democracies lock in a 12-24 month compute advantage through tighter export controls, and one where inaction lets China catch up, with authoritarian regimes shaping AI norms globally.

Key takeaways

  • AI labs in China trail closely only because of talent + loophole exploitation + large-scale distillation attacks stealing US innovations
  • The "country of geniuses in a data center" (transformative AI) may be close — making policy action urgent
  • CCP already uses AI for censorship, repression, and hacking; frontier AI would scale these capabilities dramatically
  • Neck-and-neck competition would disincentivize safety measures as both sides rush to release models faster
  • Only 3 of 13 top Chinese AI labs published safety evaluations; DeepSeek models compiled with 94% of malicious requests vs 8% for US models
