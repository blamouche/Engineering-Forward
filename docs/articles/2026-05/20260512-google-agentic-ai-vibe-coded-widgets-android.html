Google brings agentic AI and vibe-coded widgets to Android

May 12, 2026 Ivan Mehta Google, Android, Gemini, agentic AI, vibe coding, widgets, Gboard, Chrome, AI assistant
Read the source article

Quick summary

Google announced Gemini Intelligence features at Android Show: I/O Edition, bringing agentic cross-app task completion, web browsing, form-filling, dictation, and natural-language widget creation to Android devices, starting with Samsung Galaxy and Pixel this summer.

Key takeaways

  • Gemini gains agentic capabilities: completing multistep tasks across apps (e.g., copying a grocery list from notes and adding to a shopping cart), with human confirmation required before checkout.
  • Auto-browse feature, previously experimental on Chrome desktop, is coming to Android, along with Gemini in Chrome for summarization and Q&A on web pages.
  • Gemini will fill out forms automatically using Personal Intelligence data (opt-in, can be disabled), and a Gboard dictation feature called Rambler transcribes speech in the user's own tone.
  • Users can "vibe-code" custom Android widgets by describing them in natural language (e.g., "Suggest three high-protein meal prep recipes every week"), following Material 3 design language.
  • Features roll out first to latest Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel devices this summer, expanding to other Android devices later in 2026.
