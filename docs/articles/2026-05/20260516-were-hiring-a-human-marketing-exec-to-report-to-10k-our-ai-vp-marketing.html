We're Hiring a Human Marketing Exec to Report to 10K, Our AI VP Marketing. Could You Report to an AI?

May 13, 2026 Jason Lemkin AI VP, human-AI collaboration, marketing jobs, demand generation, AI agents, management structure, AI SDR, Replit, 10K
Read the source article

Quick summary

SaaStr is hiring a human marketing executive to report directly to their AI VP of Marketing (10K), signaling a new organizational model where humans bring taste, judgment, and relationship skills while AI handles campaign execution, data analysis, and 1:1 personalization at scale.

Key takeaways

  • 10K, SaaStr's AI VP Marketing, now ships fully autonomous campaigns end-to-end with 1:1 customization for 400,000+ recipients
  • The bottleneck is no longer great ideas — 10K generates more campaign concepts than can be executed — but human taste, judgment, and hands-on review
  • The role demands someone who treats AI as a partner, not a tool: daily planning with 10K, reviewing every proposed campaign before shipping
  • 10K's self-assessment acknowledges it "can't do strategy, hire people, handle cross-functional politics, or crisis response" — defining the human half
  • Five years from now, every demand gen leader will work this way; early adopters will have an insurmountable career advantage
