DeepMind spinout Isomorphic Labs raises $2.1bn

May 12, 2026 Freya Pratty AI drug discovery, Isomorphic Labs, DeepMind, AlphaFold, Series B, Thrive Capital, UK Sovereign AI fund, biotech, fundraising, GV, MGX, Temasek, Eli Lilly, Novartis
Read the source article

Quick summary

Isomorphic Labs, the DeepMind spinout using AlphaFold for AI-designed drugs, raised a $2.1bn Series B led by Thrive Capital, backed by UK's Sovereign AI fund and sovereign wealth funds, marking a massive escalation in AI-biotech investment just one year after its $600m first raise.

Key takeaways

  • Isomorphic Labs secured a $2.1bn Series B led by Thrive Capital, with participation from UK's Sovereign AI fund, Abu Dhabi's MGX, GV (Alphabet), and Singapore's Temasek
  • The raise comes just one year after its $600m first external capital round in May 2025
  • Isomorphic was spun out of Google DeepMind in 2021, founded by Colin Murdoch and Demis Hassabis
  • The company uses DeepMind's AlphaFold protein-structure prediction technology to build a drug discovery platform
  • It competes with OpenAI (which released a life sciences model in 2026) and has signed pharma deals with Eli Lilly and Novartis; pre-clinical trials have begun
