Gemini 3.5: frontier intelligence with action

May 19, 2026 Koray Kavukcuoglu, Jeff Dean, Oriol Vinyals, Noam Shazeer Gemini 3.5, Google DeepMind, agentic AI, Flash, Antigravity, coding, multimodal
Read the source article

Quick summary

Google DeepMind unveils Gemini 3.5 Flash, a frontier model that combines top-tier reasoning with exceptional speed, purpose-built for long-horizon agentic workflows and coding at scale.

Key takeaways

  • Gemini 3.5 Flash matches flagship-level intelligence while being 4x faster in output tokens per second than other frontier models, landing in the top-right quadrant of the Artificial Analysis index.
  • The model excels at agentic and coding benchmarks: Terminal-Bench 2.1 (76.2%), MCP Atlas (83.6%), and multimodal understanding via CharXiv Reasoning (84.2%).
  • Paired with Google's Antigravity harness, 3.5 Flash can deploy collaborative subagents for multi-step workflows like codebase migrations, game development, and financial document preparation.
  • Enterprise partners (Shopify, Macquarie Bank, Salesforce, Ramp, Xero, Databricks) are already using it for production workloads ranging from customer onboarding to autonomous tax form processing.
  • 3.5 Flash powers the new Gemini Spark personal AI agent and AI Mode in Search, while 3.5 Pro is in internal use and rolling out next month.
