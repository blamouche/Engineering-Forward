Meta to Release Muse Spark in Voice Mode and Meta Glasses

May 12, 2026 Erin (TestingCatalog) Meta, Muse Spark, voice mode, Meta Glasses, Ray-Ban Meta, multimodal AI, Meta AI, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, shopping mode, real-time visual recognition
Read the source article

Quick summary

Meta rolled out its Muse Spark model across its entire app ecosystem with voice conversations, live camera recognition, and smart shopping features, while expanding integration into Ray-Ban Meta and Oakley Meta glasses, positioning the model as a real-world contextual AI assistant.

Key takeaways

  • Muse Spark now powers Meta AI across WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger, Threads, the Meta AI app, and AI glasses
  • Voice conversations support natural speech with mid-conversation topic and language switching, plus on-the-fly image generation and recommendations
  • Shopping mode aggregates Facebook Marketplace and internet-wide listings with map-based browsing, price/style filters, and direct brand content in a grid layout
  • Live AI camera features allow users to point their device at objects or landmarks for immediate contextual recognition and assistance
  • The model is designed as a compact, fast architecture capable of advanced reasoning in science, math, and health, with subagent-based multitasking
