Introducing Googlebook, designed for Gemini Intelligence

May 12, 2026 Alex Kuscher, Senior Director, Laptops & Tablets Googlebook, Gemini, Android, ChromeOS, AI laptop, Magic Pointer, widgets
Read the source article

Quick summary

Google unveils Googlebook, a new category of AI-first laptops merging Android and ChromeOS with Gemini Intelligence at its core, featuring a context-aware "Magic Pointer" and seamless phone integration.

Key takeaways

  • Googlebook is a new laptop category built from the ground up for Gemini Intelligence, merging the best of Android (apps, modern OS) and ChromeOS (world's most popular browser).
  • The Magic Pointer, developed with Google DeepMind, brings contextual AI suggestions directly to the cursor — wiggle it and Gemini offers relevant actions based on what you're pointing at.
  • "Create your Widget" lets users build custom desktop dashboards by prompting Gemini, which can pull data from Gmail, Calendar, and the web into a single personalized view.
  • Deep Android integration allows running phone apps directly on the laptop and accessing phone files instantly via "Quick Access" — no transfers needed.
  • Premium hardware from Acer, ASUS, Dell, HP, and Lenovo, distinguished by a unique "glowbar" design element; devices launch in fall 2026.
