Europe's Hot AI Startups Set to Poach More Talent from Big Tech

May 12, 2026 Anne Sraders AI talent, Big Tech brain drain, Cusp AI, Apple, John Giannandrea, AMI Labs, Multiverse, Lovable, European startups, recruitment
Read the source article

Quick summary

A growing wave of top AI talent is migrating from Big Tech to European startups — epitomized by Apple's former AI chief John Giannandrea joining materials discovery startup Cusp AI — as startups offer faster innovation cycles, meaningful equity, and the chance to build rather than maintain.

Key takeaways

  • Apple's former AI chief John Giannandrea joined UK-based Cusp AI, a materials discovery startup, in a landmark hire for European tech
  • Other notable moves include Multiverse poaching Microsoft's "Knight" Distinguished Engineer from Meta, AMI Labs hiring a former Google DeepMind VP, and Lovable recruiting from Spotify
  • Startups like Cusp AI (valued at ~$113M after a $262M round), Wayve, and Synthesia are offering £150K-200K base salaries plus equity that can become "life-changing" at exit
  • Recruiters report Big Tech talent is increasingly frustrated with bureaucracy and maintenance work — they want to build new things
  • The trend signals a potential decentralization of AI innovation, with Europe emerging as a hub for specialized AI applications
