Beyond a Sleek Interface: The New Wave of European Fintech

May 21, 2026 Lara Bryant fintech, embedded finance, SME banking, Wamo, neobanks, AI in banking, financial operating system, European startups
Read the source article

Quick summary

European fintech is entering a new phase where the goal is no longer a sleeker banking app — it's building an integrated financial operating system that connects cash management, payables, receivables, and credit for small businesses.

Key takeaways

  • European fintech attracted €13B in 2025 and €4.3B in 2026 year-to-date, putting the sector on track for roughly €11.7B by year-end.
  • The first wave (neobanks like Monzo and Revolut) proved that consumers wanted to manage money on smartphones; the new wave targets SMBs with connected financial operating systems.
  • SMBs typically juggle 3-4 bank accounts and 10+ disconnected tools; the fragmentation, not UI, is the core problem.
  • AI should be the engine running the platform — handling compliance, underwriting, and risk assessment — not just a customer service chatbot.
  • Wamo CEO Deniz Guven predicts a surge in partnerships between traditional banks (who can't afford €100M rebuilds) and fintechs with modern infrastructure.
