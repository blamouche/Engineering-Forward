Inside the Biggest Bet in Corporate History

May 20, 2026 Byron Deeter, Sam Bondy (Bessemer Venture Partners) hyperscalers, AI capex, cloud computing, AI investment, software market, infrastructure, trillion-dollar companies, venture capital, economic value
Read the source article

Quick summary

Bessemer Venture Partners analyzes the $8T cumulative AI capex bet by hyperscalers (AMZN, MSFT, GOOGL, META, ORCL), concluding it implies a $6T software revenue opportunity — the largest greenfield money-making event in corporate history — with winners more distributed across the stack than in the previous cloud wave.

Key takeaways

  • Hyperscaler AI capex will cross $1T/year by 2027, totaling ~$8T cumulatively from 2025-2031, requiring $3T in incremental annual revenue to justify the investment at 15% ROIC.
  • The math implies $6T in customer-facing global software revenue — 4x+ growth from today's ~$1.4T — targeting the $40T+ annual knowledge worker economy (legal, accounting, consulting, healthcare, etc.).
  • At a 6x revenue multiple, this represents ~$36T of new market cap, or ~14% of the entire global corporate ecosystem.
  • Unlike the cloud wave where hyperscalers captured outsized value, this wave's winners will be more distributed: hardware vendors first, then hyperscalers, foundation model companies, and finally AI-native applications.
  • The "picks-and-shovels" AI infrastructure stack (orchestration, evals, observability, fine-tuning, agent runtime, etc.) mirrors the cloud-era infrastructure companies and is still in early innings.
