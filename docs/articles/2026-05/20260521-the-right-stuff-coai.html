Quick summary

CO/AI's Harry DeMott frames Andrej Karpathy's move to Anthropic as the completion of a "Mercury Seven" — seven top CTOs who left leadership roles at well-funded companies for individual-contributor research at Anthropic — and argues Karpathy's mandate to use Claude to accelerate pre-training research is the AGI thesis stated plainly: the model itself becoming the binding input to its own improvement.

Key takeaways

  • Karpathy is the seventh signing in a 14-month migration of CTOs from Adept AI, Super.com, Box, Instagram, You.com, and Workday into IC research roles at Anthropic — a "Mercury Seven" for AI.
  • Karpathy's stated mandate is "using Claude to accelerate pre-training research itself" — recursive self-improvement as the core AGI strategy.
  • The essay frames Karpathy's career as five frontiers in eleven years: OpenAI founding (2015), Tesla Autopilot (2017), GPT-3/ChatGPT era (2022), AI education via Eureka Labs (2024), and now recursive improvement at Anthropic (2026).
  • Karpathy's auto-research work already demonstrated AI agents finding 20 things human review missed — applied to pre-training, this is a qualitatively different research program.
  • Google, OpenAI, and Meta were all options — Karpathy chose Anthropic specifically, suggesting the recursive self-improvement thesis is most credible there.
