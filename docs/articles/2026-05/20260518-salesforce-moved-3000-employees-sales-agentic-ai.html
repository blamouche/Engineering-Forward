Salesforce moved 3,000 employees into sales roles as agentic AI reshapes operations: Vala Afshar

May 18, 2026 Moneycontrol (PTI / CNBC-TV18 interview with Vala Afshar) Salesforce, agentic AI, workforce transformation, reskilling, digital labour, AI augmentation, Vala Afshar, Agentforce, hybrid workforce
Read the source article

Quick summary

Salesforce has internally redeployed 3,000 employees from support and other functions into sales roles after its Agentforce AI platform automated their previous work — a real-world case study in how agentic AI is not displacing humans but fundamentally restructuring the workforce toward higher-value functions.

Key takeaways

  • Salesforce moved 3,000 employees into sales roles through internal reskilling as its Agentforce AI platform handled over 2.6 million customer conversations and resolved 3.5 million support cases in 16 months
  • The company's AI agents achieved a 63% resolution rate with customer satisfaction scores matching human agents, freeing up support engineers for higher-value roles
  • Vala Afshar, Salesforce's Chief Digital Evangelist, argues that agentic AI's impact could be "10 times bigger than the Industrial Revolution in one-tenth of the time"
  • Salesforce employs a formal "4Rs" framework — Redesign, Reskill, Redeploy, Rebalance — to manage the transition to an agentic workforce, and has 25,000+ customers using Agentforce globally
  • Afshar warns of "digital Darwinism": companies that fail to adopt AI-driven systems face irrelevance, calling it a binary choice between being "an agentic business or a dying one"
