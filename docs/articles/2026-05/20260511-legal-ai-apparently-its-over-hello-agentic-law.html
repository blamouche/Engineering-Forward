Legal AI? Apparently, it's over. Hello, 'Agentic Law'

May 11, 2026 John Thornhill legal AI, agentic law, Legora, Anthropic, legal tech, M&A, specialist AI, foundation models
Read the source article

Quick summary

As Swedish legal AI startup Legora raises $550m at a $5.6bn valuation, the question emerges: can specialist AI companies compete when general-purpose platforms like Anthropic enter their domain with faster, cheaper agentic capabilities?

Key takeaways

  • Legora, a Swedish legal AI startup, raised $550m at a $5.6bn valuation, signaling strong investor confidence in vertical AI despite platform competition.
  • The company's CEO declared "Legacy AI is over" and announced a pivot toward "agentic law" — AI systems that don't just assist lawyers but autonomously execute legal workflows.
  • The central challenge: specialist legal AI companies must now compete with Anthropic's agentic models, which can handle domain-specific tasks as part of broader platform capabilities, potentially faster and cheaper.
  • Legora employs over 7,600 people and serves clients across 150 countries, demonstrating that vertical AI companies can achieve significant scale even amid platform competition.
  • The article frames this as a broader question for all vertical AI companies: when foundation model platforms expand into your domain, does specialist expertise and workflow ownership provide enough moat?
