Meet the Sifted 100: Southern Europe's Fastest-Growing Startups of 2026

May 20, 2026 Éanna Kelly Southern Europe, startups, Sifted 100, leaderboard, Milan, Barcelona, B2B SaaS, AI, venture capital, revenue growth
Read the source article

Quick summary

The Sifted 100 Southern Europe leaderboard ranks the region's fastest-growing startups, topped by Milan-based Hotiday (hotel subletting, 1331% CAGR), Lexroom (AI legal tools, 1130% CAGR), and Enginy (AI sales platform, 890% CAGR).

Key takeaways

  • The top three companies — Hotiday, Lexroom, Enginy — are all based in Italy or Spain, confirming Milan and Barcelona as Southern Europe's startup hubs.
  • B2B software dominates the ranking at 32% of companies, continuing a three-year trend, followed by climate tech (9 companies) and AI-native startups (13 companies).
  • Milan leads with 27 companies, Barcelona has 14, Madrid has 7.
  • Six companies in the top 100 raised funding in 2026, with Lexroom's $50M Series B being the largest.
  • The leaderboard methodology uses two-year revenue CAGR with minimum revenue thresholds of €10k in base year and €100k in the most recent year.
