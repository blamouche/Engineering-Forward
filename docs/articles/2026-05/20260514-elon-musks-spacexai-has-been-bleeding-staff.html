Elon Musk's SpaceXAI has been bleeding staff since its merger

May 14, 2026 Rebecca Bellan (TechCrunch) SpaceXAI, xAI, SpaceX, Elon Musk, talent exodus, Grok, pre-training, Meta, Thinking Machines Lab, AI talent war, merger, startup culture
Read the source article

Quick summary

More than 50 researchers and engineers have left SpaceXAI since the SpaceX-xAI merger in February 2026, with key departures including pre-training team lead Juntang Zhuang and core contributors to coding, world models, and Grok voice, raising questions about the company's commitment to frontier model development amid a culture of extreme work and a tightening AI talent market.

Key takeaways

  • Over 50 SpaceXAI employees have departed since February 2026, including the leader of the pre-training team (Juntang Zhuang), with the core pre-training team now reduced to a handful of people.
  • Rivals Meta and Thinking Machines Lab are actively recruiting former xAI staff: at least 11 xAI employees joined Meta, and at least 7 joined Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab.
  • Nearly half of xAI's founding team had already left the company by February 2026, prior to the merger announcement.
  • Employee departures are attributed to multiple factors: Elon Musk's extreme work culture with unrealistic deadlines, post-merger leadership changes, and liquidity events that reduce retention incentives.
  • The pre-training team exodus raises strategic questions about whether SpaceXAI remains committed to developing leading foundation models or is pivoting toward applied products.
