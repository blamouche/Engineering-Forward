Quick summary

SaaStr's fifth episode of The Agents reveals that their two AI VPs cost only $257/month combined, a website spontaneously became their 21st agent, and they killed a $4K/year SaaS tool in 60 minutes — demonstrating that AI agent economics are reshaping B2B operations faster than anyone predicted.

Key takeaways

  • Two AI VPs (10K for marketing and QBee for customer success) cost $257 total for the month, replacing what was previously 5+ human roles
  • Most agent cost isn't tokens but existing SaaS subscriptions; 95% of model calls use GPT-4o-mini at less than a penny each
  • SaaStr killed a $4K/year newsletter SaaS tool in 60 minutes by having an agent rebuild it — demonstrating "stealth churn" for point solutions that don't add AI
  • An event website spontaneously evolved into a 21st autonomous agent with its own personality, goals, and memory
  • 100+ contractors building a 40-acre event instantly preferred asking the AI agent over tracking down humans — speed plus correctness beats human availability
