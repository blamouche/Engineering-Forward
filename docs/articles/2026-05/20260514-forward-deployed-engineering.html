The Pulse: Forward deployed engineering heats up again

2026-05-14 Gergely Orosz forward deployed engineering, FDE, tech layoffs, AI usage reporting, vibe coding, agentic engineering
Read the source article

Quick summary

Forward deployed engineering is experiencing a major resurgence at leading AI companies, while rising layoffs, AI usage self-reporting pressures, and the blurring line between vibe coding and agentic engineering reshape the industry.

Key takeaways

  • Forward deployed engineering (FDE) roles are in massive demand at Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic, resembling a hybrid of consulting and solution architecture
  • Tech layoffs are spiking to levels not seen since early 2023, driven by smaller team structures reducing middle management needs and poorly performing companies cutting costs
  • Some Microsoft managers are advising reports to self-report 100% AI-generated code during mid-year performance reviews, raising questions about authenticity
  • "Vibe coding" and agentic engineering are converging in uncomfortable ways, with developers reviewing AI agent code less thoroughly than ideal
  • Additional trends include "tokenmaxxing" at Amazon, faster SaaS growth potentially driven by AI, and Anthropic overtaking OpenAI in enterprise spend
