Apple's Genmoji and Image Playground Set for Major Visual Overhaul in iOS 27

2026-05-25 Mark Gurman (via Power On) / Priya Singh (Mashable) Apple, iOS 27, Genmoji, Image Playground, on-device AI, image generation, WWDC 2026, third-party AI models, Mark Gurman
Read the source article

Quick summary

Mark Gurman reports that iOS 27 will deliver a major visual overhaul to Apple's Genmoji and Image Playground, significantly improving on-device image generation quality while also opening Image Playground to third-party AI models — a strategic pivot for Apple's AI image tools after their disappointing iOS 18 debut.

Key takeaways

  • Apple's own image generation models for Genmoji and Image Playground have been "improved significantly" with a major quality boost in iOS 27.
  • Image Playground will expand beyond ChatGPT integration to support third-party AI models, with Google's Nano Banana likely among the additions.
  • The initial iOS 18.2 launch was widely criticized: Genmoji was "passable" but Image Playground performed poorly compared to competing tools.
  • The tools are powered by on-device models, which partially explains the initial quality gap but does not fully justify it.
  • Official unveiling expected at WWDC 2026 in June, with public release alongside iOS 27 in September.
