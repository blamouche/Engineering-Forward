Introducing Muse Spark: MSL's First Model, Purpose-Built to Prioritize People

April 8, 2026 (updated May 12, 2026) Meta (Meta Superintelligence Labs) Meta AI, Muse Spark, MSL, Meta Superintelligence Labs, multimodal, AI assistant, personal superintelligence, open-source, shopping AI, voice AI, AI glasses
Read the source article

Quick summary

Meta unveils Muse Spark, the first model from its newly formed Meta Superintelligence Labs, designed to power a smarter, faster, multimodal Meta AI across WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger, and AI glasses — with voice conversations, visual perception, shopping features, and parallel sub-agent reasoning.

Key takeaways

  • Muse Spark is the debut model from Meta Superintelligence Labs, rebuilt from the ground up in nine months and purpose-built for Meta's product ecosystem.
  • It introduces dual-mode interaction: "Instant" for quick answers and "Thinking" for complex reasoning, with parallel sub-agents that can tackle multiple subtasks simultaneously.
  • Strong multimodal perception lets Meta AI see and understand photos — ranking snacks by protein content, identifying products, analyzing health images, and generating visual code (websites, mini-games) from prompts.
  • Shopping mode surfaces Marketplace listings alongside web results with map integration, brand/creator browsing, and new grid-format product display.
  • Voice conversations support natural interruption, topic switching, and language swapping, plus live camera AI for real-world object recognition.
  • The model is rolling out progressively across all Meta surfaces, with private API preview for select partners and plans to open-source future versions.
