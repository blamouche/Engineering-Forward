David Sacks's 11th-Hour Plea Led to Trump's Backtrack on AI Executive Order

May 24, 2026 Amrith Ramkumar, Natalie Andrews, Meghan Bobrowsky AI regulation, Trump, executive order, David Sacks, AI safety, China competition, AI policy, doomers
Read the source article

Quick summary

Venture capitalist and Trump adviser David Sacks successfully lobbied President Trump at the last minute to backtrack on an AI executive order, arguing that voluntary government testing requirements could evolve into mandatory regulations that handicap US companies against Chinese competitors.

Key takeaways

  • Trump was poised to sign a long-awaited executive order establishing a voluntary process for government testing of advanced AI models for safety risks.
  • David Sacks intervened in an 11th-hour call, warning that voluntary testing frameworks inevitably become mandatory regulations.
  • Sacks framed the order as a victory for "AI doomers" who advocate strong safety guardrails, arguing it would slow US AI progress in the race with China.
  • The backtrack represents a win for business-minded officials who prioritize speed and competitiveness over precautionary safety measures.
  • The episode illustrates ongoing tension within the administration between national security-driven AI caution and economic competitiveness imperatives.
