OpenAI Debuts $4B AI Services Company As Rival Anthropic Builds Its Own

May 11, 2026 CRN OpenAI, Anthropic, services business, Deployment Company, AI consulting, channel partners, enterprise AI
Read the source article

Quick summary

OpenAI has launched a $4 billion-plus AI services company — the OpenAI Deployment Co. — hot on the heels of a similar $1.5 billion move by Anthropic, marking a new phase where frontier AI labs are building professional services arms to bridge the gap between model capability and enterprise deployment.

Key takeaways

  • OpenAI's "DeployCo" is backed by $4B+ from 19 investment firms, consultancies, and system integrators including Capgemini, Bain, McKinsey, TPG, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and SoftBank
  • The first acquisition is London-based AI consulting firm Tomoro, bringing 150 forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) with clients including Tesco, Virgin Atlantic, and Supercell
  • The move mirrors Anthropic's recent $1.5B services company launch with Blackstone, Hellman & Friedman, and Goldman Sachs
  • Both companies are simultaneously investing in channel partner programs — OpenAI hired Colleen Kapase (ex-Google Cloud, Snowflake) and Anthropic launched its Claude Partner Network with $100M initial investment
  • DeployCo engagements follow a structured methodology: AI diagnostics → priority workflow identification → design/build/test → production deployment connecting models to customer data, tools, and business processes
  • A rivalry is emerging between traditional solution providers (who bring decades of enterprise process knowledge) and born-in-AI upstarts (who move faster and have deeper AI-native expertise)
  • OpenAI reports more than 1 million businesses have adopted its products and APIs, while DeployCo's investor/consulting partners sponsor over 2,000 businesses globally
