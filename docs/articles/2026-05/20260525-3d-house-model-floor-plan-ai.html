How to Create an Interactive 3D House Model from a Floor Plan Using AI

May 25, 2026 ChatPRD (from Felix Rieseberg interview) Claude Cowork, 3D modeling, floor plan, furniture, email parsing, Gmail, interactive
Read the source article

Quick summary

A step-by-step workflow for turning a realtor's floor plan into an interactive 3D walkthrough by using Claude to find reference dimensions in permits, extrude walls into 3D, and populate the model with actual furniture parsed from email receipts.

Key takeaways

  • Step 1: consolidate all house documents in one folder; Claude finds a reference (e.g., garage permit dimensions) to calculate the entire floor plan.
  • Step 2: instead of asking Claude to place furniture, ask it to build an interactive 2D planner — automate the outcome, not the task.
  • Step 3: Claude converts the 2D plan to 3D by analyzing wall positions and extruding them, creating a walkthrough-like experience.
  • Step 4: connect Gmail to Claude Cowork; Claude parses furniture purchase receipts for dimensions and adds actual items to the 3D model.
