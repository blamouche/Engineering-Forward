This 11-Person London Startup Wants to Make AI 100x Cheaper Every Year

2026-05-15 Maya Dharampal-Hornby (Sifted) Doubleword, Meryem Arik, AI inference, cost reduction, GPU optimization, sovereign compute, London startup, MIT, NVIDIA
Read the source article

Quick summary

Doubleword, an 11-person London startup founded by former NVIDIA and TitanML engineer Meryem Arik, is building AI inference infrastructure that claims to be 100x cheaper than competitors — and has already secured £2M in funding and a deployment on France's national supercomputer.

Key takeaways

  • Doubleword's pricing claims to be 100x cheaper than competitors like Together AI and Fireworks, with inference at $0.07 per million tokens versus competitors at $7–$14
  • The company is already deployed on France's Jean Zay supercomputer through a partnership with GENCI, France's national computing agency
  • Arik argues that current AI inference pricing is artificially inflated by GPU scarcity and vendor lock-in, not by underlying cost structures
  • The startup targets "sovereign compute" — enabling nations and enterprises to run AI workloads on their own infrastructure rather than depending on US hyperscalers
  • Arik describes the current AI market as "a bubble where someone's paying for it right now" and positions Doubleword for the inevitable pricing correction
