Anthropic has acquired the dev tools startup used by OpenAI, Google, and Cloudflare

May 18, 2026 Kirsten Korosec Anthropic, Stainless, acquisition, SDK, developer tools, MCP, OpenAI, Google, Cloudflare, Sequoia, Andreessen Horowitz, Alex Rattray
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic acquires Stainless for more than $300 million, removing a critical SDK infrastructure provider from the hands of competitors including OpenAI and Google, and will shut down all hosted Stainless products going forward.

Key takeaways

  • The deal is reportedly valued at over $300 million, with The Information first reporting the talks; Stainless was backed by Sequoia Capital and Andreessen Horowitz
  • Anthropic will wind down all hosted Stainless products, including the SDK generator — existing customers retain ownership of previously generated SDKs but lose access to the platform
  • Stainless counted OpenAI, Google, Replicate, Runway, and Cloudflare among its customers, making this a direct strike at competitors' infrastructure
  • The acquisition reflects the growing strategic importance of SDK tooling as AI agents increasingly need to connect to external APIs
  • Founder Alex Rattray, a former Stripe engineer, framed the acquisition as letting the team "keep doing the work we love, on the platform where it matters most"
