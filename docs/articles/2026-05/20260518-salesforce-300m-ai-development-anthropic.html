Salesforce sets aside $300M for AI development with Anthropic

May 18, 2026 Mels Dees Salesforce, Anthropic, Claude, AI tokens, Marc Benioff, Slack, software development, AI agents, enterprise AI, Agentforce
Read the source article

Quick summary

Salesforce expects to spend approximately $300 million on Anthropic AI tokens this year, primarily for AI-assisted software development, making it one of Anthropic's largest customers and signaling AI's shift from experimental technology to fixed operational cost.

Key takeaways

  • CEO Marc Benioff disclosed the spending plan on the All-In podcast, stating the majority of the $300M budget goes toward AI-assisted coding
  • Salesforce plans to integrate programming functionality into Slack, building on dozens of AI features already added to Slackbot this year
  • The company expects Slack to generate approximately $3 billion in revenue this year, with its AI agent division Agentforce showing strong recurring revenue growth
  • Benioff advocates for an intelligent routing layer that directs complex tasks to powerful models like Claude and simpler tasks to smaller, cheaper models
  • Salesforce previously invested hundreds of millions in Anthropic equity, partly because it couldn't secure a position in OpenAI
