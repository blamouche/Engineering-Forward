Helix-02 humanoid robot handles full 8-hour factory work shifts

May 13, 2026 Neetika Walter Figure AI, Helix-02, humanoid robots, autonomous shifts, factory automation, neural control, BMW, robotics
Read the source article

Quick summary

Figure AI demonstrates its Helix-02 humanoid robots running fully autonomous 8-hour factory shifts — combining vision, touch, and whole-body control in a single neural network — marking a milestone toward human-scale robotic labor in industrial settings.

Key takeaways

  • Helix-02 is a unified neural network handling walking, manipulation, balance, and coordination without separate controllers — replacing 109,000 lines of C++ code with learned control
  • The "System 0" controller was trained on 1,000+ hours of human motion data for stable, human-like movement
  • Robots demonstrated fine motor tasks: unscrewing caps, extracting pills, pushing syringes, picking parts from cluttered bins using tactile sensing
  • Multi-robot collaboration shown: two robots resetting a bedroom in under 2 minutes, coordinating around shared objects without a central controller
  • Figure AI previously deployed at BMW, completing 10-hour shifts and supporting production of 30,000+ vehicles
