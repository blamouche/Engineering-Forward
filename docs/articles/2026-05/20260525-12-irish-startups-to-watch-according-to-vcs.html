12 Irish Startups to Watch, According to VCs

May 25, 2026 Martin Coulter Irish startups, venture capital, Dublin Tech Week, European tech, startup ecosystem, entrepreneurship
Read the source article

Quick summary

As investors descend on Dublin for Tech Week, VCs highlight 12 Irish startups to watch spanning AI, fintech, healthtech, and B2B SaaS, reflecting Ireland's maturing ecosystem beyond its traditional role as a US tech gateway.

Key takeaways

  • Ireland's startup ecosystem raised €335M across 114 deals in 2025, recovering from €0.2B across 145 deals the prior year, signaling renewed investor confidence.
  • Dublin Tech Week is attracting global investors, underscoring Ireland's position as an English-speaking launchpad for US tech companies entering Europe.
  • The 12 featured startups span diverse sectors: AI-driven legal/compliance tools, B2B SaaS, fintech infrastructure, healthtech, and deep tech.
  • Key themes include AI-first business models, platforms built for European regulatory complexity, and companies that leverage Ireland's unique position between US and EU markets.
  • VCs emphasize that Ireland's talent pool — fed by multinational tech offices and strong universities — is producing a generation of experienced founders.
