Clouded Judgement 5.22.26 - The Neocloud Boom

May 22, 2026 Jamin Ball neocloud, AI infrastructure, data centers, Coreweave, Nebius, IREN, SpaceX, xAI, GPUs, enterprise value
Read the source article

Quick summary

Jamin Ball makes the case for a massive neocloud boom driven by AI infrastructure buildout, estimating $7.5 trillion in total data center capex over 4.5 years and $13.5 trillion in potential enterprise value creation.

Key takeaways

  • Anthropic and OpenAI are estimated at ~3-3.5GW of compute capacity each by end of 2025, with OpenAI targeting 30GW by 2030.
  • Building 1GW of Blackwell-dominated data center capacity costs approximately $50 billion, with ~70% going to chips/compute and ~20% to data center shell and power infrastructure.
  • The estimated ~150GW of new capacity over 4.5 years represents ~$7.5T in total spend, equivalent to ~5% of annual US GDP.
  • Coreweave and IREN trade at roughly $90 billion of enterprise value per deployed GW, suggesting $13.5T in total enterprise value creation if the math holds.
  • SpaceX's xAI acquisition created an instant ~$15B/year neocloud business line renting Colossus data center capacity to Anthropic — nearly matching SpaceX's entire 2025 revenue of $18.7B achieved over 23 years.
