Everything Google Announced at I/O 2026: Gemini, Search, Android XR, and More

May 19, 2026 Abner Li google, io-2026, gemini, android, search, ai, gemini-spark, antigravity, android-xr
Read the source article

Quick summary

Google I/O 2026 delivered a sweeping set of AI-powered features across Gemini, Search, Workspace, YouTube, and Android, anchored by the Gemini 3.5 Flash model, a new proactive "Spark" agent, and intelligent eyewear running Android XR.

Key takeaways

  • Gemini 3.5 Flash combines frontier intelligence with agentic capabilities, surpassing 3.1 Pro in benchmarks at Flash-tier speed
  • Gemini Spark is a proactive personal agent that takes actions on users' behalf, integrating with Gmail, Docs, and MCP-enabled third-party tools
  • Google shifted from daily prompt limits to a "compute-used" model, factoring prompt complexity and features
  • Google AI Ultra dropped from $250 to $200/month, with a new $100/month tier introduced
  • Android XR "intelligent eyewear" — audio glasses made with Samsung, Qualcomm, Gentle Monster, and Warby Parker — arriving this fall with iPhone support
