Europe's Hot AI Startups Set to Poach More Talent from Big Tech

May 12, 2026 Anne Sraders AI talent migration, Big Tech exodus, European startups, Cusp AI, talent poaching, tech recruiting, startup compensation, equity incentives
Read the source article

Quick summary

A growing wave of European AI startups including Cusp AI, AMI Labs, Multiverse, and Lovable are successfully poaching senior talent from Big Tech giants like Apple, Google, and Meta, with recruiters predicting this trend will accelerate as AI startups offer compelling equity upside and mission-driven work.

Key takeaways

  • The watershed moment was Apple's former AI chief John Giannandrea joining UK-based AI materials startup Cusp AI, signaling that even the most senior Big Tech AI leaders see greater opportunity in European startups.
  • European AI startups like Cusp AI, AMI Labs, Multiverse Computing, and Lovable have all recently made high-profile hires from tech giants including Apple, Meta, and Google DeepMind.
  • Recruiters note that Big Tech companies can't match the equity upside potential—startups can offer life-changing returns at exit that even generous FAANG RSU packages cannot compete with.
  • Compensation for top AI talent at European startups has escalated dramatically, with some roles commanding packages in the £400k-600k range and CTO positions reaching the millions.
  • Some startups have adopted a "promethean profile" hiring approach: targeting former founders and builders who have already demonstrated they can build under pressure and thrive in uncertainty.
