25 European YC Startups Due to Raise a Series A

May 13, 2026 Federico Scolari and Oshin Narang Y Combinator, Series A, European startups, demo day, VC funding, accelerator, spring 2026 batch
Read the source article

Quick summary

Sifted profiles 25 European startups from Y Combinator's spring 2026 batch that are approaching demo day and preparing to raise Series A funding, collectively having raised nearly $1 billion, spanning sectors from agentic AI and fintech to biotech and climate tech.

Key takeaways

  • Y Combinator's spring 2026 batch includes 25 European startups approaching demo day, collectively having raised approximately $966M
  • The European contingent spans diverse sectors: agentic/frontier AI, fintech, biotech, climate tech, devtools, defense, and enterprise SaaS
  • Some companies have already raised significant pre-Series A capital, with individual funding reaching up to hundreds of millions
  • YC's European founder intake has grown dramatically from just 9 companies in spring 2025 to 25 in spring 2026
  • The batch reflects Europe's maturing startup ecosystem and YC's role as a bridge connecting European entrepreneurs to international capital
