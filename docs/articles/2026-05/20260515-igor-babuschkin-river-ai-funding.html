Igor Babuschkin seeks up to $1 billion for River AI

May 15, 2026 Igor Babuschkin, River AI, AI funding, billion-dollar raise, AI startup
Read the source article

Quick summary

Former DeepMind/OpenAI researcher Igor Babuschkin is reportedly seeking up to $1 billion in funding for his new AI venture, River AI, joining the wave of elite researchers raising massive rounds for independent AI labs.

Key takeaways

  • Babuschkin previously worked at DeepMind and OpenAI, specializing in reinforcement learning and language models
  • River AI joins the trend of Big Tech AI alumni raising massive rounds: Ineffable Intelligence ($1.1B), AMI Labs ($1B), Recursive Superintelligence ($650M)
  • The billion-dollar seed round phenomenon reflects investor conviction that the next AI breakthroughs will come from independent labs rather than incumbents
  • This continues the pattern of top researchers leaving major labs to pursue more focused, ambitious research agendas
