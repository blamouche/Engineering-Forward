Pope Approves Creation of Interdicasterial Commission on Artificial Intelligence

May 16, 2026 Isabella H. de Carvalho Vatican, Pope Leo XIV, artificial intelligence, Catholic Church, AI ethics, human dignity, interdicasterial commission
Read the source article

Quick summary

Pope Leo XIV has approved the creation of a new Vatican Interdicasterial Commission on Artificial Intelligence, bringing together seven Church bodies to coordinate the Holy See's response to AI's challenges to human dignity, justice, and labor.

Key takeaways

  • The rescript was signed May 12, released May 16, following a May 3 audience between the Pope and Cardinal Michael Czerny
  • Seven Vatican bodies compose the commission: Dicasteries for Integral Human Development, Doctrine of the Faith, Culture and Education, Communication, plus the Pontifical Academies for Life, Sciences, and Social Sciences
  • One institution coordinates for one-year renewable terms, with the Pope deciding rotations — the Dicastery for Promoting Integral Human Development goes first
  • The commission traces back to Pope Leo XIV's first days, when he cited his namesake Leo XIII's *Rerum Novarum* response to the industrial revolution as inspiration for the Church's AI response
  • This builds on the January 2025 joint note *Antiqua et Nova* on the relationship between artificial and human intelligence
