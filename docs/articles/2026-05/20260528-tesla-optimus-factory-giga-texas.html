Tesla's Dedicated Optimus Factory Construction Underway at Giga Texas

2026-05-28 TLDR / Tesery tesla, optimus, humanoid-robot, gigafactory-texas, manufacturing, robotics, automation
Read the source article

Quick summary

Tesla's dedicated Optimus humanoid robot factory is officially under construction at Gigafactory Texas, with drone footage confirming steel structures rising on the North Campus — a facility targeting 10 million robots per year (~27,000/day) with high-volume production starting summer 2027.

Key takeaways

  • Construction confirmed May 27, 2026 via drone footage by Joe Tegtmeyer — steel structure rising at Giga Texas North Campus, part of a 5.2M+ sq ft expansion
  • Production target: 10 million Optimus units per year (~27,000 robots/day) — a figure with no precedent in complex manufacturing
  • The factory building extends nearly the full length of the main Giga Texas vehicle factory (~4,000+ ft), optimized specifically for humanoid robot assembly
  • Two-phase strategy: Phase 1 pilot production at Fremont starting July/August 2026 (internal deployment only), Phase 2 high-volume at Texas targeting summer 2027
  • Co-located with the planned Terafab AI chip factory — robot "brains" produced adjacent to robot "bodies" for tightly coupled supply chain
  • Musk's thesis: Optimus will be more valuable than Tesla's entire automotive business, with a target price under $20,000 and an addressable market of global labor
  • Tesla ended Model S and Model X production at Fremont to clear space for the Optimus pilot line
