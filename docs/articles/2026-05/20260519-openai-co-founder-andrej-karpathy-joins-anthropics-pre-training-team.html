OpenAI co-founder Andrej Karpathy joins Anthropic's pre-training team

May 19, 2026 Rebecca Bellan, Lorenzo Franceschi-Bicchierai Andrej Karpathy, Anthropic, OpenAI, pre-training, Claude, AI talent, frontier models, Chris Rohlf, red team
Read the source article

Quick summary

Andrej Karpathy, the AI researcher who co-founded OpenAI and led Tesla's Autopilot, has joined Anthropic's pre-training team to build a group using Claude to accelerate pre-training research — signaling that AI-assisted research, not just raw compute, is Anthropic's competitive strategy.

Key takeaways

  • Karpathy joins Anthropic's pre-training team under Nick Joseph, focused on using Claude to accelerate pre-training research — a meta-application of AI to improve AI development.
  • His hire signals Anthropic's belief that AI-assisted research, rather than pure compute scaling, is the key to staying competitive with OpenAI and Google.
  • Karpathy is one of the rare researchers who bridges LLM theory and large-scale training practice, having co-founded OpenAI, led Tesla's FSD/Autopilot, and created educational AI content.
  • His education startup Eureka Labs appears paused; Karpathy says he "remains deeply passionate about education and plan to resume my work on it in time."
  • Separately, Anthropic hired cybersecurity veteran Chris Rohlf (ex-Yahoo Paranoids, Meta) for its frontier red team to stress-test advanced models against severe threats.
