Android is getting a big AI overhaul in 2026

May 12, 2026 Ryan Whitwam Android, Google, Gemini, AI, automation, Auto Browse, Autofill, widgets, Rambler, voice input, Android Auto, Android 17, privacy, security
Read the source article

Quick summary

Google is rolling out a sweeping AI-powered overhaul of Android in 2026, infusing the Gemini Intelligence platform across app automation, voice input, widgets, web browsing, and in-car experiences, while Android 17 adds incremental camera, security, and privacy improvements.

Key takeaways

  • App automation is expanding through Gemini Intelligence to handle multi-step cross-app tasks (e.g., parsing a syllabus from Gmail and adding books to a shopping cart), but remains limited to select food, grocery, and ride-hailing apps.
  • Chrome's Auto Browse agent is coming to Android 12+ devices in late June, enabling Gemini to navigate mobile web pages and perform multi-step tasks — though desktop performance has been underwhelming.
  • A new "Create My Widget" feature lets users generate AI-powered, resizable Material-themed widgets via prompts or suggested recipes, displaying personalized data from accounts or the web.
  • The "Rambler" voice-input feature in Gboard uses AI to clean up and summarize dictated speech, removing filler words while preserving the speaker's tone and context, with no audio or text retention.
  • Android Auto gets a major refresh with adaptive display sizing, Immersive Navigation, video playback when parked (with select automakers), new Material 3 Expressive themes, and vehicle-camera integration for lane guidance.
