After Automation

May 21, 2026 Dan Shipper AI, automation, future of work, human-AI collaboration, agents, knowledge work, commoditization, expertise
Read the source article

Quick summary

The more we automate with AI, the more expert human work there is to do — because AI commoditizes past competence, flooding the world with sameness that creates new demand for human judgment and originality.

Key takeaways

  • AI adoption at Every (30-person team) has not reduced headcount but transformed how work gets done — nobody writes code by hand anymore, and AI handles 95% of the CEO's email.
  • Agent work is settling into two modes: asynchronous "agent employees" (coworker and embedded agents) and synchronous human-AI collaboration in tools like Codex and Claude Code.
  • The "human sandwich" pattern: humans frame the task, AI collapses the work, humans judge and extend the result — this applies beyond coding to writing, email, and all knowledge work.
  • AI's feedback loop: cheap competence → rapid adoption → abundance of sameness ("slop") → demand for differentiation → new demand for human experts.
  • The current generation of models only knows work that has been done; humans know what needs to be done right now, in this specific moment, making human judgment irreplaceable even as AI capabilities accelerate.
