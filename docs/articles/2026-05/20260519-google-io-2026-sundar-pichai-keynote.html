I/O 2026: Welcome to the agentic Gemini era

May 19, 2026 Sundar Pichai Google I/O 2026, Gemini, agentic AI, AI Mode, Search, Gemini app, infrastructure, tokens
Read the source article

Quick summary

Sundar Pichai's Google I/O 2026 keynote declares the "agentic Gemini era," revealing that Google processes 3.2 quadrillion tokens monthly — a 7x increase — with 900M Gemini app users and 1B+ AI Mode users.

Key takeaways

  • Token processing surged from 480 trillion to 3.2 quadrillion monthly, with 8.5 million developers building on Google's models and APIs processing 19 billion tokens per minute.
  • AI Overviews hit 2.5 billion monthly active users; AI Mode surpassed 1 billion monthly users in just one year, becoming the "biggest upgrade to Search ever."
  • The Gemini app doubled to 900 million monthly users, and daily requests grew 7x year-over-year.
  • New consumer features: Ask YouTube (AI-powered video navigation), Docs Live (voice-powered document creation), and natural conversational AI in Maps and other products.
  • Google's full-stack approach — custom silicon, research, models, products — is positioned as a competitive moat enabling faster iteration across billions of users.
  • Pichai framed the shift from AI as a feature to AI as a persistent agent, with 50 billion images generated via Nano Banana models reflecting latent creative demand.
