Anthropic beats OpenAI on business adoption

May 13, 2026 Ara Kharazian (Ramp Economics Lab) Anthropic, OpenAI, AI model providers, business adoption, Ramp AI Index, enterprise AI, Claude, GPT, AI inference platforms, cost optimization, Codex
Read the source article

Quick summary

For the first time, Anthropic passed OpenAI in business adoption at 34.4% to 32.3%, but three headwinds — misaligned incentives, declining Claude quality, and cost-inefficient model updates — suggest the lead may be fragile.

Key takeaways

  • Anthropic quadrupled business adoption over the last year while OpenAI grew by only 0.3%, according to Ramp spend data across thousands of businesses
  • Overall AI adoption reached 50.6% of businesses, meaning half of companies still run no AI in production
  • Anthropic's incentive structure is misaligned with customers: the company profits more when businesses use expensive models, creating pressure to route to costly models even when cheaper ones suffice
  • Claude quality complaints (frequent outages, rate limits, declining results) and a model update that 3x token costs for image prompts are eroding goodwill
  • The fastest-growing vendors on Ramp's platform are AI inference platforms providing access to cheap, open-source models, suggesting a potential shift toward cost-optimized alternatives
