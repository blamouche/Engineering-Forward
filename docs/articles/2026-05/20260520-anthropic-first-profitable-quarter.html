Mind-Blowing Growth Is About to Propel Anthropic Into Its First Profitable Quarter

2026-05-20 Berber Jin Anthropic, revenue, profitability, AI boom, Dario Amodei, funding round, valuation
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic is projecting a 130% quarterly revenue surge to $10.9 billion and its first-ever operating profit in the June 2026 quarter, defying skeptics who argued that AI companies' massive infrastructure spending would prevent near-term profitability — all while raising a funding round likely to push its valuation above OpenAI's.

Key takeaways

  • Revenue projected to more than double from Q1 to Q2 2026, reaching $10.9 billion (130% quarterly growth)
  • The company expects its first operating profit in the June quarter, a milestone that challenges the "AI companies can't make money" narrative
  • Disclosed to investors as part of an ongoing funding round expected to push Anthropic's valuation above OpenAI's
  • The growth rate was described as "mind-blowing" and defies conventional wisdom about AI startup economics
  • The projections come as Anthropic simultaneously locks in massive compute deals (SpaceX $45B) — suggesting revenue growth is outpacing infrastructure costs
