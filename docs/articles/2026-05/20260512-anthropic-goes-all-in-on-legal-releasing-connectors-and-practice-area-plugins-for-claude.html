Anthropic Goes All-In on Legal, Releasing More Than 20 Connectors and 12 Practice-Area Plugins for Claude

May 12, 2026 Bob Ambrogi Anthropic, Claude, legal AI, MCP connectors, practice-area plugins, legal tech, Thomson Reuters, Harvey, iManage, NetDocuments, e-discovery, law firms, access to justice
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic released more than 20 MCP connectors linking Claude to virtually every segment of legal technology — from iManage and NetDocuments to Relativity and Thomson Reuters — along with 12 practice-area-specific plugins, marking its most aggressive push into the legal market yet.

Key takeaways

  • The 20+ MCP connectors cover document management (iManage, NetDocuments), e-discovery (Relativity, Everlaw), contracts (Ironclad, DocuSign), M&A (Box, Datasite), and legal research (Midpage, Trellis, Harvey).
  • Thomson Reuters integrated bidirectionally: CoCounsel Legal runs on Claude, and Claude can now call CoCounsel as a tool — a pattern where the foundation model both underpins and competes with the application layer.
  • The 12 new plugins span Commercial, Corporate, M&A, Employment, Privacy, Product, Regulatory, AI Governance, IP, and Litigation practice areas, each starting with a setup interview that learns a team's specific playbooks.
  • Anthropic emphasized Claude now works across Microsoft Word, Outlook, Excel, and PowerPoint, carrying context across all four applications.
  • A public-service component includes partnerships with Free Law Project, Courtroom5 (for self-represented litigants), and discounted pricing for legal aid organizations.
  • The announcement builds on February's initial legal plugin, which rattled legal tech stocks; today's release is far more substantial, and legal AI vendors like Harvey and Relativity are now betting on deep integration rather than competition.
