We're Hiring a Human Marketing Exec to Report to 10K, Our AI VP Marketing. Could You Report to an AI?

May 2026 Jason Lemkin AI VP of Marketing, human-AI collaboration, demand generation, email marketing, agentic marketing, AI-native org chart, SaaStr, 10K, AI agents, B2B marketing, taste, judgment
Read the source article

Quick summary

SaaStr is hiring a human Director of Digital Marketing to report to 10K, their AI VP of Marketing — a role that inverts the traditional org chart and demands taste, judgment, and hands-on campaign execution as the primary human contributions in an agentic marketing stack.

Key takeaways

  • 10K (14,230 lines of code on Replit) already autonomously ships end-to-end campaigns: daily briefs based on pacing data, audience builds, copy variants, send sequences, and post-mortems — some meaningfully outperforming human-sent campaigns due to true 1:1 personalization at 400K+ recipient scale.
  • The human role is explicitly not a strategist or manager of agents; it's a hands-on demand gen leader who plans daily with 10K, reviews every campaign before it ships, and runs the plays the AI proposes.
  • Three things 10K explicitly cannot do: connect dots into winning weekly plays (requires human orchestration), exercise taste and judgment (AI can produce 50 variants, human picks the 3 that ship), and handle relationship work (sponsor calls, partner conversations, key account outreach).
  • The job description filters for candidates already using AI daily to ship more than their peers — explicitly not training people on how to work with agents, expecting the hire to teach SaaStr things.
  • The pitch to candidates is explicit: "No politics. No drama. No games. Just execute." — targeting senior demand gen leaders drowning in quarterly reorg theater at public B2B companies.
  • The career argument: five years from now, every demand gen leader will have learned to plan and ship with AI; the ones who learned first, inside a real production deployment, will be running demand gen at the best companies.
  • The org chart explicitly inverts: the human reports to the AI VP. Lemkin frames this as a filter — if "I report to a bot" sounds insulting rather than interesting, this isn't the role.
  • 10K's agent stack includes: Artisan (3 SDR instances, outbound), Qualified (inbound BDR, $1M+ closed-won, 71% of closed deals), Agentforce (win-back), QBee (AI VP of CS, 70% reduction in human hours), Momentum (call intelligence), and Hexy (revenue diagnostics).
