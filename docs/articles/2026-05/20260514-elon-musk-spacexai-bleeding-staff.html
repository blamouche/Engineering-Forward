Elon Musk's SpaceXAI has been bleeding staff since its merger

May 14, 2026 Rebecca Bellan SpaceXAI, xAI, Elon Musk, talent exodus, Meta, Thinking Machines Lab, pre-training, AI talent war
Read the source article

Quick summary

More than 50 researchers and engineers have left Elon Musk's SpaceXAI since the February merger, with key leaders in coding, world models, and Grok voice departing — and the pre-training team dwindling to just a handful of people.

Key takeaways

  • At least 11 xAI employees defected to Meta, 7 to Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab, and key leaders across coding, world models, and Grok voice have left
  • The pre-training team — responsible for building new AI models — has been decimated following the exit of team lead Juntang Zhuang
  • Musk's culture of extreme work and unrealistic deadlines contributed to departures, with sources saying corners were cut on Grok
  • SpaceX tender offers and IPO expectations may paradoxically accelerated exits by reducing equity lock-in incentives
  • The question now is whether SpaceXAI remains committed to developing leading AI models or pivots toward applied products
