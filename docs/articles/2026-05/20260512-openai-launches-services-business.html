OpenAI Launches $4B AI Services Business on Heels of Similar Anthropic Announcement

May 12, 2026 CRN Staff OpenAI, DeployCo, Anthropic, AI services, channel partners, system integrators, enterprise AI, Tomoro, TPG, Goldman Sachs
Read the source article

Quick summary

OpenAI announced a $4 billion-plus AI services company called DeployCo — built on the acquisition of UK consultancy Tomoro — following closely on Anthropic's own ~$1.5 billion services company announcement, signaling an arms race in enterprise AI deployment services.

Key takeaways

  • DeployCo will embed forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) specialized in frontier AI into organizations, working alongside OpenAI's Frontier Alliance partner ecosystem and the broader consulting industry.
  • Investors include TPG (lead), Brookfield ($500M), Advent, Bain, Goldman Sachs, SoftBank, and Warburg Pincus — with private equity sponsors able to leverage portfolio companies for early AI transformation use cases.
  • The Tomoro acquisition brings ~150 FDEs and deployment specialists; more acquisitions are planned, with the deal expected to close "in the coming months."
  • Both OpenAI and Anthropic are simultaneously investing in channel partner programs — OpenAI hired Colleen Kapase (formerly Google Cloud, Snowflake) for a top channel post, while Anthropic launched its Claude Partner Network with $100M.
  • A rivalry is emerging between traditional solution providers (who argue they bring better security and business process understanding) and born-in-AI upstarts (who claim faster execution and deeper AI expertise).
