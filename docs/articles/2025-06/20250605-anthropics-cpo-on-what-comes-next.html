Anthropic's CPO: Here's what comes next

June 5, 2025 Lenny Rachitsky Anthropic, product strategy, AI safety, enterprise, roadmap
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic's CPO describes the next phase of the company as a shift from model breakthroughs to reliable deployment, emphasizing safety, enterprise adoption, and product practices that scale trust alongside capability.

Key takeaways

  • The near-term focus is on turning model advances into dependable products and workflows.
  • Enterprise adoption depends on reliability, governance, and clear safety boundaries.
  • Product strategy must balance rapid capability growth with controlled rollout and evaluation.
  • Competitive advantage comes from integrated systems, not just raw model performance.
  • The organization is aligning research, product, and go-to-market around trust and usability.
