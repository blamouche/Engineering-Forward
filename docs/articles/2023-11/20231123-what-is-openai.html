What is OpenAI, Really?

2023-11-23 Gergely Orosz OpenAI, Sam Altman, board governance, nonprofit, for-profit, Microsoft, corporate structure, AI safety, ChatGPT
Read the source article

Quick summary

The November 2023 firing-and-return of Sam Altman exposed a fundamental governance tension at OpenAI between its nonprofit mission and for-profit commercial ambitions, nearly destroying the company in five days.

Key takeaways

  • OpenAI's board of six (3 employees, 3 outsiders) fired Sam Altman in a coup led by chief scientist Ilya Sutskever over safety and speed concerns
  • 743 of 778 employees signed a petition threatening to join Microsoft unless the board resigned and Altman returned
  • The crisis revealed OpenAI's exotic corporate structure: a nonprofit board controls a capped for-profit entity
  • Microsoft, which invested $10B and was not notified in advance, ultimately gained leverage with Altman briefly joining Microsoft
  • The core tension between nonprofit AI safety mission and for-profit commercialization remains unresolved
