Proof Indigestion: Terence Tao on AI Generating Proofs Humans Can't Verify

2026-08-30 Every Staff Terence Tao, mathematical proofs, AI proofs, proof indigestion, Lean, verification, mathematics
Read the source article

Quick summary

Terence Tao, one of the world's greatest mathematicians, warns that AI is now generating apparently correct proofs faster than humans can verify them — creating a "proof indigestion" problem where the bottleneck has shifted from finding proofs to understanding them.

Key takeaways

  • Terence Tao identifies a new problem: AI generates proofs faster than humans can verify them
  • AI produced a proof of Sendov's conjecture (a longstanding problem about polynomial roots) that passed all 90,000 lines but was barely understandable to humans
  • Tao's human version ran to about 15,000 lines and was "remarkably elementary" using surprisingly basic tools
  • The division of labor between humans and machines in mathematics is changing — humans may become translators of AI proofs
  • This parallels the AI-native SDLC problem: when AI produces output too complex for human review, the verification bottleneck becomes the issue
