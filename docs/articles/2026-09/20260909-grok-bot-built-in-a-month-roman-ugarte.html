How We Built Grok Bot in a Month | Roman Ugarte (SpaceXAI)

2026-09-08 Lenny's Newsletter (interview with Roman Ugarte, formerly Cursor Growth lead, now SpaceXAI) Grok Bot, SpaceXAI, Roman Ugarte, Cursor, agent product building, from-scratch decision, onboarding, colleague-pilled philosophy, moats, competitive market
Read the source article

Quick summary

Roman Ugarte, who previously helped scale Cursor from 15 to 1,000+ people before its SpaceX acquisition, led a small isolated team that built Grok Bot from first line of code to working internal product in four weeks and a hugely successful public launch three weeks later — by building from scratch rather than bolting onto Cursor, personally onboarding ~300 first users, and adopting a "colleague-pilled" product philosophy.

Key takeaways

  • A small, isolated team took Grok Bot from first code to working internal product in 4 weeks, and to public launch in 7 weeks total
  • The key strategic decision was building Grok Bot from scratch rather than adding it to Cursor — despite Cursor's existing user base and infrastructure
  • The team personally onboarded nearly 300 of its first users, investing in direct human contact to learn what mattered before scaling
  • Two early product decisions were critical to Grok Bot's success (detailed in the full interview)
  • The "colleague-pilled" product philosophy treats the AI agent as a coworker rather than a tool, which shapes everything from UX to feature design
  • Roman's advice on moats: what has allowed Cursor to keep winning in the most competitive market in the world (AI coding tools) despite well-funded competition
