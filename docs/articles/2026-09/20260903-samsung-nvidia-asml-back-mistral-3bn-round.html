Samsung, Nvidia, ASML Back €3bn Mistral Round

2026-09-03 Sifted Daily (Tom Nugent) Mistral, Samsung, Nvidia, ASML, European AI, Series D, sovereign AI, semiconductor, EU Scaleup Fund, European tech funding
Read the source article

Quick summary

French AI startup Mistral is set to announce a €3bn Series D led by Samsung with participation from Nvidia, ASML, and the EU Scaleup Fund — an oversubscribed round that signals deep strategic alignment between European AI and the global semiconductor supply chain.

Key takeaways

  • Mistral is raising a €3bn Series D, oversubscribed and capped at €3bn, expected to close in coming days
  • Samsung is the largest investor, followed by Nvidia and ASML
  • ASML led Mistral's previous €1.7bn Series C at €11.7bn post-money valuation one year ago
  • The round includes the European Union's Scaleup Fund, signaling political backing for sovereign European AI
  • Nvidia backs Spanish spinout IPronics in $125m Series B for optical switches in AI data centers
  • Sifted 100 Nordics ranking shows 27 of 100 fastest-growing Nordic startups are climate techs, with energy startups making up nearly 1 in 10
  • Norwegian hydrogen startup Hystar ranks second overall on the Nordics leaderboard; Danish electricity supplier Reel ranks fourth
