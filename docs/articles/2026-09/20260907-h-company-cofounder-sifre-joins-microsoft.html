H Company Cofounder Laurent Sifre Joins Microsoft to Build Paris AI Research Team

2026-09-07 Freya Pratty H Company, Laurent Sifre, Microsoft, Paris AI, agentic AI, DeepMind, talent migration
Read the source article

Quick summary

Laurent Sifre, cofounder of Paris-based agentic AI startup H Company, has joined Microsoft to build a Paris-focused AI research team, signaling Big Tech's continued pull on European AI talent.

Key takeaways

  • Laurent Sifre, cofounder of H Company (Paris-based agentic AI startup), has joined Microsoft
  • He is tasked with building a Paris-based team focused on AI research at Microsoft
  • H Company was one of the prominent European agentic AI startups, and Sifre's departure is a significant talent loss
  • The move underscores the ongoing gravitational pull of US tech giants on European AI talent, even as Europe invests in sovereignty
  • Sifre previously worked at DeepMind before founding H Company, continuing the DeepMind-to-startup-to-Big-Tech pipeline
