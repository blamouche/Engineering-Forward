What the Past Year Taught Meta

2026-09-03 Nikhyl Singhal (Lenny's Newsletter) Meta, AI transformation, product management, senior IC, data science, design, Facebook, hiring, organizational structure, Jagjit Chawla
Read the source article

Quick summary

A conversation with Meta VP Jagjit Chawla reveals how Meta's AI transformation has actually played out over the past year: smaller, flatter orgs are building more, the senior IC track became real, expert functions evolved rather than died, and Facebook is experimenting faster and bigger than before.

Key takeaways

  • Meta's smaller, flatter organization is building more, not less — with less process and more time spent on the product
  • The senior IC track became a credible path to the top without managing anyone
  • Expert functions like data science and design didn't die — they shed routine work and kept precision work
  • Facebook is experimenting faster and bigger, with purpose-built apps for sellers, group admins, and creators that had been failing the prioritization bar for a decade
  • The same shift PMs went through is now happening to data science and design roles
  • Evals have become a core PM skill, not just an AI skill
  • It's a "golden time to be a senior IC" according to the conversation
