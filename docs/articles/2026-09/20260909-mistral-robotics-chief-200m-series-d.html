Mistral's Robotics Chief Seeks €200M as AI Giant Confirms €3B Series D

2026-09-09 Sifted Daily (Freya Pratty) Mistral, Series D, robotics, €3bn round, €21bn valuation, European AI, funding
Read the source article

Quick summary

French AI champion Mistral confirmed its €3bn Series D at a €21bn valuation, and its robotics division chief is now seeking an additional €200m to build physical AI agents — signaling that Europe's leading LLM maker is expanding beyond software into embodied AI.

Key takeaways

  • Mistral confirmed a €3bn Series D round at a €21bn valuation, making it one of Europe's most valuable AI companies
  • The robotics division head is separately seeking €200m to fund Mistral's push into physical AI agents and embodied intelligence
  • The round had been scooped by Sifted a week early, with Samsung, NVIDIA, and ASML reported as backers of the broader €3bn round
  • Mistral's expansion into robotics follows a pattern of frontier AI labs diversifying beyond language models into physical-world applications
  • The move positions Mistral as a direct competitor to Figure, 1X, and other robotics-AI hybrid companies, but with the advantage of its own foundation models
