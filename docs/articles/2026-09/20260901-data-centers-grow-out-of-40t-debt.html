Scott Bessent Says We Will Grow Out of Our $40T Debt. Data Centers Are the Only Way.

2026-09-01 Harry and Anthony (CO/AI Signal/Noise) data centers, Scott Bessent, US debt, AI infrastructure, $1.1 trillion buildout, fiber boom, firm power, nuclear, trades jobs, Trump, reindustrialization
Read the source article

Quick summary

The $1.1 trillion US data-center buildout — roughly 604 projects, half the planet's facilities on US soil — is the only plausible engine for growing out of $40 trillion in national debt, paralleling the 1999 fiber boom that paved twenty-five years of growth, but the binding constraint isn't chips, it's firm power: ~90 gigawatts short by 2030.

Key takeaways

  • $1.1 trillion in US data-center construction is already in the pipeline — roughly 604 projects, about half the planet's facilities on US soil
  • Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent says the US can "grow its way out" of $40T debt — this build is the only plausible engine, requiring ~4.3% growth vs CBO's 1.8% projection
  • The 1999 fiber boom parallels: builders went bankrupt, the market got torched, but the glass was lit years later and became the rail under 25 years of growth
  • The binding constraint is firm power — the US is ~90 gigawatts (about 100 power plants) short by 2030 — not chips or GPUs
  • Trump went all-in on the buildout on Truth Social, promising "far lower taxes and jobs" and warning that opposing data centers means ending up "backwards and poor" — splitting his own base
  • $31M in campaign ads mention data centers this year; 99% of the spend is against them; 70% of voters oppose having one nearby
  • AWS will add 2 million more Nvidia GPUs across 2027-28; Nvidia's supply commitments doubled to $279B in a quarter — the ad war doesn't slow the concrete
  • The labor story: manufacturing jobs aren't returning to assembly lines — they're construction, electrical, plumbing, and crews that run these buildings. "You can't download a gigawatt and you can't download an electrician."
