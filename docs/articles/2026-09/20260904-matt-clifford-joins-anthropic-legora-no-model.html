Matt Clifford Joins Anthropic; Legora Explains Why It Won't Build Its Own AI Model

2026-09-04 Tom Nugent (Sifted Daily) Anthropic, Matt Clifford, Entrepreneurs First, Legora, legaltech, AI models, Revolut, US banking licence, UK spinouts
Read the source article

Quick summary

Matt Clifford, cofounder of accelerator Entrepreneurs First and former AI adviser to the UK prime minister, announced he is joining Anthropic — drawing criticism from parts of the tech world. Meanwhile, Swedish legaltech Legora explains why training a frontier model doesn't make business sense, and Revolut clears its first hurdle toward a US banking licence.

Key takeaways

  • Matt Clifford, who cofounded Entrepreneurs First before becoming the UK government's AI adviser to the PM, is joining Anthropic — a move that drew criticism as a revolving-door conflict of interest
  • Legora, the Swedish legaltech that raised $550m in March, says it won't build its own AI model because training a frontier model "doesn't make business sense" — contrasting with rival Harvey which has launched one
  • Legora has made five acquisitions since January and switched some tiers to consumption-based pricing, which its engineering team prefers
  • Revolut received conditional approval from the US Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) for its US banking licence
  • The UK government ruled out a proposed spinout exit tax, relieving investors who feared a "double tax" on academic founders
