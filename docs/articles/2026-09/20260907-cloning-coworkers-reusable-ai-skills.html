The Case for Cloning Your Coworkers: Capturing Judgment as Reusable AI Skills

2026-08-30 Laura Entis AI skills, cloning coworkers, reusable skills, judgment capture, Every, agent skills, organizational knowledge
Read the source article

Quick summary

Companies are starting to "clone" their best coworkers — capturing the judgment of key people as reusable AI skills — and Every is running this experiment on its own team to turn individual expertise into shared infrastructure.

Key takeaways

  • Companies are beginning to capture the judgment of their best performers as reusable AI skills
  • Every is running this experiment on its own team, turning individual expertise into shared infrastructure
  • The concept: rather than losing institutional knowledge when someone leaves, their judgment persists as an AI skill
  • This extends the "skills" paradigm from tool automation to expertise capture
  • The approach raises questions about consent, ownership, and what it means to "clone" a colleague's professional judgment
