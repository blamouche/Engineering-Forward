AI Unmasked Our Work as Scaffolding

March 27, 2026 Daniel Miessler knowledge work, scaffolding, AI disruption, cybersecurity, consulting, software development, commoditization
Read the source article

Quick summary

AI is revealing that 75-99% of knowledge work consists of scaffolding overhead — process maintenance rather than genuine expertise — and that AI's ability to handle scaffolding will fundamentally restructure professional services.

Key takeaways

  • 75-99% of knowledge work across domains is scaffolding: context-building, tooling maintenance, report formatting, process overhead
  • In cybersecurity, 99% of testing time is not finding new vulnerabilities but context-building and workflow management
  • In software development, most time is peripheral infrastructure work rather than actual coding
  • High-end consulting primarily applies templated solutions with only 2-12% situation-specific insights
  • AI crushes scaffolding work by packaging context, methodologies, and knowledge bases into reproducible systems
