What Do AI-Based Layoffs Say About TAM?

2026-03-16 Elliot Smith AI layoffs, TAM, growth ceiling, SaaS, WiseTech, Block, Atlassian, efficiency, valuation
Read the source article

Quick summary

Elliot Smith argues that AI-driven efficiency layoffs at SaaS companies like WiseTech, Block, and Atlassian inadvertently expose growth ceilings that contradict the high-revenue multiples their valuations depend on.

Key takeaways

  • Companies claiming 2x efficiency gains from AI should theoretically be able to double output—but if that doesn't increase revenue proportionally, they've revealed a growth ceiling
  • WiseTech, Block, and Atlassian are cutting headcount while citing AI productivity improvements
  • If AI doubled efficiency but revenue doesn't double, the extra efficiency has no market to absorb it
  • This tension contradicts the high-growth tech valuations premised on substantial future expansion
  • Upcoming financial results will reveal whether efficiency gains translate to sustained business improvement or mask growth constraints
