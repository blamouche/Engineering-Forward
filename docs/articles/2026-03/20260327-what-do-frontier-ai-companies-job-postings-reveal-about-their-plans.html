What do frontier AI companies' job postings reveal about their plans?

March 27, 2026 Epoch AI hiring signals, AI labs, go-to-market, hardware, robotics
Read the source article

Quick summary

By analyzing job postings at OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, and DeepMind, Epoch AI infers shifting priorities: heavier go-to-market hiring, new hardware and robotics bets, and divergent strategies for securing compute and data.

Key takeaways

  • Sales and deployment roles now dominate open listings, signaling a push to help customers adopt AI products.
  • OpenAI and DeepMind postings suggest bets on hardware devices and robotics, while Anthropic focuses on core product improvements.
  • Job ads reveal differences in how labs pursue compute, including OpenAI’s custom silicon versus Anthropic’s datacenter partnerships.
  • Hiring patterns point to geographic sales focus in the U.S., with growing investment in Europe and Asia-Pacific.
  • Job postings are an imperfect but valuable lens into product roadmaps and input constraints.
