Instagram’s Cofounder on Why Great Products Are Still Hard to Build

March 25, 2026 Laura Entis product development, AI agents, Anthropic, vibe coding, technical skills
Read the source article

Quick summary

A conversation with Instagram cofounder Mike Krieger argues that agent‑native products demand tighter tradeoffs and faster iteration, while Every’s team debates how persistence and technical depth shape reliable AI-built software.

Key takeaways

  • Krieger says agent‑native products must balance freedom and reliability, since users push tools beyond original intent.
  • AI shrinks build cycles from months to hours, making it easier to ship incoherent products without enough feedback.
  • Anthropic Labs runs tiny product teams to move fast and avoid coordination drag.
  • Every argues persistence and clear questioning can substitute for some technical skills in early prototypes.
  • Reliable production systems still require technical depth to prevent failures when vibe‑coded prototypes hit real users.
