OpenAI's data center pivot underscores Wall Street spending concerns ahead of IPO

March 22, 2026 OpenAI, data centers, IPO, compute spend, Stargate, infrastructure
Read the source article

Quick summary

OpenAI is dialing back its ambitions to build massive data centers and instead leaning on partners for compute, signaling a shift toward fiscal discipline ahead of a potential IPO.

Key takeaways

  • OpenAI is pivoting from owning data centers to purchasing large-scale cloud capacity from partners.
  • Wall Street skepticism about massive infrastructure commitments is pushing a more disciplined spending narrative.
  • The Stargate project illustrates the complexity and timelines of building gigawatt-scale facilities.
  • OpenAI’s compute needs remain enormous, but the company is reframing them to align with revenue growth.
  • Competitors like Anthropic, Google, and Meta are racing for the same scarce compute resources.
