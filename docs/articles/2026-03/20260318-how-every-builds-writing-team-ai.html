How Every Builds a Writing Team in the Age of AI

2026-03-18 Dan Shipper (host), Kate Lee (guest) Every.to, editorial, AI writing, Kate Lee, Dan Shipper, publishing, copy editing, recruitment
Read the source article

Quick summary

Every's Editor-in-Chief Kate Lee explains how she's integrating AI into editorial workflows—from screening hundreds of job applications to maintaining editorial standards and managing rapid AI model review publishing.

Key takeaways

  • AI processes hundreds of job applications for editorial roles—handling the high-volume screening that previously consumed significant editor time
  • AI maintains editorial standards and develops editorial judgment consistency across a distributed writing team
  • Every published two major AI model release reviews within 24 hours using AI-accelerated workflows
  • Copy editing bottlenecks remain a challenge for automation—human judgment still required at the final quality gate
  • Lee's journey from literary agent to tech editorial leader (Medium, WeWork, Stripe Press, Every) provides perspective on publishing transformation
