Warner, Rounds Unveil Bipartisan Plan to Prepare American Workers for AI-Driven Workforce Changes

Unknown AI policy, workforce, legislation, commission, labor economics
Read the source article

Quick summary

Senators Warner and Rounds introduced the Economy of the Future Commission Act to study AI’s labor impacts and recommend bipartisan workforce policies within a year.

Key takeaways

  • The bill creates a bipartisan commission focused on AI‑driven workforce changes.
  • Interim and final reports would outline expected job impacts and policy options.
  • Recommendations cover training, reskilling, unemployment insurance, and taxation.
  • The commission includes lawmakers and experts from industry, academia, and government.
  • Broad endorsements signal a push for coordinated national preparedness.
