Labor market impacts of AI: A new measure and early evidence

March 08, 2026 labor market, AI exposure, Anthropic Economic Index, employment, automation
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic proposes a new “observed exposure” metric that blends theoretical task capability with real usage data, finding that AI adoption still lags potential but is already correlated with lower projected job growth in more exposed occupations.

Key takeaways

  • The paper introduces “observed exposure,” weighting real‑world AI usage and automation over pure theoretical capability.
  • Actual AI usage covers only a fraction of tasks that could be automated, signaling a large adoption gap.
  • Occupations with higher observed exposure are projected to grow more slowly through 2034.
  • Highly exposed workers skew older, more female, more educated, and higher paid.
  • There is no clear rise in unemployment yet, but hiring for younger workers in exposed roles appears to be slowing.
