China Is Mobilizing Thousands of One-Person AI Startups

March 23, 2026 China, one-person companies, AI startups, incentives
Read the source article

Quick summary

Chinese cities are subsidizing “one‑person companies” by offering housing, office space, and compute to spur AI‑driven solopreneurship.

Key takeaways

  • Local governments are competing to attract one‑person AI startups.
  • Incentives include free apartments, office space, and discounted compute.
  • Policies aim to expand AI adoption across the economy.
  • Some initiatives repurpose underused data centers and office buildings.
  • The long‑term viability of these solo startups remains uncertain.
