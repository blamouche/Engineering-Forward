What 81,000 People Want from AI

2026-03-18 Saffron Huang et al. user research, AI aspirations, 81k interviews, multilingual study, productivity, displacement fears, economic security, global AI adoption
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic's study of 80,508 Claude users across 159 countries is the largest multilingual qualitative AI study ever—revealing that professional excellence (18.8%) and personal transformation (13.7%) are users' top aspirations, while hallucinations (26.7%) and economic displacement (22.3%) are their primary concerns.

Key takeaways

  • Top aspirations: Professional Excellence (18.8%), Personal Transformation (13.7%), Life Management (13.5%), Time Freedom (11.1%), Financial Independence (9.7%).
  • Top concerns: Unreliability/hallucinations (26.7%), Economic displacement (22.3%), Loss of autonomy (21.9%), Cognitive decline from over-reliance (16.3%).
  • "Light and shade" tensions: the same AI capabilities produce both benefits and harms simultaneously—people appreciate AI's availability while fearing dependency.
  • Benefits are grounded in experience (32% report actual productivity gains); systemic harms remain largely speculative.
  • Regional variation: lower/middle-income countries view AI more optimistically than wealthy nations, particularly for entrepreneurship and learning.
