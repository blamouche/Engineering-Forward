From IDEs to AI Agents with Steve Yegge

2026-03-11 Gergely Orosz AI agents, software engineering, vibe coding, IDE evolution, developer productivity, AI adoption levels
Read the source article

Quick summary

Steve Yegge explores how artificial intelligence is fundamentally transforming software development from manual coding toward agent-based orchestration, reshaping what engineers need to know and how they work.

Key takeaways

  • Rapid prototyping replaces traditional development cycles: Teams now build multiple implementations quickly and deploy the best one, with Claude Cowork reportedly launching in 10 days.
  • IDEs are becoming conversational interfaces: Future development tools will focus on managing AI agent conversations and monitoring rather than traditional code editing.
  • Reading ability is becoming a productivity bottleneck: As AI generates verbose outputs, visual interfaces and spoken interactions will likely replace text-heavy terminal displays.
  • AI adoption exists on a spectrum: Most engineers operate at lower levels (requesting suggestions, reviewing output), while advanced practitioners orchestrate multiple agents in parallel.
  • Monolithic codebases limit AI effectiveness: AI agents struggle with codebases exceeding a few million lines, creating infrastructure challenges for enterprises.
