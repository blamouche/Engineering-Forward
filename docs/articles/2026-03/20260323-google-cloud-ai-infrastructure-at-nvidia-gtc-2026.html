Google Cloud AI Infrastructure at NVIDIA GTC 2026

March 23, 2026 Google Cloud Google Cloud, NVIDIA, GTC 2026, G4 VMs, AI infrastructure
Read the source article

Quick summary

Google Cloud and NVIDIA announced expanded AI infrastructure collaboration, including G4 VMs, fractional GPUs, and upcoming Vera Rubin systems.

Key takeaways

  • Google Cloud introduced G4 VMs powered by NVIDIA RTX Pro 6000 Blackwell GPUs.
  • Fractional G4 VMs provide smaller GPU slices for cost‑efficient workloads.
  • NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 systems are planned for late 2026.
  • Integrations include Dynamo with GKE Inference Gateway and Vertex AI support.
  • A Kaggle competition and public‑sector accelerator were also announced.
