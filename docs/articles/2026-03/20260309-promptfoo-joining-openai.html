Promptfoo is Joining OpenAI

2026-03-09 Ian Webster, Michael D'Angelo Promptfoo acquisition, OpenAI acquisition, AI security, AI evals, open source AI security
Read the source article

Quick summary

Promptfoo, a widely-adopted open-source AI security and evaluation platform with 350,000+ developers, has been acquired by OpenAI to accelerate its mission of helping teams build and deploy secure AI systems.

Key takeaways

  • Acquisition Announcement: Promptfoo agreed to be acquired by OpenAI while maintaining its open-source status and continuing service to users and customers.
  • Significant Market Traction: The platform achieved remarkable adoption with 350,000+ developers, 130,000 monthly active users, and deployment across 25%+ of Fortune 500 companies.
  • Open-Source Commitment: The company will "continue to maintain the open-source suite as a best-in-class red teaming, static scanning, and evals tool" supporting diverse AI providers.
  • Integration Strategy: OpenAI plans to integrate Promptfoo's technology into model and infrastructure layers, enabling earlier vulnerability detection in the development pipeline.
  • Resource Enhancement: The acquisition provides access to OpenAI's research capabilities at the model and inference levels to strengthen AI security and compliance efforts.
