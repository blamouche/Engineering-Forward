The 10 traits of great PMs, how AI will impact your product, and Slack’s product development process

Quick summary

An interview with Slack’s CPO Noah Weiss covers what distinguishes great product managers, how Slack builds product culture, and how the company is approaching AI adoption within product teams.

Key takeaways

  • Strong PMs blend customer empathy with strategic judgment rather than simply mirroring user demands.
  • Slack’s product culture emphasizes clear principles, prioritization discipline, and rituals that reinforce quality.
  • “Complaint storms” are used to surface user pain and build empathy across teams.
  • AI/ML is treated as a pragmatic tool, embedded where it meaningfully improves user outcomes.
  • Senior PM growth requires better communication, data fluency, and judgment under ambiguity.
