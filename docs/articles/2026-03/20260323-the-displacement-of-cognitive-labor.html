The Displacement of Cognitive Labor and What Comes After

March 23, 2026 Sahaj Garg cognitive labor, automation, AI agents, labor markets
Read the source article

Quick summary

An essay arguing that cognitive labor automation is already here and that the remaining bottleneck is organizational scaffolding, not model capability.

Key takeaways

  • The author claims AI already exceeds humans in many cognitive domains.
  • Productivity gains can compress weeks of work into hours with agent tools.
  • The next bottleneck is organizational structure and deployment, not intelligence.
  • Agent orchestration and computer‑use tools are seen as key accelerants.
  • Physical labor automation may follow faster once cognitive R&D is automated.
