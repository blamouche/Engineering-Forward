Rethinking Open Source Mentorship in the AI Era

March 19, 2026 Abigail Cabunoc Mayes open source, mentorship, AI contributions, maintainers
Read the source article

Quick summary

GitHub proposes a “3 Cs” framework to help maintainers mentor effectively amid rising AI‑generated contributions.

Key takeaways

  • AI makes contribution quality signals harder to interpret, raising review costs.
  • Mentorship remains essential but must be more selective to avoid burnout.
  • The “3 Cs” framework: Comprehension, Context, Continuity.
  • AI disclosure and AGENTS.md can improve reviewer context.
  • Strategic mentorship protects the “multiplier effect” in open source communities.
