How to Make Sense of AI

2026-03-18 Cedric Chin AI business strategy, uncertainty, adaptation, field reports, framework, hype navigation, Commoncog
Read the source article

Quick summary

During AI's rapid change, ignore opinions and forecasts—consume only detailed field reports of actual AI use cases, then evaluate them against four questions about outcomes, actions, context-specific value, and causal relationships.

Key takeaways

  • The core problem: during technological upheaval, fear-driven content proliferates to capture attention, creating distraction rather than clarity.
  • The proposed method: ignore all opinions and forecasts; consume only detailed field reports of actual AI use cases.
  • Four questions to evaluate each field report: What new outcomes are possible? What actions can we take? What's the relative value to our specific context? What causal relationships exist?
  • Core insight: "When a new technology emerges, impacts are uneven and ultimately unpredictable"—context-specific observation beats broad analysis.
  • Effective businesspeople don't predict futures—they adapt faster than competitors by observing present capabilities clearly.
