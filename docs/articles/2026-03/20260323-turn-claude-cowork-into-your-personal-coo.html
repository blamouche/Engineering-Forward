Turn Claude Cowork Into Your Personal COO

March 23, 2026 Linas Claude Cowork, agents, workflows, automation, operations
Read the source article

Quick summary

A guide to using Claude Cowork as an autonomous “COO,” emphasizing outcome‑based delegation, file‑based context, and agentic workflows.

Key takeaways

  • Cowork is positioned as an agentic AI that executes workflows, not just a chat tool.
  • Delegation works best when describing outcomes rather than steps.
  • File‑based context (about‑me, brand voice, preferences) boosts quality.
  • Cowork supports sub‑agents, plugins, and MCP connectors for parallel work.
  • Structured prompts and guardrails reduce errors and improve autonomy.
