The internet ruined customer service. AI could save it.

2026-03-18 Sarah Wang (a16z) customer service, AI agents, concierge business, scale businesses, Decagon, NPS, deflection rate, personalization
Read the source article

Quick summary

The internet scaled commerce but degraded customer experience by eliminating personalization—AI collapses the cost of devoted attention, turning every business into a potential concierge business with 80% deflection rates and improved satisfaction scores.

Key takeaways

  • Scale businesses (Amazon, Uber, banks) offer cheap but impersonal service; concierge businesses (luxury brands) provide personalized service exclusively to high-value customers. The internet forced most businesses toward scale at the cost of experience.
  • "When AI collapses the cost of high-quality attention, every business becomes a concierge business."
  • Decagon achieves 80% deflection rates and doubled Net Promoter Scores for clients like Chime—demonstrating automation enhancing rather than degrading satisfaction.
  • AI agents can provide proactive, personalized, continuous service rather than episodic reactive support.
  • Historical parallel: email created new communication categories rather than just replacing mail—AI will generate unprecedented customer engagement models.
