OpenAI ads pilot tops $100 million in annualized revenue in under 2 months

2026-03-26 OpenAI, ChatGPT, advertising, revenue, product strategy
Read the source article

Quick summary

OpenAI’s early ChatGPT ads pilot surpassed $100M in annualized revenue within two months, signaling a fast‑growing monetization channel despite cautious rollout.

Key takeaways

  • The U.S. ads pilot for ChatGPT has crossed $100M in ARR.
  • OpenAI is working with 600+ advertisers and reports stable privacy‑trust metrics.
  • Ads are labeled, placed at the bottom of responses, and restricted for minors.
  • Eligibility is broad, but daily ad exposure remains limited during testing.
  • Expansion tests are being explored in Canada, Australia, and New Zealand.
