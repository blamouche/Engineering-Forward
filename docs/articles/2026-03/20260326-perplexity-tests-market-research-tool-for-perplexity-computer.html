Perplexity tests Market Research tool for Perplexity Computer

Unknown Perplexity, market research, agentic search, enterprise AI, data sources
Read the source article

Quick summary

Perplexity is testing a Market Research vertical powered by its agentic “Computer” system, aiming to automate consultant-style analyses using premium data sources.

Key takeaways

  • The Market Research tab appears alongside Finance, Travel, and Shopping in Perplexity’s UI.
  • Queries are routed to the agentic “Computer” system rather than standard search.
  • Built-in workflows cover TAM analysis, competitive landscapes, growth frameworks, and business model tooling.
  • The feature is reportedly powered by premium datasets like CB Insights, PitchBook, and Statista.
  • The move aligns with Perplexity’s enterprise push and subscription-first strategy.
