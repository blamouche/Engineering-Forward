2026-03-08 Claire Vo Midjourney, AI image generation, brand consistency, mood boards, style references, personalization codes
Read the source article

Quick summary

An AI creative director shares a systematic, repeatable workflow for generating cohesive brand assets using mood boards, style references, and personalization codes rather than complex prompts.

Key takeaways

  • Visual Communication Over Text: "A picture is worth a thousand words—and for an image model, that's literally true." Leverage mood boards and visual references instead of lengthy prompts.
  • Style References (SREFs) Enable Precision: Individual style references from curated mood boards produce better results than feeding entire collections, allowing for iterative refinement.
  • Personalization Codes Embed Taste: Training custom style preferences through rating exercises creates reusable codes that apply consistent aesthetic sensibilities across multiple generations.
  • Strategic Simplification in Prompts: Effective prompting uses "cheat codes" like publication names (Vogue, Dazed), camera models (Sony RX100), and plain language rather than technical specifications.
  • Refinement Tools Enhance Output: Secondary tools like Nano Banana enable selective image modification—replacing specific elements while preserving context—and upscaling without destroying original composition.
