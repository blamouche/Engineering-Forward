Broad Timelines

March 19, 2026 Toby Ord AI timelines, uncertainty, forecasting, transformative AI
Read the source article

Quick summary

Toby Ord argues that the right stance on AI timelines is not “short” or “long” but a broad distribution reflecting deep expert disagreement.

Key takeaways

  • AI timeline estimates are highly uncertain and definitions are fuzzy.
  • Expert disagreement implies we should hold a wide probability distribution.
  • “Broad timelines” is proposed as the correct epistemic stance.
  • Planning should account for both short and long possibilities simultaneously.
  • The essay reframes timeline debates from winner‑picking to uncertainty management.
