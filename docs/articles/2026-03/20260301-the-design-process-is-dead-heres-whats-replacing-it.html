The design process is dead. Here's what's replacing it.

March 1, 2026 Lenny Rachitsky design, product development, prototyping, collaboration, AI tools
Read the source article

Quick summary

Traditional design handoffs are being replaced by continuous, cross-functional prototyping loops where designers and engineers co-own product shaping, with AI tools compressing iteration cycles and forcing new collaboration patterns.

Key takeaways

  • Linear design phases are giving way to rapid, shared prototyping that merges design and engineering work.
  • Designers are expected to be fluent in technical constraints and to think in systems, not just screens.
  • AI tooling makes exploration cheap, shifting the hardest work to framing problems and making trade-offs.
  • Shared artifacts, not handoffs, are becoming the primary coordination mechanism across teams.
  • Organizations need to update roles, metrics, and decision rights to match this new workflow.
