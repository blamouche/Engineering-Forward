AI Style Guides: How to Help AI Write Like You

2026-03-17 Katie Parrott and Claude AI writing, style guide, Claude, voice, tone, prompt engineering, content creation, Every.to
Read the source article

Quick summary

Every.to's comprehensive guide to creating AI style guides argues that specificity about voice, structure, and anti-patterns—not generic tone descriptions—is what transforms AI output from generic to authentically personal.

Key takeaways

  • Without a style guide, AI converges toward "average approximation of good writing"—competent but impersonal
  • An AI style guide is an "operating manual" that makes instinctive writing knowledge legible to machines
  • Effective guides include voice/tone descriptions, structural templates, sentence-level preferences, signature moves, anti-patterns with examples, and revision checklists
  • Three implementation levels: Starter (20 minutes), Working (daily-use Claude Project), Compound (automated workflow integration)
  • Build the guide by having AI interview you about reaction to specific writing samples rather than self-describing abstractly
