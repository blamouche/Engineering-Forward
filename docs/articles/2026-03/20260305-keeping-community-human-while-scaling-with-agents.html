Quick summary

A practical guide to using agents to scale community operations without eroding trust or human tone.

Key takeaways

  • Agents can absorb repetitive moderation and routing tasks effectively.
  • Human oversight is essential for ambiguous, high-context interactions.
  • Clear policy boundaries improve consistency across human and AI actions.
  • Community health metrics should include qualitative trust signals.
  • The best model is augmentation: agents for speed, humans for judgment.
