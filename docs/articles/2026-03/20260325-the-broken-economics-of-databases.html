The Broken Economics of Databases

March 25, 2026 databases, economics, gross margin, cloud, competition
Read the source article

Quick summary

A deep dive into why database companies struggle to capture value: high gross margins coexist with weak operating margins because cloud providers own key complements and competition erodes pricing power.

Key takeaways

  • Database vendors show high gross margins but weak or negative operating margins.
  • The market sits between monopoly and perfect competition, compressing pricing power.
  • Innovation yields temporary rents but requires ongoing, costly R&D.
  • Cloud providers control critical complementary assets, weakening vendor leverage.
  • Vendors drift toward defensibility tactics as differentiation erodes.
