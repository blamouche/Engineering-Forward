OpenAI is throwing everything into building a fully automated researcher

March 20, 2026 AI agents, automated research, reasoning models, OpenAI, scientific discovery
Read the source article

Quick summary

OpenAI is reorienting its R&D toward an “AI researcher,” starting with a near‑term autonomous research intern and aiming for a multi‑agent research system by 2028, betting that improved reasoning models and long‑running agents can tackle complex scientific and business problems while raising new reliability and safety questions.

Key takeaways

  • OpenAI’s new “North Star” is a fully automated research system built from agents, reasoning models, and interpretability work.
  • The roadmap targets an autonomous research intern by September and a larger multi‑agent researcher by 2028.
  • Codex is framed as an early prototype for long‑running research agents, with broader domains beyond coding as the goal.
  • OpenAI expects capability gains (reasoning, longer horizons, task decomposition) to unlock sustained autonomous work.
  • The vision faces reliability and safety challenges, including compounding error rates and misuse risks.
