The most important question nobody's asking about AI

2026-03-11 Dwarkesh Patel Anthropic, Department of War, AI governance, mass surveillance, autonomous weapons, AI alignment, regulation, AI safety, government contracts
Read the source article

Quick summary

The DOW's designation of Anthropic as a supply chain risk for refusing to remove safeguards against mass surveillance and autonomous weapons reveals the most important unresolved question in AI: to whom should AI systems be aligned?

Key takeaways

  • The DOW designated Anthropic as a supply chain risk when the company refused to remove safeguards against mass surveillance and autonomous weapons use from its models.
  • The government should have simply declined to use Anthropic's models rather than using designation as coercion—the difference matters for the precedent it sets.
  • Structural vulnerability: as AI becomes more pervasive, tech companies will increasingly be unable to segregate AI for specific government uses, potentially facing forced choices between government contracts and their AI providers.
  • Mass surveillance risk: AI gives "everybody more leverage on whatever assets and authority they currently have"—including authoritarian and abusive uses by powerful actors.
  • Purpose-built AI regulation will be weaponized by power-seeking actors, making restrictive regulatory frameworks potentially more dangerous than beneficial.
  • The central question: "to whom should AI systems be aligned?" has not been adequately addressed by society, policy, or the AI industry.
