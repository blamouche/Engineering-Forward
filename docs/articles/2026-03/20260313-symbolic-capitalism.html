Neo Symbolic Capitalism

2026-03-13 Om Malik symbolic capital, Bourdieu, Elon Musk, Stripe, Twitter, tech media, reputation, attention economy
Read the source article

Quick summary

Om Malik applies Bourdieu's concept of symbolic capital to the tech industry, arguing that reputation and prestige have become more valuable than financial capital in an attention economy where social media rewards what moves over what is true.

Key takeaways

  • Symbolic capital—reputation and prestige—now converts more efficiently than financial capital for fundraising, talent, and thought leadership in tech
  • Elon Musk's Twitter/X acquisition exemplifies the deliberate construction of a "perpetual machine for symbolic capital"
  • Stripe's Collison brothers built symbolic capital through Stripe Press, podcasts, and strategic intellectual positioning before financial dominance
  • Media rewards speed and shareability over depth and accuracy: "the network does not reward what is true or what is deep. It rewards what moves."
  • Tech executives increasingly understand themselves as "players" in a symbolic capital game with X as the primary platform
