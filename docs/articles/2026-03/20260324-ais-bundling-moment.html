AI's Bundling Moment

2026-03-24 Tomasz Tunguz SaaS, AI platforms, bundling, enterprise software, go-to-market
Read the source article

Quick summary

As AI cycles accelerate, buyers favor trusted, integrated platforms over best‑of‑breed point solutions, pushing AI companies to rebundle workflows and expand into verticalized suites.

Key takeaways

  • SaaS winners historically unbundled single workflows, but AI shifts incentives toward bundling.
  • Rapid model iteration makes best‑of‑breed stacks hard for buyers to maintain.
  • AI vendors are expanding from point solutions into industry‑specific platforms.
  • Trust and workflow integration become the main purchasing criteria.
  • Broad adoption enables AI companies to capture more workflows as software costs fall.
