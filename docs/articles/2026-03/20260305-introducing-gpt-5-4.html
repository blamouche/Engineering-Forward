Introducing GPT‑5.4

March 05, 2026 Simon Willison OpenAI, GPT-5.4, models, pricing, context window
Read the source article

Quick summary

Simon Willison summarizes OpenAI’s GPT‑5.4 release, highlighting a million‑token context window, higher prices, and notable gains on coding and spreadsheet tasks.

Key takeaways

  • OpenAI released two API models: gpt‑5.4 and gpt‑5.4‑pro, also in ChatGPT and Codex CLI.
  • Both models have a 1 million token context window and an August 31, 2025 cutoff.
  • Pricing is slightly higher than GPT‑5.2, with extra costs above 272k tokens.
  • GPT‑5.4 beats GPT‑5.3‑Codex on coding benchmarks, suggesting convergence of model lines.
  • OpenAI emphasizes improvements in business workflows like spreadsheets and presentations.
