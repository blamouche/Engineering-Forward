Hey, You Should Probably Check Your Chatbot’s Privacy Settings

March 08, 2026 privacy, data collection, chatbots, AI training, consent
Read the source article

Quick summary

Most major chatbot providers still opt users into training by default, so unless you change settings, your conversations—potentially including sensitive data—can be reused for model training.

Key takeaways

  • Leading AI labs default to collecting chat conversations for training unless users opt out.
  • Settings are buried and framed as “help improve the model,” which obscures the real trade‑off.
  • Sensitive data in chats (medical, financial, personal) increases privacy risk if retained for training.
  • Researchers warn that retraining on AI‑generated content can cause model collapse, making user data especially valuable.
  • Users can disable training in ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini through specific settings menus.
