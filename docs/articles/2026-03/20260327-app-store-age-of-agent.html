App Store | Age of Agent

Unknown agents, discovery, payments, MCP, platform economics
Read the source article

Quick summary

The piece argues the App Store model will be unbundled in the agent era, with open connection protocols commoditizing integration while discovery and payments become the primary battlegrounds for value capture.

Key takeaways

  • MCP (Model Context Protocol) makes service connection a commodity, not a moat.
  • The App Store’s four functions (discovery, distribution, trust, payments) will separate for agents.
  • Discovery is the most strategic layer because agents decide and transact for users.
  • Payment aggregation could be lucrative but less monopolistic without Apple‑style lock‑in.
  • Platform power will hinge on ranking algorithms and preference data governance.
