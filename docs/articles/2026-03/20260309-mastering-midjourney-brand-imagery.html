Mastering Midjourney: How to create consistent, beautiful brand imagery without complex prompts

2026-03-09 Lenny Rachitsky (guest: Jamey Gannon) Midjourney, AI image generation, brand consistency, visual systems, style references, personalization codes, creative workflow
Read the source article

Quick summary

An AI creative director reveals her system for generating on-brand imagery through visual references and personalization codes rather than complex text prompts.

Key takeaways

  • Minimize text prompting: The most effective workflow avoids lengthy written descriptions in favor of visual communication through reference images and style codes.
  • Style references outperform mood boards: Using Midjourney's SREF feature provides more consistent results than traditional mood boards that can cause style "averaging."
  • Personalization codes encode aesthetic preferences: Systematically rating generated images creates unique codes that reliably deliver preferred visual styles across generations.
  • Use editorial and camera shorthand: Referencing known publications (e.g., "Dazed editorial") or camera models instantly communicates complex aesthetic qualities.
  • Remove problematic elements rather than fix them: When unwanted elements appear, deletion from reference sets proves more effective than additional prompting.
