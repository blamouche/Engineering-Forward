Mapped: The AI Labs Taking Over King's Cross

2026-07-03 Freya Pratty and Tom Nugent (Sifted) London AI, King's Cross, Ineffable Intelligence, Reflection AI, European AI labs, David Silver, venture capital, AI geography
Read the source article

Quick summary

London's King's Cross has become the epicenter of the city's AI boom, with a cluster of well-funded AI labs — including Ineffable Intelligence co-founded by DeepMind's David Silver — transforming the area into Europe's densest concentration of AI talent and capital.

Key takeaways

  • London brought in €15bn in funding in H1 2026, a 53% jump on the previous six months, with a significant chunk going to companies based near King's Cross.
  • Ineffable Intelligence, co-founded by David Silver (the DeepMind researcher behind AlphaGo and AlphaStar), raised $1.1bn in June at a $5.1bn valuation, working on reinforcement learning.
  • The area is home to a diverse set of AI labs working on everything from world models trained on the physical world to self-improving AI agents designed to discover new knowledge.
  • King's Cross already housed Google and Meta's UK offices, and now OpenAI has joined, creating a gravitational pull for AI talent and capital.
  • The cluster effect mirrors Silicon Valley's geographic density advantage — proximity enables talent flow, serendipitous encounters, and shared infrastructure that distributed ecosystems struggle to replicate.
