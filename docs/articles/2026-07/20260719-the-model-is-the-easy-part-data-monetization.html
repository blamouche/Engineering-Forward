The Model Is the Easy Part

2026-07-19 Every Staff (Alex Duffy) AI commoditization, data monetization, model evaluation, enterprise AI, Good Start Labs, frontier models
Read the source article

Quick summary

As AI models commoditize, the real challenge shifts from building a model to defining what "good" means for your business—and the companies that can measure and monetize their domain data will capture disproportionate value in the next phase of AI adoption.

Key takeaways

  • Arkadium's Game Lab benchmark revealed that frontier models lose 90% of Gin Rummy games against casual players—models are shaped by what they've seen, and popular AI models may not have experience with whatever you work on.
  • An expert model with 4.6 million parameters in an 18MB file beat human players 90% of the time at 1M requests/day for about $60/year—versus multi-millions for a frontier LLM approach.
  • Reddit, Shutterstock, and News Corp have already turned their data into hundreds of millions in recurring revenue by selling to frontier labs.
  • Anthropic asked big pharma for their data, and nearly everyone said no—companies whose data is their product must protect their IP from labs that might become competitors.
  • Defining and measuring "good" is emerging as the next stage of AI adoption; companies that can set clear goals, build benchmarks, and measure progress will be the ones that capture value.
