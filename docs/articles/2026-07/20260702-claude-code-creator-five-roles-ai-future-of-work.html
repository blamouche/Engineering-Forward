The Claude Code Creator Just Mapped Out How AI Will Rewrite the Future of Work

2026-07-02 Linas Beliūnas Boris Cherny, Claude Code, Anthropic, AI future of work, job titles melting, Prototyper, Builder, Sweeper, Grower, Maintainer, AI-native roles
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic's Head of Claude Code Boris Cherny argues that traditional job titles are "melting" into five new archetypes—Prototyper, Builder, Sweeper, Grower, Maintainer—that cut across every function and shift over the life of a product in an AI-native world.

Key takeaways

  • Boris Cherny, Head of Claude Code at Anthropic, has not personally written a line of code in more than six months
  • Traditional job titles (engineer, PM, designer, analyst) are becoming lagging indicators of how work actually gets done in an AI-native world
  • Five new archetypes replace traditional titles: Prototyper (rapid exploration), Builder (production implementation), Sweeper (cleanup and verification), Grower (scaling and optimization), Maintainer (ongoing operations)
  • These roles cut across every function and shift over the life of a product, meaning the same person may play different roles at different stages
  • The framework is stress-tested against productivity studies, labor-market signals, and corporate memos from Klarna, Shopify, Meta, and Anthropic itself
