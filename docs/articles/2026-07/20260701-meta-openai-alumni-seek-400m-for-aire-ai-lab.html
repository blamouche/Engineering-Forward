Exclusive: Meta and OpenAI Alumni Seek $400m for New AI Lab Aire

2026-06-30 Daphné Leprince-Ringuet aire, meta, openai, fundraise, ai-lab, startup, $400m
Read the source article

Quick summary

A trio of Meta and OpenAI alumni are seeking $400 million for a new AI startup dubbed Aire, based between the US, France, and Israel, reflecting the ongoing talent exodus from frontier AI labs.

Key takeaways

  • Three former employees of Meta and OpenAI are seeking $400m for a new AI startup called Aire.
  • The company will operate across three countries: the US, France, and Israel.
  • The fundraise reflects the continuing trend of top AI talent leaving frontier labs to start their own ventures.
  • The multi-country structure (US/France/Israel) suggests Aire may be positioning itself to navigate different regulatory environments and access diverse talent pools.
  • This comes amid a broader wave of AI lab spinouts, with ex-founders and researchers from major labs raising hundreds of millions to build competing organizations.
