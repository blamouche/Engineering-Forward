Learning to Replicate Expert Judgment in Financial Tasks

2026-07-02 Thinking Machines financial AI, expert judgment, fine-tuning, proprietary datasets, frontier models, differentiated intelligence, custom models
Read the source article

Quick summary

Frontier models struggle with relatively simple financial tasks, but custom models fine-tuned on high-quality proprietary datasets labeled by expert investors perform better and are substantially cheaper, signaling a future of differentiated intelligence.

Key takeaways

  • Frontier models struggle with relatively simple financial tasks, revealing gaps in domain-specific reasoning
  • Custom models fine-tuned on high-quality proprietary datasets labeled by expert investors perform better than frontier models on financial tasks
  • Custom models are also substantially cheaper to operate than frontier models for domain-specific work
  • Models of the future will likely feature "differentiated intelligence"—organizations will have custom models tuned to their specific needs that outperform frontier models in their domain
  • The key bottleneck is access to high-quality, expert-labeled proprietary data, not model architecture
