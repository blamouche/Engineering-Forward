Meta Watermelon Matches GPT-5.5 Benchmarks; Anthropic Explores Samsung Chip Partnership

2026-07-03 TLDR AI / Dan Meta, Watermelon, GPT-5.5, benchmarks, Anthropic, Samsung, chips, AI models, compute
Read the source article

Quick summary

Meta's upcoming "Watermelon" model reportedly matches GPT-5.5 on key benchmarks, while Anthropic explores a Samsung chip partnership to diversify its compute stack beyond Google, Amazon, and Nvidia.

Key takeaways

  • Meta's superintelligence chief Alexandr Wang claims the upcoming "Watermelon" model has caught up with OpenAI's GPT-5.5 on closely followed AI benchmarks, though it's still in training
  • Watermelon reportedly uses an order of magnitude more compute than Muse Spark, Meta's previous flagship model, signaling an aggressive compute scaling strategy
  • Anthropic is exploring a custom AI chip collaboration with Samsung, looking to diversify its compute stack beyond existing partnerships with Google, Amazon, and Nvidia
  • Anthropic stated that chips from Google, Amazon, and Nvidia remain central to its hardware strategy—suggesting the Samsung partnership would be additive rather than a replacement
  • Meta has not given a timeline for Watermelon's release, keeping the competitive pressure on without committing to a specific date
