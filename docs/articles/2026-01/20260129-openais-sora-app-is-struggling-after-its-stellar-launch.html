OpenAI's Sora app is struggling after its stellar launch

January 29, 2026 Sarah Perez OpenAI, Sora, AI video, mobile apps, consumer adoption
Read the source article

Quick summary

After an explosive debut, OpenAI's Sora video app is seeing declining downloads and spending, highlighting the challenge of sustaining consumer AI momentum once hype fades and constraints tighten.

Key takeaways

  • Sora hit the top of the App Store early but has since lost traction.
  • Appfigures data shows month-over-month declines in downloads and spending.
  • Competition from Google and Meta is intensifying in AI video.
  • Copyright controls and limits on IP usage reduced some of the viral appeal.
  • The app still has a sizable user base, but momentum has slowed.
