Veo 3.1 Ingredients to Video: More consistency, creativity and control

2026-01-13 Ricky Wong (Lead Product Manager, Google DeepMind) Veo 3.1, Google DeepMind, video generation, AI video, image-to-video, vertical video, upscaling, Gemini, SynthID, YouTube Shorts
Read the source article

Quick summary

Google DeepMind's Veo 3.1 "Ingredients to Video" update brings native vertical video, 1080p/4K upscaling, and dramatically improved character/background consistency — turning reference images into expressive, mobile-first video clips with professional-grade output quality across the Gemini app, YouTube, Flow, and API platforms.

Key takeaways

  • Veo 3.1 now supports native 9:16 vertical video for mobile-first platforms (YouTube Shorts, TikTok format), eliminating the need to crop or lose quality when adapting horizontal generation for short-form content
  • The "Ingredients to Video" capability lets creators upload reference images (characters, objects, textures, backgrounds) and generate videos that maintain identity consistency — characters look the same across scenes, objects don't morph, backgrounds stay coherent
  • Professional upscaling to 1080p and 4K is now available, with Google describing it as "state-of-the-art" for high-fidelity production workflows on Flow, the Gemini API, and Vertex AI
  • Distribution is broad: consumers get it in the Gemini app and YouTube Shorts/Create app; professionals get it in Flow, Google Vids, the Gemini API, and Vertex AI — a full spectrum from casual creator to enterprise
  • All Veo-generated videos include SynthID digital watermarks, and Google has deployed a verification tool in the Gemini app to help users determine if content is AI-generated
