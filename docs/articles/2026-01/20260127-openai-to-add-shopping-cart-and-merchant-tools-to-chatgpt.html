OpenAI to Add Shopping Cart and Merchant Tools to ChatGPT

January 24, 2026 Alexey Shabanov ChatGPT, OpenAI, e-commerce, shopping cart, merchant tools, AI commerce, product feeds
Read the source article

Quick summary

OpenAI is developing commerce features for ChatGPT including a shopping cart for in-conversation purchases and merchant integration tools that would allow sellers to upload product feeds directly into the platform.

Key takeaways

  • A dedicated shopping section will allow users to collect products discussed during conversations, review items, select delivery addresses and payment methods, and complete purchases
  • The feature mirrors Microsoft Copilot's existing shopping integration, suggesting convergence in AI assistant commerce capabilities
  • Temporary chat sessions will leverage user data, chat history, and preferences to personalize shopping recommendations while balancing privacy
  • A merchant submission portal will enable sellers to upload product feeds, potentially broadening reach for small business owners
  • No public launch timeline exists, though leadership has signaled additional products are forthcoming
