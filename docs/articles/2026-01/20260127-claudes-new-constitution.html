Claude's New Constitution

January 22, 2026 Anthropic Claude, constitution, AI values, safety, ethics, Anthropic, AI training, behavioral guidelines
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic releases a revised foundational document outlining Claude's values and behavioral guidelines, emphasizing the reasoning behind desired behaviors rather than rigid rules, with the constitution directly shaping model training through synthetic data generation.

Key takeaways

  • The new constitution explains why Claude should behave in certain ways rather than merely specifying what it should do, recognizing that AI models need to understand the reasoning behind rules
  • Four foundational principles in priority order: broadly safe, broadly ethical, compliant with Anthropic's guidelines, and genuinely helpful
  • Claude itself generates synthetic training data using the constitution, creating conversations and response rankings that align with stated values
  • The document addresses helpfulness, ethics and honesty, safety and human oversight, and philosophical questions about Claude's nature
  • Anthropic acknowledges gaps between intention and reality while committing to ongoing refinement through external feedback
