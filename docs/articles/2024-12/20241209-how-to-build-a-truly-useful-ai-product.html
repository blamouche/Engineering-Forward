How to Build a Truly Useful AI Product

2024-12-09 Chris Pedregal Generative AI, startup strategy, AI product development, LLMs, application-layer startups, product design
Read the source article

Quick summary

Building AI products requires fundamentally different strategies than traditional software startups because rapid model improvements, high inference costs, and universal API access create unique competitive dynamics that demand founders focus on narrow use cases and long-term model capabilities rather than current technical limitations.

Key takeaways

  • Anticipate model evolution: Don't solve problems that upcoming AI versions will automatically resolve; predict future capabilities and build around them instead.
  • High marginal costs create opportunity: Unlike traditional software, AI products maintain constant per-user costs, limiting large-scale deployment and allowing startups to deliver premium experiences that tech giants cannot.
  • Context matters more than instructions: Effective AI prompting requires providing curated contextual information rather than detailed rule-sets, treating models like "interns on their first day."
  • Narrow focus beats generalization: Compete against ChatGPT and Claude by specializing deeply in specific use cases rather than attempting broad functionality.
  • Non-AI elements drive satisfaction: Product quality depends equally on peripheral features like user interface design and integration quality as on the underlying AI capabilities.
