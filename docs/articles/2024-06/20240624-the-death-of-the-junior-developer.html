The Death of the Junior Developer

2024-06-24 Steve Yegge junior developers, AI coding, job displacement, CHOP, LLMs, software engineering future, AI tools, Sourcegraph Cody
Read the source article

Quick summary

LLMs are displacing junior-level work across industries, shifting software development toward a "Chat-Oriented Programming" paradigm where senior developers craft prompts and review outputs—a transition that amplifies experienced engineers while creating genuine danger for those without deep fundamentals.

Key takeaways

  • Chat-Oriented Programming (CHOP): senior developers craft prompts and review AI outputs rather than writing code manually; GPT-4o class models make this viable for complex programming tasks.
  • The law firm analogy: LLMs produce legal documents as effectively as junior associates, eliminating demand for entry-level roles across knowledge work broadly.
  • The safety paradox: while CHOP accelerates experienced developers, it creates danger for juniors who lack the judgment to detect when AI "gives you bad guidance."
  • Strong fundamentals matter more, not less: algorithms, systems design, and operations knowledge are what allow experienced developers to evaluate AI output correctly.
  • Advice: build deep fundamentals, gain real-world experience through internships and open-source contributions, master AI tools as essential professional instruments.
