The Token Reckoning is Here and It's Not What You Think

2026-06-01 Alex Kantrowitz and Marty Swant (Big Technology) token-economics, AI-spend, ROI, tokenmaxxing, enterprise-AI, EntelligenceAI, productivity-paradox, Anthropic, Opus-4.8
Read the source article

Quick summary

A year after Sam Altman promised intelligence "too cheap to meter," companies are blowing through annual token budgets in months — but the real problem isn't tokenmaxxing; it's that 82 cents of every AI dollar never reaches production, and unproductive uses of AI can only be solved by finding productive uses.

Key takeaways

  • For every $1 in AI spend, 82 cents never make it to production — only 18 cents turn into shipped product, with the rest going to bug fixes, rewrites, and review processes (per EntelligenceAI)
  • Uber COO Andrew Macdonald's viral statement that AI spending wasn't leading directly to productivity crystallized the industry's ROI anxiety
  • Anthropic raised $65B at a $965B valuation, launched Opus 4.8 (3x cheaper, 2.5x faster), and filed for an IPO in the same week
  • The panic about tokenmaxxing may be misplaced — token waste in service of productive use is different from waste for the sake of waste
  • The industry must sort out the ROI question or risk a significant comedown; negative AI stories travel further than positive ones
