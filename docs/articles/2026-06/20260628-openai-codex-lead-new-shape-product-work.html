OpenAI Codex Lead on the New Shape of Product Work

2026-06-28 Andrew Ambrosino (OpenAI) via Lenny's Newsletter openai, codex, product-development, taste, zone-defense, ai-first-workplace, product-management
Read the source article

Quick summary

OpenAI's Codex lead Andrew Ambrosino explains how AI has completely flipped product development, why "taste" is emerging as the most valuable skill in an AI-first workplace, and why eliminating roles entirely is a mistake even when everyone can build anything.

Key takeaways

  • Nearly 100% of OpenAI employees — not just engineers — now use Codex weekly
  • AI has completely flipped the product development process: the bottleneck is no longer building but deciding what to build
  • "Taste" is emerging as the most valuable professional skill in an AI-first workplace — the ability to judge quality and make good decisions when anyone can build anything
  • The "zone defense" model: PMs at OpenAI operate without rigid role boundaries, picking up whatever needs doing, rather than playing man-to-man coverage on specific features
  • Andrew believes the Codex app would have failed if launched in November vs February — timing and model readiness matter
  • Eliminating the concept of roles entirely is a big mistake: collapsed roles work, but role-less teams lose structure and accountability
