White House Asks OpenAI to Stagger AI Model Release

2026-06-26 TLDR OpenAI, GPT-5.6, White House, AI policy, model release, government regulation, Bedrock, national security
Read the source article

Quick summary

The US government has requested that OpenAI release GPT-5.6 to a short list of 20 trusted partners through Amazon's Bedrock before any wider release, with OpenAI staff instructed to work with the Trump administration on safety and restrictions.

Key takeaways

  • The US government requested that OpenAI release GPT-5.6 to a short list of trusted partners before a wider release, marking an unprecedented level of executive branch involvement in model deployment.
  • OpenAI staff have been instructed to work with the Trump administration on any input officials have on safety and restrictions.
  • GPT-5.6 will initially be released to 20 partners through Amazon's Bedrock software platform.
  • The Trump administration is continuing to collaborate with frontier AI labs to develop a shared approach for addressing the challenges of scaling the technology.
  • This staggered release model effectively creates a government-approved gating mechanism for frontier model access.
