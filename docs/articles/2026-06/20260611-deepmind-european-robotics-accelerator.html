Google DeepMind's European Robotics Bet: First Accelerator Cohort

June 11, 2026 Tom Nugent (Sifted) / Google DeepMind Google DeepMind, robotics accelerator, Europe, physical AI, Gemini Robotics Models, startup cohort, Neura Robotics, Zurich, London
Read the source article

Quick summary

Google DeepMind announces the inaugural cohort of its first robotics accelerator, selecting 15 early-stage European startups across 10 countries for a three-month program with access to Google's AI stack, Gemini robotics models, and $350K in cloud credits.

Key takeaways

  • The Google DeepMind Robotics Accelerator is a three-month, equity-free program running June–September 2026 at DeepMind's London headquarters
  • 15 startups from 10 European countries were selected, building everything from AI agents that understand machines to microbots that navigate brain tissue
  • Selected startups receive mentorship, strategic guidance, access to Google's AI stack, and Gemini Robotics Models
  • Up to $350K in Google for Startups Cloud program credits are offered to each selected company
  • European robotics startups raised €1.45bn across 125 deals in 2025; so far in 2026 they've raised €847m across 70 deals
  • Zurich is currently the hottest city for robotics deals in Europe with 13 bets since start of 2025, followed by London and Paris
  • Neura Robotics separately raised $1.4bn, Europe's biggest ever robotics round, backed by Amazon, Nvidia, and Qualcomm
  • The program ends with a Demo Day showcasing achievements to mentors, partners, and Google teams
