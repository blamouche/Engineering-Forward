SpaceX Acquires AI Coding Startup Cursor for $60 Billion

2026-06-17 CNBC SpaceX, Cursor, acquisition, AI coding, $60 billion, Elon Musk, Anthropic, OpenAI
Read the source article

Quick summary

SpaceX is acquiring AI coding startup Cursor for $60 billion in stock, marking one of the largest tech acquisitions in history and signaling Elon Musk's determination to compete directly with Anthropic and OpenAI in the AI tooling space.

Key takeaways

  • SpaceX is buying Cursor for $60 billion in stock, making it one of the largest tech acquisitions ever
  • Cursor, founded in 2022, crossed $1 billion in annualized revenue in November 2025 and has experienced explosive growth in its AI coding tool
  • The deal will make Cursor a wholly owned subsidiary of SpaceX when it closes in Q3 2026
  • The acquisition is part of Musk's broader strategy to compete with Anthropic and OpenAI in AI, following his xAI venture
  • Cursor's popular AI coding tool helps developers generate, edit, and review code, and has become a staple in developer workflows
