DeepSeek Raises $7.4 Billion, Becomes China's Most Valuable AI Startup

2026-06-17 Anisha Sircar (Forbes) DeepSeek, Liang Wenfeng, funding, $7.4 billion, $50 billion valuation, China AI, Tencent, CATL, state investment, limited partnership
Read the source article

Quick summary

DeepSeek closed its first-ever external funding round at $7.4 billion, valuing the company above $50 billion and making it China's most valuable AI startup—but investors received equity through a limited partnership with a five-year lock-up and zero voting rights, while only China's state-backed AI fund got direct voting rights.

Key takeaways

  • DeepSeek raised over 50 billion yuan (~$7.4B) in its first external round, valuing the company above $50 billion—making it China's most valuable AI startup
  • Founder Liang Wenfeng committed ~20 billion yuan (~$3B) of his own money, making him both founder and largest investor in the round
  • External investors (including Tencent at ~10B yuan and CATL at ~5B yuan) invested through a limited partnership controlled by Liang, with a 5-year lock-up and zero voting rights
  • China's National AI Industry Investment Fund invested directly into DeepSeek—uniquely receiving voting rights and no lock-up—raising state-alignment concerns
  • Despite the massive round, DeepSeek remains much smaller than US counterparts: Anthropic raised $65B at a $965B valuation, OpenAI $122B at $852B
