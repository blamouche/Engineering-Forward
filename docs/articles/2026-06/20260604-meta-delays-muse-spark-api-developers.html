Meta Keeps Delaying the Muse Spark API Developers Were Promised

2026-06-04 Ana-Maria Stanciuc (TNW) meta, muse-spark, api, developer-platform, ai-model, closed-source, monetization, superintelligence-labs
Read the source article

Quick summary

Meta launched its flagship Muse Spark AI model in April 2026 but has repeatedly delayed the developer API with no firm launch date, leaving developers unable to build on a model that Meta claims rivals OpenAI and Anthropic — raising questions about Meta's ability to monetize its massive AI investment.

Key takeaways

  • Muse Spark was announced in April 2026 as the centerpiece of Meta Superintelligence Labs, competitive with OpenAI and Anthropic's frontier models
  • The API has been delayed repeatedly: from April to May (bugs and infrastructure), then to June, with no firm launch date as of June 2
  • Muse Spark is Meta's first closed-source AI model — without an API, outside developers have no way to access it programmatically
  • Meta's internal evaluations rate Muse Spark competitive with OpenAI and Anthropic, but no outside evaluator has tested the model — the competitive claim remains unverified
  • The delay tests Meta's monetization strategy as the company raised its 2026 capex forecast to as much as $145 billion, up from $115–135 billion
  • The model powers Meta's AI assistant across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, and Threads, but third-party developers cannot integrate it into their own products
