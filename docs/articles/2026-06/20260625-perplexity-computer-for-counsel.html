Perplexity Introduces Computer for Counsel: AI for Lawyers

2026-06-25 Perplexity Perplexity, Computer for Counsel, legal AI, agents, law, enterprise AI
Read the source article

Quick summary

Perplexity launched Computer for Counsel, an AI agent tailored for legal professionals that connects research databases, document repositories, and contract tools — expanding the "Computer" agent platform into the legal vertical.

Key takeaways

  • Computer for Counsel connects legal research databases (Midpage), compliance data (Deel), and contract templates (LegalZoom) to Perplexity's Computer agent platform
  • Powered by 20+ frontier AI models with automatic model selection for research, reasoning, and contracts
  • Integrates with Microsoft 365 (Word, SharePoint, Outlook, Teams), Box, Carta, Clio, DeepJudge, Docusign, Ironclad, and NetDocuments
  • Always cites sources back to cases, statutes, regulations, or internal documents for attorney verification
  • 75% of lawyers say administrative tasks are a big time challenge (Thomson Reuters survey)
  • Available to Perplexity Enterprise and Max subscribers
  • Key workflows: NDA intake and review, regulatory monitoring dashboards, case research with citation review
  • Perplexity Enterprise does not train on company data; internal files stay under firm control
