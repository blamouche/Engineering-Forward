Vibe Check: Fable 5 Is the Best Coding Model in the World

June 9, 2026 Dan Shipper and Katie Parrott (Every) Claude-Fable-5, Mythos, coding-benchmark, Senior-Engineer-benchmark, AI-coding, model-evaluation, power-users, vibe-check
Read the source article

Quick summary

The Every team's five-day intensive testing of Claude Fable 5 found it to be the best coding model in the world — scoring 91/100 on their Senior Engineer benchmark versus Opus 4.8's 63 and GPT-5.5's 62 — but concluded it is a "warp drive for power users" that is overpowered for everyone else.

Key takeaways

  • Fable 5 scored 91/100 on Every's Senior Engineer benchmark, dramatically outperforming Opus 4.8 (63) and GPT-5.5 (62)
  • In one-shot testing, Fable 5 built a pixel-perfect 3D rendering of Borges's Library of Babel, a custom audio-enhancement web app, and a full survey-analytics synthesis — all from single prompts
  • The model is the first publicly available "Mythos-class" model, a category Anthropic previously declared too dangerous for public release
  • The Every team spent five days handing Fable ambitious projects across coding, writing, knowledge work, and custom agents
  • Fable 5 is described as the first model that "pushed us" rather than being pushed — it proactively suggests directions and capabilities the user hadn't considered
  • The model is best suited for power users who can harness its full capability; for simpler tasks, faster and cheaper models may serve better
  • Dan Shipper's upcoming podcast with Mike Krieger (head of Anthropic Labs) covers how Fable changes work
