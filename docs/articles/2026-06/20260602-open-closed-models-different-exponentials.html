Open and Closed Models Are on Different Exponentials

2026-06-02 Nathan Lambert (Interconnects) open models, closed models, AI economics, margins, oligopoly, open weights, inference, customization, market structure, AI industry
Read the source article

Quick summary

The open and closed AI model economies are growing on different exponentials—closed labs monetize the top end of knowledge work through integrated products, while the open model economy will take longer but eventually exceed the cumulative value of the closed lab oligopoly through broader diffusion across the economy.

Key takeaways

  • The largest debate defining the future balance of power between open and closed AI ecosystems is primarily economic: will users continue to pay dramatically more for top closed models?
  • Closed labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, with Google expected to catch up) will always make the most efficient models for intelligence at a given cost—they operate as an oligopoly resembling the cloud market
  • Many businesses want to switch to open models but the models aren't yet good enough in out-of-distribution tasks; eventually open model builders will stop chasing Claude and GPT on benchmarks and fill this niche
  • The total market value of the open model economy will dramatically exceed the cumulative value of OpenAI and Anthropic, but revenue and margins will be distributed across many companies
  • Open models are by nature not integrated, so they rely on multiple companies coordinating to serve them—each layer will have alternatives, driving prices to commodity levels
