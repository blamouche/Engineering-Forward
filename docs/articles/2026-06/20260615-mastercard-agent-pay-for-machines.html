Mastercard Launches Agent Pay For Machines (AP4M)

2026-06-15 Mastercard (via Big Technology) Mastercard, Agent Pay, AP4M, AI agents, payments, machine-to-machine, Cloudflare, crypto, autonomous payments, agent economy
Read the source article

Quick summary

Mastercard launched Agent Pay For Machines (AP4M), a new payment feature designed specifically for AI agents to make autonomous transactions, with 30+ initial partners including Cloudflare, banks, payment providers, and crypto companies.

Key takeaways

  • AP4M is designed for AI agents that need to make payments autonomously—machine-to-machine transactions without human intervention at the point of sale
  • 30+ initial partners include Cloudflare, various crypto companies, banks, and payment providers, signaling broad industry support for agent-native payment infrastructure
  • The launch signals that payment networks are building for the agent economy where AI systems need their own payment rails to purchase compute, data, and services
  • This represents a new layer in the payments stack: traditional payments serve humans making purchases; AP4M serves agents making purchases on behalf of humans or other systems
