France Seeks to Block UK Role in €5bn EU Scaleup Fund

2026-06-29 Martin Coulter and Freya Pratty (Sifted) EU Scaleup Fund, France, UK, late-stage VC, European tech, scaleup, EQT, British Business Bank, geopolitics
Read the source article

Quick summary

France is seeking to block the UK's participation in the EU's €5bn Scaleup Europe Fund, proposing geographical restrictions on investments that could upend months of negotiations and deny British startups access to the bloc's ambitious late-stage investment vehicle.

Key takeaways

  • The EU's €5bn Scaleup Europe Fund was designed to back strategically important startups before they're tempted across the Atlantic
  • France is pushing for geographical restrictions that would effectively exclude UK-based startups from the fund
  • The move casts doubt on hopes that British startups could access the bloc's late-stage investment vehicle
  • The fund involves major players including EQT and the British Business Bank, whose participation is now uncertain
  • The dispute reflects broader tensions about UK-EU tech cooperation post-Brexit
