Health Unicorn Alan Raises €480m Series G Round Led by Prosus

2026-06-26 Martin Coulter (Sifted) Alan, Prosus, Series G, healthtech, AI healthcare, France, digital insurance, fundraising, €5.5bn valuation
Read the source article

Quick summary

French healthtech startup Alan raised a €480m Series G round led by Prosus at a €5.5bn valuation, with the company hitting €800m ARR and planning international expansion powered by its AI-native healthcare platform.

Key takeaways

  • Alan raised €480m in a Series G funding round led by Dutch investment giant Prosus, alongside existing backers Index Ventures and Teachers' Venture Growth, and new investor Dara Holdings.
  • The round values Alan at €5.5 billion, making it one of Europe's most valuable healthtech companies.
  • Alan reached over €800m in annual recurring revenue in Q1 2026, representing 53% year-over-year growth, and is profitable in France.
  • The company serves more than 1.1 million members and over 37,000 businesses with its integrated health insurance, care, and prevention platform.
  • Prosus plans to support Alan's international expansion and provide access to its AI capabilities, including its Large Commerce Model.
  • Alan CEO Jean-Charles Samuelian-Werve, who also co-founded Mistral, said the round gives the company means to expand to new countries and invest more in AI and product.
