Runway's Big Week: AI Film Festival, Ron Howard, and Lionsgate Expansion

2026-06-15 Alex Kantrowitz and Marty Swant (Big Technology) Runway, AI video, AI Film Festival, Ron Howard, Lionsgate, Sora, AI filmmaking, photorealism, creative AI, video generation
Read the source article

Quick summary

Big Technology's coverage of Runway's fourth annual AI Film Festival reveals an AI video company maturing into a creative platform—1,000 attendees, a partnership with Ron Howard, an expanded Lionsgate deal with equity, and CEO Cristóbal Valenzuela's thesis on why video generation costs won't follow the LLM cost trajectory.

Key takeaways

  • 1,000 people attended the fourth annual Runway AI Film Festival in NYC, featuring nearly a dozen AI-generated short films across genres chosen from thousands of global submissions
  • Runway expanded its Lionsgate partnership: Lionsgate takes an equity interest in Runway, with a new joint development program starting with short-form episode series based on Lionsgate IP
  • CEO Valenzuela argued AI video costs won't follow the LLM runaway cost pattern: video generation is usage-based and time-bounded—"the creative process is the answer itself"—unlike agents that run for hours chasing answers
  • On Sora shutting down: Valenzuela said it validated Runway's focused thesis—"You can't just be doing 15 things"—companies trying to do video alongside other efforts lose on attention and prioritization
  • AI video models crossed the photorealism threshold within the last year; filmmakers are now splitting between photorealism chasers and those deliberately moving away from it toward "something more raw or more strange"
