Attention Is the New Startup Infrastructure: The Founder's Playbook for the AI Age

2026-06-29 Linas Seksys (Linas's Newsletter) attention-economy, go-direct, a16z, startup-strategy, narrative, distribution, ai-age
Read the source article

Quick summary

In an AI-saturated market where anyone can build faster, copy faster, and bundle faster, the founder's ability to command attention has become the scarcest input in company building — and it's an operating capability you build, not a personality trait.

Key takeaways

  • The old playbook (build product, find PMF, layer distribution on top) is breaking because AI lets anyone build, copy, and bundle faster — product alone is no longer the moat
  • Erik Torenberg's "New Media, One Year In" defines a16z's offering as "go-direct as a service" for startups: build your brand, reach key people, win the battle for attention
  • "Interesting" is not a personality trait — it is an operating capability you build, instrument, and compound like a product
  • Attention as infrastructure means treating narrative like a product surface: measured, iterated, and integrated into the company's core operating system
  • The practical playbook: founders must go direct, be interesting consistently, and build systems that turn narrative into customers, hires, capital, and category ownership
