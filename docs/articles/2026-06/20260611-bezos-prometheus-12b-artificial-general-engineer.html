Jeff Bezos's Prometheus Raises $12B to Build an Artificial General Engineer

June 11, 2026 Techibe Jeff Bezos, Prometheus, artificial general engineer, physical AI, Vik Bajaj, JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, BlackRock, labor scarcity, manufacturing
Read the source article

Quick summary

Jeff Bezos's physical AI startup Prometheus raised $12 billion at a $41 billion valuation to build an "artificial general engineer" that automates the design and manufacturing of complex physical systems — from jet engines to drug compounds — backed by JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, and BlackRock.

Key takeaways

  • Prometheus raised $12 billion in a new round valuing the company at $41 billion, with investors including Bezos himself, JPMorgan Chase, Goldman Sachs, and BlackRock.
  • The company has raised $18.2 billion in total ($6.2B at launch, $12B in June 2026) and employs 150 people — $273 million of valuation per employee.
  • The goal is to build an "artificial general engineer" that automates design and manufacturing of physical systems: jet engines, pharmaceutical compounds, industrial processes.
  • Bezos argues AI-driven productivity will create "labor scarcity" — demand for workers outpacing supply — putting him at odds with those who predict net displacement.
  • The presence of the world's largest financial institutions signals that physical AI is no longer a research topic but a near-term institutional bet.
