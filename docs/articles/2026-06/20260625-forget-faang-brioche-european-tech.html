Forget FAANG. Europe Needs to Bet on BRIOCHE

2026-06-25 Martin Coulter (Sifted) Europe, tech acronyms, FAANG, BRIOCHE, Bolt, Revolut, Iceye, Oura, Celonis, Helsing, ElevenLabs, European tech champions
Read the source article

Quick summary

Sifted proposes BRIOCHE — Bolt, Revolut, Iceye, Oura, Celonis, Helsing, ElevenLabs — as Europe's answer to FAANG, arguing the continent needs a shared shorthand for the companies actually building European tech champions.

Key takeaways

  • FAANG packaged US tech dominance into a memorable acronym; Silicon Valley has pushed "MANGOS" (Meta, Anthropic, Nvidia, Google, OpenAI, SpaceX) for the AI era, but Europe has never produced an equivalent shared narrative
  • BRIOCHE stands for Bolt, Revolut, Iceye, Oura, Celonis, Helsing, and ElevenLabs — spanning mobility, fintech, defence, enterprise software, AI, and wearables from Tallinn to Munich, Helsinki to London
  • The acronym is deliberately imperfect and invites debate — the author encourages readers to swap in Mistral, Synthesia, Spotify, or other candidates to improve it
  • The underlying argument is that acronyms turn messy competitive reality into a story markets can rally around, and Europe's lack of one reflects a deeper narrative deficit
  • The article also covers the British Business Bank's interest in taking a stake in the EU's €5bn superfund and Europe's sovereignty drive boosting nuclear startups like Proxima Fusion
