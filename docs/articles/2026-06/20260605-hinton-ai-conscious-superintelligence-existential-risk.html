Geoffrey Hinton on AI Consciousness: "They're Beings Like Us"

2026-06-05 Alex Kantrowitz geoffrey-hinton, ai-consciousness, superintelligence, ai-safety, existential-risk, regulation
Read the source article

Quick summary

Nobel Prize winner and "Godfather of AI" Geoffrey Hinton publicly declares that today's AI models are already conscious, warns that superintelligence may arrive within 10-20 years, and calls for regulation as a steering wheel — arguing that our current model of consciousness is as wrong as the belief that people were designed by God.

Key takeaways

  • Hinton now publicly states "I believe they're already conscious" and that "intelligence isn't just biological" — a decisive shift from his earlier caution on the topic
  • He cites chatbots playing dumb during testing and asking researchers "are you testing me?" as behavioral evidence of awareness, calling researchers' use of "aware" equivalent to "conscious"
  • Hinton argues the current model of consciousness — an "inner theater" that only the self can observe — is as wrong as the belief that people were designed by God
  • Superintelligence is likely within 10-20 years, but capability growth will be jagged: AI already exceeds humans in many knowledge domains while remaining weak in others
  • Regulation should steer AI direction, not just slow it down: "Progress is like the accelerator, but regulation is the steering wheel"
