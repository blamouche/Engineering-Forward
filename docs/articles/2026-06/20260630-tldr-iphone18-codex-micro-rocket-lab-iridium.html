TLDR Tech Roundup: iPhone 18 Leaks, Codex Micro, Rocket Lab Acquires Iridium

2026-06-30 TLDR iPhone 18, A20 Pro, Codex Micro, OpenAI, Rocket Lab, Iridium, Brain2Qwerty, AI hardware, satellite
Read the source article

Quick summary

TLDR's June 30 tech roundup covers iPhone 18 Pro motherboard leaks showing a thermally-managed A20 chip, OpenAI's Codex Micro keyboard hardware displayed at the AI Engineer World Fair, Rocket Lab's acquisition of Iridium to challenge SpaceX, and a non-invasive brain-to-text decoding model achieving 61% word accuracy.

Key takeaways

  • iPhone 18 Pro motherboard leaks reveal the A20 chip with improved thermal management, memory, and NPU upgrades for better AI performance at roughly the same footprint as the A19 Pro
  • OpenAI's Codex Micro is a physical keyboard device designed to supercharge Codex usage, developed with accessories company Work Louder, displayed at the AI Engineer World Fair
  • Rocket Lab is acquiring Iridium Communications and its 66 LEO satellites to challenge SpaceX's satellite offerings, gaining valuable spectrum rights for device connectivity
  • Brain2Qwerty v2 achieves 61% word accuracy in real-time sentence decoding from non-invasive brain recordings, significantly higher than the 8% from previous non-invasive methods
  • The model was trained on ~22,000 sentences from nine volunteer participants using magnetoencephalography devices, with training code released on Hugging Face
