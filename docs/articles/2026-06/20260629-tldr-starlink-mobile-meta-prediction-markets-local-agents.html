TLDR: Starlink Mobile, Meta Prediction Markets, and Local Coding Agents

2026-06-29 TLDR starlink, spacex, mobile-service, meta, prediction-markets, polymarket, kalshi, local-coding-agents
Read the source article

Quick summary

SpaceX plans a Starlink mobile service competing with Verizon, AT&T, and T-Mobile; Meta explores a prediction markets app à la Polymarket/Kalshi; and local coding agents emerge as a new development paradigm.

Key takeaways

  • SpaceX plans to launch Starlink mobile service in the US, potentially building its own terrestrial mobile network — direct competition with major carriers
  • Meta is building a prediction markets app; Zuckerberg urges exploration of working with Polymarket and Kalshi, which handled a combined $50B in trades last year
  • Meta's pattern: let startups prove demand, then deploy 3.56 billion daily active users against them (Stories → Snapchat, Reels → TikTok, Arena → Polymarket)
  • Local coding agents are highlighted as an emerging trend — agents that run on local machines rather than cloud APIs
  • Meta previously tried a prediction markets app (Forecast, 2020) focused on COVID outcomes — shut down in 2022 with minimal traction
