11 AI agent startups to watch, according to investors

August 4, 2025 Kai Nicol-Schwarz AI agents, startups, venture capital, Europe, market mapping
Read the source article

Quick summary

A Sifted investor roundup highlights 11 AI-agent startups that VCs are watching, framing agentic AI as a fast-moving category where infrastructure, workflow automation, and end-user tooling are attracting early conviction.

Key takeaways

  • The article is a market map rather than a deep technical teardown: it surfaces investor-selected companies seen as promising in agentic AI.
  • The framing comes from four venture firms — Speedinvest, Creandum, Firstminute Capital, and Cherry Ventures — which makes the piece useful as a snapshot of current VC attention.
  • The core signal is that agentic AI is broadening from general hype into a set of startup bets around practical workflows, software automation, and AI-native product experiences.
  • Public snippets around the article reference companies such as Ankar AI, Manex AI, and Granola, suggesting the list mixes specialist agent builders with broader AI productivity plays.
  • For operators, the value is less the exact ranking than the pattern: investors are looking for companies that turn language models into repeatable products, not just demos.
