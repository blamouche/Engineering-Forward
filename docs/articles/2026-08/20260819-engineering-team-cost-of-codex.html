An Engineering Team for the Cost of Codex

2026-08-19 Laura Entis / Every Codex, AI agents, harness engineering, Monologue, agent orchestration, AGENTS.md, solo developer, AI engineering team
Read the source article

Quick summary

Naveen Naidu, the one-person shop behind Every's dictation app Monologue, now manages a "team of engineers" — except they're all custom Codex agents with their own AGENTS.md files, skills, memory, and codebases, representing a new paradigm where a solo founder orchestrates an AI engineering organization.

Key takeaways

  • Naveen Naidu runs Monologue, Every's smart dictation app, as a solo developer but now operates as a "manager of engineers" — where the engineers are custom Codex agents.
  • His agent roster includes dedicated engineer agents across different disciplines, a customer support agent, and a growth strategist, all maintaining the Monologue website and app.
  • Each agent is a Codex project with a custom AGENTS.md file, skills, folders, memory, codebases, and context that turns it into a specialist.
  • Agent-to-agent workflows are already operational: a customer support agent received a glowing review and handed it to the web engineer agent, which added the testimonial to the site autonomously.
  • The future of harness engineering — building and maintaining the infrastructure around AI models — is trending toward doing less: the systems are becoming capable enough that the orchestration layer shrinks while the agent autonomy expands.
