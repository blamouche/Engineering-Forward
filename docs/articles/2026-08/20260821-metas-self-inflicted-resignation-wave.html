The Pulse: Meta's Self-Inflicted Resignation Wave

2026-08-20 Gergely Orosz Meta, layoffs, engineering culture, retainer equity, AI talent war, engineering leadership
Read the source article

Quick summary

Meta's 10% layoffs and forced reassignment of 20-30% of engineers to AI data labeling have triggered an exodus of top talent to Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google — and the $400K–$1M+ retainer equity grants Meta is now offering to stem the bleeding are often being outbid by competitors.

Key takeaways

  • Meta offered large retainer equity grants ($400K–$1M+, vesting over 3–4 years) to IC6+ engineers who resigned — a practice the company never did before, indicating the severity of the talent drain.
  • Anthropic and OpenAI are the main destinations for departing Meta engineers; two out of three engineers offered $1M+ counteroffers from Meta still chose Anthropic, and the third initially accepted then left a month later.
  • The forced reassignments to AI data labeling were done with no explanation and no input from managers, treating engineers as "replaceable commodities" and stripping them of autonomy.
  • Meta Muse Spark, the AI model whose development prompted the reassignments, currently ranks 7th in capability on Artificial Analysis' index, tied with Grok 4.5 — ahead of Google's Gemini but arguably not worth the organizational damage.
  • The 2022-2023 stock price means equity refreshers granted then are worth ~3x today, but TC is set to drop for many engineers by year-end, creating further attrition risk.
