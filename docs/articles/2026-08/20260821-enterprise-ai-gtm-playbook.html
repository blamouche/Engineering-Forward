The New Enterprise AI Go-To-Market Playbook

2026-08-06 Linas Beliūnas enterprise-ai, go-to-market, lighthouse, landgrab, a16z, distribution
Read the source article

Quick summary

A16z's "Lighthouse or Landgrab" framework identifies two fundamentally different enterprise AI sales motions — and most founders pick the wrong one, burning a funding round finding out that they should have been doing the other.

Key takeaways

  • The framework's two-question diagnostic asks: (1) how exposed is the person signing the deal, and (2) does social proof travel in that market — answering these determines whether you should pursue Lighthouse or Landgrab
  • Lighthouse strategy targets a small number of high-profile enterprise logos, investing heavily in making each one successful so that social proof spreads through the industry
  • Landgrab strategy signs hundreds of mid-market accounts quickly, relying on volume and self-service rather than deep custom deployments
  • 95% of enterprise GenAI pilots die before production, often because founders chose the wrong GTM motion and got trapped in permanent pilot purgatory
  • Sierra, Glean, Abridge, Rogo, Legora, Clay, and Cursor are cited as examples of companies that each picked their lane deliberately
  • The article includes a downloadable Claude skill ("lighthouse-landgrab") that runs the full diagnostic on your company and drafts a motion plan
