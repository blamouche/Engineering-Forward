Google Chief Scientist Jeff Dean Leaving Company After 27 Years

2026-08-05 MacKenzie Sigalos, Kif Leswing google, jeff-dean, deepmind, ai-reorg, discovery-loop
Read the source article

Quick summary

Google's AI divisions are being reshuffled: Jeff Dean leaves after 27 years to start Discovery Loop with Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals; Demis Hassabis steps down as DeepMind CEO to become chairman and Alphabet chief scientist; Koray Kavukcuoglu takes over day-to-day operations.

Key takeaways

  • Jeff Dean, Google's chief scientist and one of the most influential engineers in AI history, is departing after 27 years to co-found Discovery Loop, a public benefit corporation focused on automating scientific research
  • He's joined by three other senior AI leaders: Sanjay Ghemawat (Google Senior Fellow), Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals — representing an extraordinary concentration of AI talent leaving simultaneously
  • Demis Hassabis transitions from DeepMind CEO to chairman, also assuming the new title of Alphabet chief scientist, signaling a move from operational to strategic oversight
  • Koray Kavukcuoglu, previously DeepMind's CTO, now leads the unit as SVP reporting directly to Sundar Pichai, and will oversee development of Gemini 4
  • Google will invest in Discovery Loop and provide Google Cloud compute, suggesting the departure is amicable but also that Google is losing its institutional memory in AI leadership
