Quick summary

A 3-hour-44-minute leaked recording of DeepSeek founder Liang Wenfeng's closed-door investor debrief reveals the most complete picture yet of the AI lab's strategy: a sixfold pricing rule that turns open weights into a moat, 20,000 GPUs, a one-year deadline to break NVIDIA's CUDA lock-in, and a dated AGI roadmap.

Key takeaways

  • DeepSeek raised ~¥50 billion (~$7.4B) at a ~$52B post-money valuation, with Liang personally investing ~¥20B (~$3B); a follow-on round was reportedly in talks at ~$70B
  • The "sixfold pricing rule" — roughly ten-month hardware payback with ~6x profit on compute cost — aligns with the 545% inference margin DeepSeek publicly disclosed in March 2025
  • First on-the-record GPU numbers from a Chinese frontier lab: approximately 20,000 GPUs in DeepSeek's fleet
  • A one-year deadline for breaking NVIDIA's CUDA lock-in, with a Huawei-based alternative strategy
  • Half of DeepSeek's core researchers are labeling data, reflecting an org design optimized for training data quality
  • A dated watchlist of falsifiable claims provides the most specific AGI roadmap any Chinese frontier lab has publicly (or in this case, privately) shared
  • The same pricing doctrine sets terms of competition for GLM-5.2, Kimi K3, and the wider Chinese open-weight field
