Nvidia Buys Hugging Face for $12.9B; Anthropic Strikes $45B Compute Deal with Nscale

2026-08-28 Sifted Daily (Mimi Billing) Nvidia, Hugging Face acquisition, Anthropic, Nscale, AI compute, European tech, venture capital, TechBBQ, Denmark startup ecosystem
Read the source article

Quick summary

Nvidia agreed to acquire AI platform Hugging Face for $12.9B while Anthropic struck a $45B deal to rent AI compute capacity from UK-based Nscale, as European tech achieves its second-best August for funding on record.

Key takeaways

  • Nvidia has agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, consolidating its position across the AI model development stack
  • Anthropic struck a $45 billion deal to rent AI compute capacity from UK-based data centre startup Nscale, signaling massive demand for dedicated AI infrastructure
  • European tech achieved its second-best August for funding on record, with multiple under-the-radar rounds across deeptech, quantum, robotics, and pharma
  • Denmark's tech ecosystem struggles with companies relocating headquarters abroad (Unity, Zendesk, Tradeshift, Netlify, Just Eat), leaving local growth capital scarce
  • Swedish legaltech Legora is actively recruiting Copenhagen's best engineers to Stockholm, exemplifying intra-European talent competition
  • Notable August rounds: VitalFluid (€17.2M, agtech), Redx Pharma ($20M, drug discovery), ColibriTD (€4M, quantum computing), Cota Robotics (€1.92M, retail robotics), Motion (€1.7M, humanoids)
