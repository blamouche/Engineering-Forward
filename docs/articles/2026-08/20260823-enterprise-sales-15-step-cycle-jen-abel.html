Quick summary

Enterprise sales expert Jen Abel reveals that the standard five-stage CRM pipeline is a forecasting tool, not a sales process — the real enterprise sales cycle is closer to 15 steps, from the "pincer model" for landing first meetings to navigating procurement and redline negotiations.

Key takeaways

  • The standard five-stage CRM pipeline is a forecasting tool, not a sales process — the real enterprise cycle has ~15 steps
  • The "pincer model" lands the first meeting at the executive level and N-minus-one level simultaneously
  • Intro calls should extract maximum intelligence before ever showing a demo — the goal is information, not persuasion
  • Craft 2-3 sentence outreach messages around giving prospects "alpha" — valuable intelligence, not feature lists
  • Pilots run 2-3 days with jointly defined success criteria; charge for longer pilots vs giving away short ones
  • Navigate pricing, procurement, redline negotiations, and final signature without losing momentum
  • The framework applies to deals from $100K to $10M, building on Abel's earlier $1M-$10M playbook
