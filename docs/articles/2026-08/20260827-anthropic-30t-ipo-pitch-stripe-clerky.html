Anthropic's $30T IPO Pitch and Stripe's Clerky Acquisition

2026-08-27 Linas's Newsletter Anthropic IPO, $30 trillion TAM, Stripe, Clerky acquisition, Revolut EURR, stablecoin, fintech, startup fundraising, AI valuation
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic's leaked IPO roadshow pitches a $30 trillion TAM—650x its current business—while Stripe acquires legal OS Clerky behind $140B in startup fundraising, and Revolut launches EURR stablecoin as a white-label distribution play.

Key takeaways

  • Anthropic's $30T IPO pitch tops SpaceX's record and runs about 650x the business Anthropic actually operates today, originating from a leaked Coatue deck slide that became a public roadshow number
  • The TAM number isn't dishonest but has a job to do: signal market opportunity to investors—the tell to watch in the upcoming S-1 is revenue concentration and actual margins
  • Stripe acquired Clerky, the legal OS behind $140B in startup fundraising—a switching-cost play disguised as a tuck-in, gaining the attorney distribution channel Atlas could never build alone
  • Revolut's EURR euro stablecoin launched with only €369 in reserves but 80M+ banking customers on the other side—the distribution pipe is the whole product, not the token
  • Revolut outsourced issuance to Stripe's Bridge and kept brand and customer relationship—a white-label split more fintechs will copy
  • Revolut's real competition isn't Circle but other banks with crypto ambitions who control similar distribution channels
