Hugging Face in $13bn Sale Talks

2026-08-25 Martin Coulter (Sifted) Hugging Face, AI acquisitions, valuation, open-source AI
Read the source article

Quick summary

Hugging Face, the AI scaleup behind the most widely used open-source model hub, is reportedly in talks for a sale at a $13 billion valuation.

Key takeaways

  • Hugging Face could soon be up for sale at a $13bn valuation, according to reports
  • The company has become the de facto repository for open-source AI models, hosting hundreds of thousands of models from labs and individual researchers
  • A sale at this valuation would represent a significant premium over its previous funding rounds
  • The potential acquisition comes amid intensifying consolidation in the AI infrastructure layer
  • The deal signals that open-source AI platforms are becoming strategic assets that large incumbents want to own rather than partner with
