What We Learned From 15 Hours of Anthropic Certification Training

2026-08-31 Natalia Quintero (Every) Anthropic, AI certification, AI education, Claude, prompt engineering, agent building, course review
Read the source article

Quick summary

An Every team member who completed four Anthropic certification courses over 15 hours reflects on the state of AI education — finding it still early, with a shared set of definitions being the key prerequisite for what comes next, and the courses themselves varying in depth from foundational prompting to advanced agent building.

Key takeaways

  • The author took four Anthropic certification courses over the summer, totaling about 15 hours of training
  • AI education is still in its early days, and a shared set of definitions is the key prerequisite for meaningful progress
  • The courses ranged from foundational prompting to more advanced agent-building topics, reflecting the spectrum of skills needed to work effectively with Claude
  • The experience recalled early online education (edX, founded by MIT and Harvard), which was cutting edge 15 years ago but has since matured — AI education is at a similar early stage
  • The piece highlights that certification programs from model providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, etc.) are becoming a new category of professional credential
