Life After Automation: 100 AI Leaders Call Their Shots

2026-08-23 Dan Shipper / Every automation, thesis-statements, ai-predictions, future-of-work, every, thesis-2027, human-work
Read the source article

Quick summary

Every's "Thesis Statements" project collects 100 specific, contestable predictions from AI leaders about what human work looks like after automation — with the first 25 live, including claims from Linear's CEO, Ness Labs' founder, and Granola's cofounder.

Key takeaways

  • Thesis Statements collects 100 specific, contestable predictions from AI leaders who live on the frontier of automation
  • Linear CEO Karri Saarinen argues AI's biggest problem will be design, not intelligence
  • Ness Labs founder Anne-Laure Le Cunff says answers will become abundant and questions will become the hard part
  • Granola cofounder Chris Pedregal predicts some of your hardest problems will solve themselves
  • The project sets the stage for November's Thesis: 2027 conference
  • Every will share more statements weekly until the conference and revisit the claims to see which pan out
  • Users can submit their own predictions to the project
