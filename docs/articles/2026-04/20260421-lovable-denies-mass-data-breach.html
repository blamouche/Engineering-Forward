Lovable denies mass data breach

April 21, 2026 Lovable, security, data exposure, vibe coding, privacy
Read the source article

Quick summary

Lovable says it did not suffer a breach, but the episode shows how ambiguous privacy defaults can turn product growth into a security credibility problem.

Key takeaways

  • Lovable rejected claims of a mass breach while acknowledging that its documentation around public project visibility was unclear.
  • The reported issue centered on exposed chat histories and project data, highlighting the sensitivity of AI product logs.
  • Fast-growing vibe-coding platforms inherit security risks from how they store prompts, code, and personal user data.
  • Security communication matters as much as the technical fix when customers fear their information was visible to others.
  • The incident lands at a moment when Lovable is under pressure from rapid growth, new partnerships, and rising competition.
