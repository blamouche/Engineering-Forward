Anthropic boasts revenue run rate of $30 billion as the Claude developer expands its partnership with Google and Broadcom

April 7, 2026 Sherwood News Anthropic, revenue, Google, Broadcom, TPUs, AI infrastructure
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic says its annualized revenue has jumped past $30 billion while expanding a multiyear compute partnership with Google and Broadcom, signaling that top-tier model labs are turning demand into hyperscale infrastructure commitments.

Key takeaways

  • Anthropic says its annualized revenue run rate has climbed from roughly $9B to more than $30B in a few months.
  • The company says more than 1,000 customers now spend over $1M annually, roughly doubling in under two months.
  • Broadcom disclosed that Anthropic will access 3.5 gigawatts of TPU-based compute starting in 2027 via Google.
  • The deal suggests frontier model competition is increasingly constrained by long-term compute supply, not just model quality.
  • Anthropic’s business momentum appears strong enough to support much larger infrastructure commitments.
