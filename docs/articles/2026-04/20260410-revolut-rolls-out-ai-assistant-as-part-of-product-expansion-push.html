Revolut rolls out AI assistant as part of product expansion push

April 10, 2026 Sifted Revolut, fintech, AI assistant, personal finance, in-app copilot, product expansion
Read the source article

Quick summary

Revolut is turning its app into a conversational finance surface, using an in-app AI assistant to handle everyday account actions, insights, and travel tasks as part of a broader product expansion strategy.

Key takeaways

  • Revolut has launched AIR, an in-app AI assistant for UK users, as its first major consumer AI product.
  • The assistant can surface spending insights, track investments, manage subscriptions, and help freeze lost cards through chat.
  • Revolut is also extending AIR into travel use cases such as trip budgeting and purchasing in-app eSIMs.
  • The company says the assistant only accesses data customers already see in the app and does not retain that data.
  • The launch fits a wider push by Revolut to broaden its product surface with new financial services and AI-powered customer interactions.
