Quick summary

RL-based post-training is a promising paradigm for aligning diffusion models with human preferences, and scaling rollout group size yields clear gains — but at prohibitive cost on large models like FLUX.1-12B.

Key takeaways

  • We propose Sol-RL (Speed-of-light RL), an FP4-empowered two-stage RL framework that decouples exploration from optimization: high-throughput NVFP4 rollouts generate a massive candidate pool, from which only the most contrastive subset is regenerated in BF16 for policy updates.
  • This algorithm-hardware co-design accelerates rollouts while preserving training integrity.
  • Experiments across SANA, FLUX.1, and SD3.5-L show superior alignment with up to 4.64× faster convergence.
  • Sol-RL enables efficient and high-fidelity text-to-image alignment.
  • We decouple high-throughput FP4 exploration from selective BF16 training, achieving up to 2.4× rollout acceleration with merely 2% computational overhead while avoiding quantization-induced corruption.
