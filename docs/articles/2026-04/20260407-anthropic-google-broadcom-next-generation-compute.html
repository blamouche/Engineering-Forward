Anthropic expands partnership with Google and Broadcom for multiple gigawatts of next-generation compute

April 7, 2026 Anthropic Anthropic, Google Cloud, Broadcom, TPUs, AI infrastructure, compute, cloud platforms
Read the source article

Quick summary

Anthropic says it has secured multiple gigawatts of future TPU capacity with Google and Broadcom, underscoring how frontier-model competition is now inseparable from long-horizon infrastructure deals.

Key takeaways

  • Anthropic signed for multiple gigawatts of next-generation TPU capacity expected to come online from 2027.
  • The company says run-rate revenue has surpassed $30 billion and $1M-plus annualized customers doubled past 1,000 in under two months.
  • Anthropic is leaning into a multi-platform hardware strategy spanning Trainium, TPUs, and NVIDIA GPUs.
  • Most of the new capacity is slated for the United States, tying compute procurement to industrial policy and domestic infrastructure buildout.
  • Compute commitments of this scale increasingly function as strategic moats, not just procurement details.
