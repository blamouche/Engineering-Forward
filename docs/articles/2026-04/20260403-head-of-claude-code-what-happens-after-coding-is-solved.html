Quick summary

Boris Cherny, creator of Claude Code, shares how a quick terminal prototype grew to 4% of public GitHub commits, why he believes coding is "solved," and what comes next when AI takes over professional work beyond programming.

Key takeaways

  • Claude Code grew from a quick hack to representing 4% of public GitHub commits, with daily active users doubling in the month of the interview
  • Boris believes coding is now effectively "solved"—the remaining question is what happens to all the other professional work adjacent to coding
  • Counterintuitive product principles drove success: underfunding teams and giving them unlimited tokens led to better AI products by forcing authentic usage
  • Boris briefly left Anthropic for Cursor, returned after two weeks—the experience sharpened his conviction about Claude Code's approach
  • Cowork represents the next expansion: bringing AI collaboration to non-engineering professional work with similar principles to Claude Code
