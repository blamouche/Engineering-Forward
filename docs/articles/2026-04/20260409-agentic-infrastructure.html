Quick summary

Every generation of software eventually demands a new generation of infrastructure.

Key takeaways

  • Then, a more important shift: infrastructure derived from the application itself.
  • LLMs and coding agents are driving the next transition, and it's happening fast.
  • In just three months, weekly deployments on Vercel have doubled, and agents are driving the growth.
  • Today, over 30% of deployments are initiated by coding agents, up 1000% from six months ago.
  • Claude Code accounts for 75%, Lovable and v0 for 6%, and Cursor for 1.5%.
