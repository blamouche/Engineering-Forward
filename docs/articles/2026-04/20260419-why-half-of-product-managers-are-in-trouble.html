Why Half of Product Managers Are in Trouble

Unknown Nikhyl Singhal (Lenny's Newsletter interview) product management, AI, PM careers, job market, organizational change, AI-first teams
Read the source article

Quick summary

Nikhyl Singhal argues that AI is about to split product management into winners and losers, rewarding PMs who reinvent themselves around AI-native work while exposing those who rely on legacy coordination-heavy roles.

Key takeaways

  • The next two years may be the most chaotic period in product management history as AI changes how products are built and how teams are staffed.
  • Many current PM roles are vulnerable because AI reduces demand for coordination, documentation, and middle-layer process work.
  • The PMs who thrive will be those who actively experiment with AI and find personal “moments of joy” that pull them into new workflows.
  • Resume prestige and logo collecting matter less in an AI-first market than adaptability, speed, and evidence of reinvention.
  • Singhal predicts large companies may cut tens of thousands of people and rehire much smaller AI-native teams optimized around leverage.
