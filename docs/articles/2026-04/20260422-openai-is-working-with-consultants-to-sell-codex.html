OpenAI Is Working With Consultants to Sell Codex

Unknown OpenAI, Codex, enterprise sales, consulting, go-to-market
Read the source article

Quick summary

OpenAI is reportedly using major consulting firms to push Codex into enterprises, a sign that coding agents are becoming large-scale transformation deals instead of just developer tool purchases.

Key takeaways

  • The Wall Street Journal reports OpenAI is working with Accenture, Capgemini, and PwC to sell Codex.
  • OpenAI also said Codex had reached four million weekly active users, up sharply within weeks.
  • The consulting channel suggests enterprise adoption requires service-heavy integration and change management.
  • Coding agents are increasingly being sold as organizational capability, not just seat-based software.
  • The fight with Anthropic for enterprise developers is becoming as much a distribution contest as a model contest.
