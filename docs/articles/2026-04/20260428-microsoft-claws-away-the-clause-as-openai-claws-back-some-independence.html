Microsoft Claws Away 'The Clause' as OpenAI Claws Back Some Independence

April 28, 2026 M.G. Siegler OpenAI, Microsoft, AGI clause, AWS, cloud strategy
Read the source article

Quick summary

The piece argues that OpenAI’s latest deal with Microsoft trades away the symbolic leverage of the AGI clause in exchange for much more practical freedom to sell and operate across rival clouds.

Key takeaways

  • The updated agreement is framed as effectively killing the old AGI-linked clause and replacing it with a simpler time boundary.
  • OpenAI appears to gain more freedom to offer products on clouds beyond Azure, especially AWS.
  • Microsoft still keeps a privileged position as primary cloud partner and likely retains first-look rights in practice.
  • The author sees the deal as a response to competitive pressure from rivals such as Anthropic and from the broader cloud market.
  • The change is presented as a pragmatic rebalance rather than a clean separation between the two companies.
