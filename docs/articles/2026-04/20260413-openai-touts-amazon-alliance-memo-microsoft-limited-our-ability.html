OpenAI touts Amazon alliance in memo, says Microsoft has 'limited our ability' to reach clients

April 13, 2026 Jordan Novet and Kate Rooney OpenAI, Amazon, Microsoft, enterprise AI, cloud, Bedrock, Anthropic
Read the source article

Quick summary

CNBC reports that OpenAI is explicitly using its Amazon Bedrock partnership to widen enterprise distribution, while internally framing its Microsoft tie-up as strategically limiting now that cloud neutrality matters more than exclusive backing.

Key takeaways

  • OpenAI’s revenue leadership is positioning Amazon Bedrock as a major enterprise growth channel because many customers prefer buying models where they already run workloads.
  • The memo shows the Microsoft relationship evolving from foundational alliance to distribution constraint as OpenAI pursues broader cloud availability.
  • The piece also highlights the competitive backdrop: Anthropic’s enterprise momentum is pressuring OpenAI to sharpen both its go-to-market story and its infrastructure strategy.
