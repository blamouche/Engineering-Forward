Lovable CEO apologises after security scare: ‘I take accountability’

April 23, 2026 Lovable, security, data exposure, privacy, vibe coding
Read the source article

Quick summary

Lovable’s CEO says the startup mishandled a security scare, turning a debate over privacy defaults and disclosure into a broader trust test for AI app builders.

Key takeaways

  • Lovable says the issue was fixed, but the incident exposed a gap between product defaults and what users believed was private.
  • The company learned about the problem through social media because its vulnerability disclosure process had broken down.
  • The episode shows how AI builders must treat chat histories and prompt data as sensitive product infrastructure, not incidental logs.
  • Security response now depends as much on communication speed and clarity as on the underlying technical fix.
  • For fast-growing AI startups, privacy UX and disclosure processes are becoming core parts of category trust.
