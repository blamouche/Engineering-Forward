Q1 2026 Timelines Update: AGI Forecasts Accelerated 1.5 Years

April 2, 2026 Daniel Kokotajlo, Eli Lifland, Brendan Halstead (AI Futures Project) AGI, timelines, forecasting, METR, automated coder, Claude Opus 4.6, AI capabilities
Read the source article

Quick summary

The AI Futures Project revised their Automated Coder (human software engineers made redundant by AI) timelines forward by 1.5 years to mid-2028/mid-2030, citing faster capability doubling times and Claude Code reaching $2.5B annualized revenue in 9 months.

Key takeaways

  • Automated Coder definition: when an AGI company would prefer to lay off all human software engineers rather than stop using AI for coding
  • Daniel's AC median moved from late 2029 to mid-2028; Eli's from early 2032 to mid-2030 — both approximately 1.5-year accelerations
  • Capability doubling time revised from 5.5 months to 4-4.5 months
  • Claude Code reached $2.5B annualized revenue 9 months after launch; Anthropic maintaining ~10x annual revenue growth
  • Daniel reduced his 80% time horizon requirement for AC from 3 years to 1 year based on Opus 4.6 performance
