Google builds elite team to close the coding gap with Anthropic

2026-04-20 Matthias Bastian Google DeepMind, Gemini, coding agents, Anthropic, Sergey Brin, self-improving AI
Read the source article

Quick summary

Google DeepMind has formed a focused strike team to improve Gemini’s long-horizon coding abilities, reflecting how central agentic software development has become to frontier AI competition and to Google’s internal productivity agenda.

Key takeaways

  • Google DeepMind assembled a specialized team under Sebastian Borgeaud to improve Gemini’s performance on complex, multi-step programming work.
  • The effort is partly driven by an internal view that Anthropic currently leads in coding-oriented AI tools.
  • Sergey Brin and Koray Kavukcuoglu are directly involved, tying coding performance to Google’s broader push toward self-improving AI systems.
  • Google is pressuring internal teams to use agentic coding tools and is reportedly tracking usage as a management metric.
  • The company is leaning on training data from its proprietary codebase, which may help internal performance even if the resulting models cannot be released publicly.
