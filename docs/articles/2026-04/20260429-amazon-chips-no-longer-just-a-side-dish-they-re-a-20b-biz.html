Amazon chips no longer just a side dish, they're a $20B biz

April 29, 2026 The Register amazon, chips, aws, trainium, infrastructure
Read the source article

Quick summary

Amazon says its custom silicon operation has become one of the world's biggest datacenter chip businesses, turning AI infrastructure into a major revenue engine rather than a support function for AWS.

Key takeaways

  • Amazon claims its chip business exceeds a $20 billion annual revenue run rate and would look closer to $50 billion if internal AWS demand were counted like external sales.
  • Graviton, Trainium, and Nitro are now central to Amazon's AI and datacenter strategy.
  • Large commitments from Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta, and Uber show Amazon's custom silicon is attracting flagship AI workloads.
  • Trainium capacity is already heavily subscribed across current and future generations.
  • The article suggests hyperscalers are becoming semiconductor businesses in their own right.
