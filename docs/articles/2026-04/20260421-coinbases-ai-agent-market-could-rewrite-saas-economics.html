Coinbase’s AI Agent Market Could Rewrite SaaS Economics

Unknown Linas Beliūnas Coinbase, AI agents, micropayments, USDC, Base, SaaS economics, machine commerce
Read the source article

Quick summary

Linas Beliūnas argues that Coinbase’s new marketplace for AI-agent services matters less as a flashy agent directory than as infrastructure for machine-to-machine payments, potentially shifting SaaS monetization toward micropayments while pulling transaction float into Coinbase’s stablecoin and blockchain stack.

Key takeaways

  • Agent.market is framed as a directory where AI agents can discover and pay for services without conventional subscriptions, logins, or API key setup.
  • The payment mechanism relies on HTTP 402-style micropayments settled in USDC on Base, with very small transaction sizes designed for automated machine traffic.
  • The article argues that the strategic value lies in changing software monetization from recurring subscriptions to usage-priced machine transactions.
  • Coinbase benefits not only from protocol adoption but also from the stablecoin balances, transaction flow, and ecosystem control that route through USDC and Base.
  • The broader implication is that agent-native commerce could sideline incumbents such as Stripe and Visa in parts of the machine-payment stack.
