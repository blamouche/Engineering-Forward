The Missing Layer in AI Adoption

April 11, 2026 Every Staff AI adoption, AI agents, organizational design, workflow automation, management, enterprise AI
Read the source article

Quick summary

Every argues that the main bottleneck in AI adoption is no longer model access but a missing organizational layer: people need workflows, management habits, and agent operating norms that translate raw capability into daily execution.

Key takeaways

  • The piece presents AI adoption as a managerial and organizational problem more than a software procurement problem.
  • Every highlights how its own operation increasingly relies on specialized agents for prioritization, reporting, planning, and task conversion.
  • The article suggests that once each employee has an agent, everyone effectively becomes a manager responsible for delegation and review.
  • It connects adoption to practical workflow design, not abstract enthusiasm about model capabilities.
  • The broader package links internal experimentation, product updates, and external case studies into a picture of AI becoming an operating layer for knowledge work.
