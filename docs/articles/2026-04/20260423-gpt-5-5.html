GPT 5.5

April 23, 2026 GPT-5.5, OpenAI, Anthropic, coding models, knowledge work
Read the source article

Quick summary

This Every analysis argues that GPT-5.5 is OpenAI’s clearest attempt to retake the high-end work-model narrative by pairing stronger coding and knowledge-work performance with noticeably faster, lower-friction execution than Claude Opus 4.7.

Key takeaways

  • The piece frames GPT-5.5 as a strategic response to Anthropic’s recent lead in coding and professional workflows.
  • Speed is presented as the model’s most visible product advantage because it makes iteration feel easier and more reliable.
  • GPT-5.5 appears especially strong on structured professional outputs such as coding, dashboards, consulting documents, and synthesis work.
  • The article still sees weaknesses in blandness, some programming languages like Ruby, and judgment-heavy or spatial tasks where Opus can remain stronger.
  • OpenAI’s broader product story becomes more coherent when GPT-5.5 is connected to Codex, agents, documents, spreadsheets, and enterprise workflows.
