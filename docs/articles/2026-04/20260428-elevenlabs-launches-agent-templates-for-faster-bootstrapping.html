Quick summary

ElevenLabs launches Agent Templates on ElevenAgents, delivering pre-built AI agent frameworks with workflows for business needs

Key takeaways

  • ElevenLabs has introduced Agent Templates on its ElevenAgents platform, offering pre-built conversational agent frameworks to simplify and accelerate the deployment of AI agents across organizations.
  • These templates address common business needs, including customer support, onboarding, sales, feedback collection, and front desk operations.
  • Each template comes with predefined system prompts, workflows, and integrations, allowing teams to customize and launch agents without starting from scratch.
  • Introducing Agent Templates - pre-configured ElevenAgents you can deploy across your business.
  • The best teams run agents across support, sales, and operations - value compounds with every use case.
