Mini-Vibe Check: Claude Design Isn’t for Designers—Yet

Unknown Claude Design, Figma, design systems, prototyping, creative workflows
Read the source article

Quick summary

Every argues Claude Design is more useful as a brand-aware prototyping and collateral tool for non-designers than as a true replacement for exploratory design work in Figma.

Key takeaways

  • Claude Design can extract a starting design system from a repo or existing files and helps non-designers produce on-brand assets quickly.
  • Its strongest use case is extending an existing visual system for collateral, prototypes, and lightweight product surfaces.
  • The live editing and annotation model gives users a more tactile workflow than a pure prompt-only interface.
  • The product still feels menu-driven and reactive, which limits the open-ended experimentation central to professional design work.
  • The article frames the product as a workflow complement, not a direct substitute for Figma’s blank-canvas exploration.
