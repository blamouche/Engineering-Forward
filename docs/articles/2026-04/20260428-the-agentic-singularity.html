The Agentic Singularity

2026-04-28 Linas Beliūnas agentic interface, operating systems, OpenAI, generative UI, digital proxy
Read the source article

Quick summary

The piece argues that AI agents create a new interface layer above apps, and that OpenAI is assembling the architectural, vertical, and distribution pieces to become that operating system.

Key takeaways

  • Computing shifts when the interface abstraction changes, and AI agents represent the next major abstraction after touch.
  • Current app-centric operating systems are poorly suited to intent-based interaction where software executes end-to-end tasks.
  • OpenAI’s hires and acquisitions are framed as pieces of a broader operating-system strategy rather than isolated product moves.
  • The proposed stack combines generative UI, deep context, and domain-specific execution across health, finance, and other verticals.
  • The strategic goal is to sit above the device layer and mediate digital life through an AI proxy.
