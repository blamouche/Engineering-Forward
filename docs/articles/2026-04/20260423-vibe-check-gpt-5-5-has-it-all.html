Vibe Check: GPT-5.5 Has It All

April 23, 2026 Katie Parrott GPT-5.5, OpenAI, Opus 4.7, model benchmark, senior engineer, vibe check, coding, writing, AI model comparison
Read the source article

Quick summary

GPT-5.5 scores nearly double Opus 4.7 on Every's Senior Engineer Benchmark (62.5 vs 33.5), excels at writing after a year of OpenAI weakness in that area, and is fast and personable enough for constant use — though Opus 4.7 still has edges in one-shot vibe coding and broader product design.

Key takeaways

  • GPT-5.5 scored 62.5 on the Senior Engineer Benchmark vs 33.5 for Opus 4.7; humans score in the high 80s-low 90s
  • GPT-5.5's best benchmark run used an Opus-written plan, suggesting the two models may be complementary rather than pure competitors
  • Writing quality saw a real comeback — cleaner structure, smoother logic than Opus 4.7, the best from OpenAI in a year
  • GPT-5.5 excels with structure (plans, existing systems, tight feedback loops); Opus 4.7 still wins on one-shot vibe coding, PowerPoint, Ruby, and broad product-design tasks
  • The model is described as "rare" — easier to use and stronger simultaneously, personable enough for collaboration
