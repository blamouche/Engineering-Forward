OpenAI announces GPT-5.5, its latest artificial intelligence model

April 23, 2026 OpenAI, GPT-5.5, coding, computer use, AI safety, enterprise deployment
Read the source article

Quick summary

CNBC reports that OpenAI is releasing GPT-5.5 as a more capable work model for coding, software operation, research, and data analysis while classifying it as high risk for cyber misuse and rolling it out first to paid ChatGPT and Codex users.

Key takeaways

  • OpenAI is continuing a rapid release cadence, shipping GPT-5.5 less than two months after GPT-5.4.
  • The company is positioning the model around practical work tasks such as coding, computer use, online research, and document or spreadsheet creation.
  • Greg Brockman frames the upgrade as a system that needs less human guidance to turn ambiguous problems into concrete next steps.
  • OpenAI says GPT-5.5 clears its deployment bar despite meeting the company’s “High” risk threshold for cyber-related misuse.
  • The initial rollout targets paid ChatGPT tiers and Codex, with API availability delayed pending additional safeguards.
