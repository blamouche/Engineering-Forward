Google tests Canvas and Connectors on NotebookLM

April 14, 2026 https://www.facebook.com/testingcatalog testingcatalog, google, tests, canvas, connectors, notebooklm
Read the source article

Quick summary

Google is preparing updates to NotebookLM, including a Canvas for visualization, source labeling, and support for Connectors!.

Key takeaways

  • Google appears to be preparing a broader expansion of NotebookLM, with one of the clearest additions being a new Canvas option inside Studio.
  • The feature looks designed to generate a visual or interactive layer on top of a notebook’s existing sources.
  • Early prompts suggest users may be able to turn source material into an interactive timeline, a web page for understanding documents, a lightweight game, or a visualizer.
  • For students, researchers, analysts, and teams working through dense material, that would push NotebookLM beyond summarization and into guided exploration built directly from the source set.
  • The update also points to a broader shift in how NotebookLM handles context.
