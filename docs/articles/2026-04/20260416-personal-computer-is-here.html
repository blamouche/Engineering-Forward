Personal Computer is Here

April 16, 2026 Perplexity Team perplexity, personal computer, mac, orchestration, assistants
Read the source article

Quick summary

Perplexity’s new Personal Computer brings its multi-model computer-use system onto the user’s own Mac so it can work across local files, native apps, connectors, and the web.

Key takeaways

  • Personal Computer extends Perplexity Computer from a hosted environment to the user’s machine.
  • The product is designed to orchestrate work across local files, apps, connectors, and the web.
  • Perplexity emphasizes persistent always-on usage, especially on a Mac mini used as a dedicated machine.
  • The company frames the product as reducing manual app switching and memory overhead for users.
  • Security is centered on user visibility, sandboxed files, auditability, and reversible actions.
