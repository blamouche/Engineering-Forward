Your CEO Just Said ‘Use AI or Else.’ Here’s What to Do Next.

April 20, 2026 Alex Duffy ai adoption, enterprise, workflows, documentation, automation
Read the source article

Quick summary

This guide translates executive pressure to “use AI” into a concrete operating model built around habit formation, documentation, workflow discovery, automation, and public learning.

Key takeaways

  • The first step is not mastering prompts but building a reflex to include AI in everyday work.
  • People should map the specific value they create so AI amplifies meaningful work instead of producing filler.
  • Documentation is framed as the hidden unlock because it turns tacit work into reusable context for AI.
  • Repeated successful prompts should become templates, tools, bots, or lightweight automations.
  • Sharing experiments early helps teams learn collectively and creates proof of progress for managers and reviews.
