Synthesia announces major hiring push, opens three new offices

Unknown Synthesia, hiring, Europe, AI video, enterprise software
Read the source article

Quick summary

Synthesia says it will expand headcount by 70% and open offices in Austin, Paris and Berlin as enterprise demand pushes the company toward $200m ARR.

Key takeaways

  • Synthesia plans to increase headcount by roughly 70% this year.
  • The company says it has more than 600 employees today and expects to add more than 400.
  • It is opening new offices in Austin, Paris and Berlin and expanding in Zurich.
  • The company says it is on track to reach $200m ARR by the end of 2026.
  • Growth is being driven by enterprise demand, especially in Europe and the US.
