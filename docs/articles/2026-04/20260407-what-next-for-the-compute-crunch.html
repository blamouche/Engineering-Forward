What next for the compute crunch?

April 7, 2026 Martin Alderson AI infrastructure, compute crunch, GPU supply, datacenters, pricing
Read the source article

Quick summary

Martin Alderson argues that the frontier AI market is entering a prolonged compute shortage driven by explosive inference demand, delayed infrastructure rollouts, and hard supply constraints in chips, memory, power, and labor.

Key takeaways

  • The author sees a sharp jump in AI usage, especially around coding agents, as evidence that inference demand is accelerating faster than supply.
  • Large compute commitments do not instantly create capacity because datacenter, power, cooling, and supply-chain buildouts lag by years.
  • GB200 liquid-cooling rollout challenges and memory constraints are described as major bottlenecks.
  • Usage rationing can smooth demand but likely cannot fully close the gap if adoption keeps compounding.
  • The article predicts price increases as the last major lever once operational and product constraints are exhausted.
