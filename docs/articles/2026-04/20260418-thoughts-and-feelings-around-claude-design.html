Thoughts and Feelings around Claude Design

April 18, 2026 Sam Henri Gold Claude Design, Figma, design tools, code as source of truth, product design
Read the source article

Quick summary

Sam Henri Gold argues that Claude Design matters less as a polished tool than as a sign that code-native, agent-friendly workflows may pull design authority away from Figma’s increasingly complex proprietary system.

Key takeaways

  • The essay argues that Figma’s abstractions became so system-heavy and proprietary that they are poorly aligned with the code-trained models driving the agent era.
  • Claude Design is compelling because it is honest about being HTML and JavaScript underneath, which makes it easier to connect design exploration with implementation.
  • The author sees design authority shifting back toward code because that is the medium LLMs understand best and where products ultimately live.
  • Figma Make is framed as a defense of the existing design-file canon, while Claude Design is treated as a bet on a different source of truth.
  • The piece predicts a split between code-native design tools and freer creative exploration environments that abandon design-system rigidity.
