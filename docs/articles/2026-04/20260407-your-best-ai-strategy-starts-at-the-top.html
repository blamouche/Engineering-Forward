Your Best AI Strategy Starts at the Top

April 7, 2026 Natalia Quintero and Mike Taylor executives, AI adoption, leadership, organizational design, management, skills
Read the source article

Quick summary

Natalia Quintero and Mike Taylor argue that executives should stop treating AI like a normal software procurement problem and instead approach it like managing a new class of highly capable but error-prone employees whose effectiveness depends on leadership judgment.

Key takeaways

  • Leadership teams cannot sensibly set AI strategy without using the tools themselves.
  • The authors argue AI behaves less like a software product and more like a junior-to-mid-level worker that needs delegation, evaluation, and context.
  • Senior leaders have an advantage with AI because they understand what “good” looks like in their domain.
  • A major opportunity is turning tacit organizational knowledge into reusable AI skills, evaluation frameworks, and documentation.
  • The article proposes five concrete actions: suspend disbelief, use tools daily, evaluate outputs fairly, hire for taste, and improve organizational documentation.
