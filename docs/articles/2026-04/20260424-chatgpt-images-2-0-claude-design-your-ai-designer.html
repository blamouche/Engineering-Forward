Quick summary

A practical guide argues that founders should pair ChatGPT Images 2.0 for creative exploration with Claude Design for structured prototyping to compress early-stage product design into a faster, cheaper workflow.

Key takeaways

  • The piece frames ChatGPT Images 2.0 and Claude Design as complementary tools rather than substitutes.
  • OpenAI is positioned as stronger for broad visual exploration, text rendering, and asset generation.
  • Anthropic is positioned as stronger for structured design systems, prototypes, and downstream implementation handoff.
  • The recommended workflow front-loads ideation outside Claude Design to preserve its limited quota for final-system generation.
  • For founders, the core claim is economic: design iteration that once required agencies, weeks, and significant budgets can now be compressed into a single afternoon.
