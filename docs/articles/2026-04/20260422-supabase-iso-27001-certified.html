Supabase is now ISO 27001 certified

2026-04-22 stephen_morgan ISO 27001, compliance, security, Supabase, ISMS, SOC 2, HIPAA, certification
Read the source article

Quick summary

Supabase has achieved ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification across its entire platform, removing a major compliance blocker for European, Asian, and public-sector customers while complementing its existing SOC 2 and HIPAA certifications.

Key takeaways

  • ISO 27001 certification covers Supabase's entire platform: Database, Auth, Storage, Realtime, Edge Functions, and Data API — validated by an accredited third-party auditor.
  • ISO 27001 and SOC 2 cover similar ground (access controls, change management, incident response, business continuity), but the key difference is geography: SOC 2 is widely accepted in North America, while ISO 27001 is the standard in Europe, Asia, and the public sector globally.
  • The two-stage certification process involved documentation review (policies, risk assessments, statement of applicability) followed by a full operational audit with staff interviews and control testing.
  • The ISO 27001 certificate is valid for three years with annual surveillance audits — controls must keep working continuously or the certificate is revoked.
  • With SOC 2, HIPAA, and now ISO 27001, Supabase covers the three major compliance frameworks, and the team invites customers to request specific additional compliance requirements.
