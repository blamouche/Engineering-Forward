Lovable poaches new engineering chief from Meta: ‘The whole industry is transforming’

April 15, 2026 Mimi Billing Lovable, hiring, engineering leadership, Meta, startup scaling, Europe, AI
Read the source article

Quick summary

Lovable has hired Meta engineering director Patrik “Totte” Torstensson to lead engineering, signalling that fast-growing AI startups are now credible destinations for senior Big Tech leaders seeking more ownership and speed.

Key takeaways

  • Lovable recruited Patrik “Totte” Torstensson after he most recently led a roughly 120-person engineering team at Meta.
  • Torstensson’s background spans Spotify, Google, and Meta, giving Lovable a senior operator with experience in large-scale engineering organisations.
  • The move reflects how AI-native startups are competing with Big Tech for top engineering leadership, not just junior or opportunistic hires.
  • The article frames the appeal as a mix of greater ownership, faster execution, and the sense that AI is reshaping the software industry’s center of gravity.
  • Lovable appears to be using leadership hiring to prepare for further scaling after rapid recent growth.
