Agents with Taste

April 22, 2026 Emil Kowalski AI coding agents, design systems, skills, UI engineering, taste transfer
Read the source article

Quick summary

Strong output from coding agents depends less on vague prompting and more on turning hard-won design judgment into explicit reusable rules.

Key takeaways

  • The article argues that visual quality can be transferred to agents by documenting concrete design rules rather than relying on intuition alone.
  • Skill files can encode decisions about easing, duration, typography, interaction targets, and animation behavior.
  • The strongest “taste” decisions are often explainable, which makes them teachable to both people and agents.
  • This approach turns design judgment into a reusable asset that scales across repeated agent-assisted work.
  • The pattern suggests a path for teams to codify high-quality frontend craftsmanship instead of leaving it to one-off manual review.
