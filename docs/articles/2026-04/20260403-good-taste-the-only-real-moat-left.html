Good Taste the Only Real Moat Left

April 3, 2026 Rajnandan AI, LLMs, taste, product judgment, design, writing, builders
Read the source article

Quick summary

Rajnandan argues that AI makes competent output abundant, so advantage shifts toward taste and judgment, but warns that humans should use taste to actively shape work under real constraints rather than become passive curators of machine-generated drafts.

Key takeaways

  • LLMs flood the market with plausible 7/10 work, which makes the middle of the quality distribution crowded and generic.
  • Taste matters less as aesthetic preference than as the ability to notice what is off, reject it, and explain precisely why.
  • The new bottleneck is judgment: knowing which drafts are context-blind, dishonest, or merely polished averages.
  • AI can help train taste by generating many variants and forcing sharper critique, especially through rejection-focused exercises.
  • Taste alone is not enough; authorship still requires ownership of stakes, direction, and constraints that models cannot truly carry.
