Chinese AI lab DeepSeek is reportedly in talks for its first external funding round at a $20 billion valuation, drawing major interest from tech giants Tencent and Alibaba

  • Investor interest pushed the valuation above $20 billion in just 48 hours, according to people familiar with the talks.
  • Tencent offered to buy up to a 20% stake, but DeepSeek does not want to give up that much control.
  • Alibaba is also negotiating, but details of its offer have not been shared.
  • The terms, valuation, and size of the funding round could still change, and none of the companies has commented publicly.
