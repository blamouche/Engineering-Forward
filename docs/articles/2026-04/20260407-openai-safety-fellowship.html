Introducing the OpenAI Safety Fellowship

April 7, 2026 OpenAI OpenAI, AI safety, alignment, fellowship, research talent, evaluation, governance
Read the source article

Quick summary

OpenAI has launched a new external safety fellowship to fund and mentor researchers working on alignment, robustness, misuse prevention, privacy, and other high-impact questions around advanced AI systems.

Key takeaways

  • The fellowship runs from September 2026 to February 2027 and targets external researchers and practitioners.
  • Priority areas include safety evaluation, robustness, scalable mitigations, privacy-preserving methods, and agentic oversight.
  • Fellows will receive mentorship, a stipend, compute support, and a Berkeley-based optional workspace via Constellation.
  • OpenAI says it values demonstrated research ability and technical judgment over formal credentials alone.
  • The program is a talent-pipeline move as well as a research-output initiative.
