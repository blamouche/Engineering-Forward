Quick summary

How will Elon Musk achieve TERAFAB's massive 1 TWh compute goal with ASML heavily backlogged? The answer involves Intel, GaN chiplets, and advanced packaging

Key takeaways

  • How Elon Musk Plans to Bypass the ASML Bottleneck to Build TERAFAB April 14, 2026 By Karan Singh Not a Tesla App When Elon Musk took the stage at the recent TERAFAB event , the stated goals were nothing short of staggering.
  • By aiming to produce 1 TWh of compute capacity per year, the combined forces of Tesla, SpaceX, and xAI are attempting to capture a massive percentage of Earth's total silicon production output.
  • However, industry analysts immediately pointed to the elephant in the room: ASML.
  • The Dutch company holds an absolute monopoly on the Extreme Ultraviolet (EUV) lithography machines required to print leading-edge chips, and its order book is already backed up for years.
  • You cannot simply order a fleet of High-NA EUV machines and expect them to be delivered next year.
