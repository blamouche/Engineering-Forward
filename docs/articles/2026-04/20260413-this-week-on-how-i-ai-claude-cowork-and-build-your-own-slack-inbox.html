Quick summary

Key takeaways

  • Lenny’s “How I AI” roundup showcases two practical patterns: use AI to build small deterministic systems around your workflow, and treat project folders, examples, and recurring tasks as the real levers of personalization.
  • This roundup pulls together two complementary stories about non-engineers using AI effectively. One is Yash Tekriwal’s Slack triage workflow, where AI helps build a digest and dashboard that reduce notification overload. The other is JJ Englert’s Cowork setup, where folders, brain files, skills, and schedules become a personal operating system for daily work.
  • What connects them is a useful philosophy: the biggest gains often come not from asking AI to do a single task repeatedly, but from using AI to build a small reusable system that does that task consistently. Once the system exists, execution can become deterministic or at least heavily scaffolded.
  • The newsletter is a good snapshot of where practical AI usage is heading for knowledge workers—toward micro-software, personalized context layers, and recurring automations that sit on top of existing tools rather than replacing them outright.
