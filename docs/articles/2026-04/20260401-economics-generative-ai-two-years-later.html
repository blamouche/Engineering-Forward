The Economics of Generative AI: Two Years Later

April 1, 2026 Apoorv Agrawal AI economics, NVIDIA, hyperscalers, OpenAI, Anthropic, semiconductors, infrastructure, applications, margins
Read the source article

Quick summary

The AI industry has grown from $90B to $435B in two years but remains structurally inverted: semiconductors (led by NVIDIA at ~$250B) capture 79% of gross profits while applications (OpenAI + Anthropic at 75% of $60B) capture only ~33% margins.

Key takeaways

  • Three-layer stack: semiconductors (~$300B, ~73% margins), infrastructure (~$75B, ~55% margins), applications (~$60B, ~33% margins)
  • NVIDIA captures ~$250B of semiconductor revenue (~80% layer share); Broadcom at ~$34B; hyperscaler direct chip purchases ~$25B
  • OpenAI and Anthropic command ~75% of application layer revenue (~$45B combined)
  • Top 5 hyperscalers spent $443B in 2025 (up 73% YoY); ~$450B projected for 2026
  • Applications would need over a decade at current rates to reach cloud-era profit levels — assuming custom silicon doesn't compress margins first
