The $725 Billion AI Spending Surge Is Missing the Real Bottleneck

April 30, 2026 Jon Markman (Forbes) AI infrastructure, capex, connectivity silicon, Nvidia, Astera Labs, data centers, bottleneck
Read the source article

Quick summary

Combined 2026 hyperscaler capex has reached $725 billion — up 77% — but the real bottleneck has shifted from GPU compute to the connectivity silicon that lets thousands of GPUs work together as a single supercomputer.

Key takeaways

  • Alphabet, Amazon, Microsoft, and Meta combined 2026 capex now totals ~$725 billion, up 77% from $410 billion in 2025
  • The bottleneck is shifting from compute (GPUs) to connectivity — the switches, retimers, cables, and controllers that enable rack-scale architectures
  • Astera Labs CEO Jitendra Mohan: "Increasingly, the bottleneck is shifting from compute to connectivity, and connectivity is where we play"
  • Amazon issued Astera Labs a warrant tied to $6.5 billion in cumulative product purchases, expanding a previous $466M warrant by 13x
  • Microsoft's CFO cited a $25 billion impact from higher component prices including memory and networking silicon
  • Astera grew from $396M to $852M revenue (115% growth) with a served addressable market projected at $25 billion by 2030
  • The market is still pricing GPUs while the hyperscalers are telling investors the constraint has moved to the layer between the GPUs
