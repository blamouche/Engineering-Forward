OpenAI raises $122 billion to accelerate the next phase of AI

April 7, 2026 OpenAI OpenAI, funding, compute, superapp, enterprise AI, infrastructure
Read the source article

Quick summary

OpenAI says it has closed a $122 billion funding round at an $852 billion post-money valuation and frames the raise as fuel for a vertically integrated strategy that links compute, consumer scale, enterprise adoption, developer tooling, and an eventual AI “superapp.”

Key takeaways

  • OpenAI says it raised $122B in committed capital at an $852B post-money valuation.
  • The company claims monthly revenue has reached $2B, with enterprise contributing more than 40% of total revenue.
  • OpenAI presents compute access as its central strategic advantage and is diversifying across cloud, chip, and data-center partners.
  • The company says ChatGPT has more than 900M weekly active users and Codex serves more than 2M weekly users.
  • Management is positioning the next phase of the business around a unified AI superapp that combines ChatGPT, Codex, browsing, and agent capabilities.
